05 / TURN A SHORTLIST INTO A PLAN

Six things to confirm next.

With these details, the next shortlist can be based on your actual academic and creative profile - with a realistic language and cost plan.

01

Your creative direction

Film/video, animation/games, graphic/visual design, interactive art, or curating and media theory?

02

Your bachelor degree

Degree title, institution, graduation date and transcript. These decide academic eligibility.

03

Your IELTS report

Academic test? Test date? Overall and every band? Accepted test format and exemptions vary by university.

04

Your portfolio

A link or PDF, plus your role in collaborative work. A few strong, clearly credited projects are more useful than quantity.

05

Your budget

Tuition ceiling and living-cost allowance. CNY/HKD totals exclude housing, travel and most other costs.

06

Your timing + Chinese

Confirm intended intake and current Chinese level. Then decide whether Chinese study is a future option.
