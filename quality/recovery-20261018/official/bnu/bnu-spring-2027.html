CHINESE-TAUGHT UNDERGRADUATE CREDIT PROGRAM (GENERAL ADVANCED STUDIES STUDENT)

Date:2020-11-26|Page View:

Beijing Normal University 2027 Admission Brochure for Undergraduate Credit Program (Chinese-taught)

 

The undergraduate credit program accepts international students who are in the second year of university (including) or above. They can take undergraduate courses in BNU schools and colleges as general advanced studies students. The study period is one semester or one academic year.


I. Application Requirements

1. Applicants should be in good health and of good character, respect Chinese habits and customs, and abide by both Chinese Law & Regulations as well as the university’s Rules & Regulations.

2.  Non-Chinese citizens aged between 18 and 45 with a valid foreign passport.

3.  Second year of college (including) or above.

4.  Applicant’s Chinese proficiency must meet the requirements of the majors applied for. A minimum score of 180 or above in the HSK test (Level 4) is required for the major of Teaching Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages. A minimum score of 180 or above in the HSK test (Level 5) is required for other majors. Applicants with prior/current academic qualifications taught in Chinese are exempt from submitting HSK score reports.

 

II. Application Period

Spring Semester (starting in March 2027) : October 10, 2026 -- December 15, 2026

If requested to supplement materials, the deadline for re-submission is December 20.

Fall Semester (starting in September 2026) : March 10, 2026 -- June 30, 2026


III. Application Procedures

1. Online application. Log on to Beijing Normal University Online Application System For International Students at https://international.bnu.edu.cn/, register and complete the application, upload the electronic version of the application documents according to the requirements in Part IV, and pay the application fee of 450 RMB.

2. Review. After submitting the application, it goes through the review process. Applicants may attend online interviews if required.

3. Print the application form. Upon passing the preliminary review, please download and print the Application Form for International Students from BNU’s Online Application System. This form is auto‑generated by the system upon your online application.

4. Sign and send the electronic application form. Please send the scanned signed application form to nondegree@bnu.edu.cn according to the instructions.


IV. Application Documents

1. Beijing Normal University International Students Application Form. Please log on to the Beijing Normal University International Student Online Application System (https://international.bnu.edu.cn/) and fill in the form online.

2. Passport Bio Page. Applicants who have a Chinese visa must provide the recent visa page.

3. Final Degree Certificate or Certificate of Enrollment.

4. Transcript of the final academic study period.

5. HSK certificate and transcript; Or the certificate issued by the institution that the prior/current academic degree is taught in Chinese.

6. A letter of recommendation from a teacher. The letter of recommendation has no fixed format, but it must be signed by the teacher.

7. Non-criminal Record. Exchange students are not required to submit.

8. Application fee of RMB 450. Please pay online through a personal account on Beijing Normal University International Student Online Application System (https://international.bnu.edu.cn/). Application fees can be waived for exchange students according to the relevant agreement.

* All application documents must be in Chinese or English. Please provide the notarized version of documents translated to Chinese or English from other languages.

* The application fee is non-refundable. It will not be returned regardless of whether the applicant is admitted by the institution.

* Applicants studying at a higher education institution in China during the application period, if admitted, must provide a completion or graduation certificate issued by that institution upon registration.

 

V. List of Enrollment Majors

BNU 2026 Undergraduate Majors Catalog for International Students

 

VI. Tuition Fee (Unit: RMB)

Liberal Arts: RMB 11600 per semester; RMB 22400 per academic year.

Science, Engineering, Arts, P.E.: RMB 13050 per semester; RMB 25300 per academic year.

 

VII. Accommodation

Beijing Normal University International Student Apartment offers single and double rooms. Please refer to the website of the International Student Office of Beijing Normal University (http://iso.bnu.edu.cn/) for the fee information. After receiving the electronic admission letter, applicants can log on to https://bnu.17gz.org/ to apply online.

 

VIII.Scholarships

To encourage and support academically excellent international students to pursue their studies in China, Beijing Normal University offers the following scholarship programmes:

--Beijing Government Scholarship for International Students

--Chinese Government Scholarship

--International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship

For applications for the Chinese Government Scholarship, please visit the website of the China Scholarship Council or consult the cultural‑and‑education section of local Chinese embassies and consulates. The website of the China Scholarship Council is: https://www.campuschina.org.

For applications for the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship, please visit its official website (https://cis.chinese.cn), or consult recommending institutions such as local Confucius Institutes and Confucius Classrooms.
