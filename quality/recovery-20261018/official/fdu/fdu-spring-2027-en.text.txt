  ABOUT INTERNATIONAL
  CULTUTRAL EXCHANGE
SCHOOL, ICES, FUDAN UNIV.
   International Cultural Exchange School, ICES, is the institution specialized in
providing Chinese language and culture programs for international students in
Fudan University. ICES is appointed to be one of the first four bases of
Teaching Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages in China. We accept
international students for long term and short term language programs, general
advanced and other tailor-made programs. ICES also provide degree programs
for undergraduates majoring in Chinese Language & Culture and Business
Chinese, and for Master candidates majoring in Teaching Chinese to Speakers of
Other Languages. Study with ICES is an opportunity to get to know Chinese
language, experience the culture and be part of Shanghai life. ICES programs
will help you achieve multicultural understanding, establish a global vision, and
get connected with peers from around the world. ICES looks forward to
welcoming you to our programs.

     OVERVIEW
INTRODUCTION
  ICES adopts a unique model of instruction which has altogether 10 levels in Chinese
learning, 4 levels (ABCD levels) in Fundamental, 4 levels (EFGH) in Intermediate and 2
levels (IJ) in Advanced. Each level takes about 9 weeks, students can finish 2 levels each
semester in half a year. Students will get into appropriate levels according to the results of
online placement test on arrival. ICES’s professional and experienced teaching staff
ensure the most efficient teaching and learning.
  Study Abroad or exchange students groups from partner institutions home and abroad,
including LSE, University of Edinburgh, Goethe University Frankfurt, Doshisha
University, Thammasat University, Chinese Testing International （HSK Headquarter,
GIIP students）etc., also join our program every year.
 MAIN COURSES

 The main courses include: Chinese Intensive Reading, Extensive Reading, Listening &
Speaking and Writing. Optional courses include: Chinese Sayings and Proverbs, Chinese
Tourism Culture, General Theory of Chinese Culture, Chinese Movies and Culture, The Art
of Chinese Music and Poetry, etc. In addition, some of our optional courses for
undergraduates will open to advanced students. There will be about 18 class hours per week
for each level.

APPLY
Study Period: Ranges from half-year to one year.
   The Program Duration are as follows(TBC):
  2027 Spring Semester, one semester Program:
    Duration of Major Study (From): 2027-02-15, (To): 2027-06-25
  2027 Spring & 2027 Autumn Semester, one year Program:
    Duration of Major Study (From): 2027-02-15, (To): 2028-01-07
  ★For detailed information, please check school calendar at http://www.ices.fudan.edu.cn
Application fee: 400 RMB
Tuition (for reference): 10500RMB/Semester, 21000RMB/Year
  ★Final tuition criteria are subject to enrolment pricing announced by Financial Office of Fudan.
Application Requirements: Applicant must be a citizen of a foreign country between 17 and 60 years of age, in good mental and
physical health.
Application periods:
   Spring Semester Intake: Applications begin on October 16. Deadline of submitting application is December 13.
Online Application Materials:
1. One completed application through the University’s Online Application System (including education background, family
    members, emergency contact person, etc.) with all required documents uploaded.
2. One regular passport photocopy (personal information page – passport validity must be adequate for study).
★Minor applicants aged 17 must submit documents including GUARANTEE STATEMENT as required. The guarantor should be an adult, living
in Chinese mainland with a stable source of income. The application outcome is subject to updates in the application system.
★If necessary, university will require applicants to submit extra materials.
How to Apply:

1. Log on to http://istudent.fudan.edu.cn/apply, and enter University’s Online Application System.
2. The online application procedure is as follows:
   Registering and activating account→Filling in application→Submitting application→Paying application fee on Online
Application System→Waiting for application result.
3. How to apply online:
    （1）Please select Personal Application, Student Type: Language Student, Admission Institution: International
    Cultural Exchange School, Teaching Language: Chinese. Please keep “Program Name” blank. Click the search
    button to show the program list.
    （2）Then click to choose “Chinese Language Program” in the list. Then click “check major” button on the
    right.
    （3）Please click “Apply” to begin your application. Please upload all necessary materials required.

 ★Please ensure that all applicant personal information, including passport number, name, date of birth, place of birth,
passport issue date and expiry date, is entered exactly as shown on your passport.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

After online application submission and fee payment, please check your application status and feedback
regularly in the portal. Statuses will show “Under Review” or “Admitted”. If documents require revision
or supplementation, resubmit within the deadline. Once marked “Admitted”, download your admission
notice directly from the system.
You shall then apply for a Chinese visa at Chinese embassies or consulates overseas with your admission
documents and China Visa Application Form. Note your visa validity. Entry is permitted 1–2 days before
registration. Early entry may lead to visa expiry and affect your studies. Fudan can provide assistance with Visa
only after your registration in school.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

  The Visa Application Form for foreign students has been digitally certified:
 Students who study for 180 days or longer shall be certified with JW202 FORM;
 Students who study less than 180 days shall be certified with the new DQ FORM;
Students can log on the Study in China website (https://www.studyinchina.edu.cn), input name, passport
number and email to view or download your JW202/DQ FORM after the admission and related procedures are
completed.

★Please ensure that the validity of your passport can cover the study period when submitting the online
application. The passport shall not be renewed and the passport number shall not be changed until your
registration on campus in Fudan. Otherwise your JW202/DQ FORM will be invalid.
