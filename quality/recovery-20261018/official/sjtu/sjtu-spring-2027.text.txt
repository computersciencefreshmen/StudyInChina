2026 Pre-University Program forInternational Students
Introduction
Pre-University Program of LEC, SJTU is a non-degree program designed forinternational students to enhance their language skills, academic performance,and cultural adaptability. The students can thus lay a solidfoundation for their further undergraduate study. Pre-University studentscan get the priority of being accepted to the undergraduate program at SJTUupon passing the official assessment test.
Chinese Program: This program is designed for students who have already achieved a goodlevel of Chinese language proficiency (HSK 4≥180 or equivalent) but require remediation in academic subjects such asChinese, English, and Mathematics. The duration is one year. A limited numberof mid-term transfer slots are available for outstanding students in the secondsemester.
English Program: This program targets international students with English proficiency(TOEFL≥80, IELTS≥5.5, orequivalent) to advance their English, Mathematics, and other academic skills.This program is conducted in English and lasts for one year. A limited numberof mid-term transfer slots are available for outstanding students in the secondsemester.
Both programs offer three specialized tracks to meet students' further educationgoals:  Science, Engineering &Agriculture; Medicine; and Humanities & Social Sciences.

Eligibility
1.High school diploma and above.
2.Non-Chinese citizens holding a valid foreign passport, in compliance with therelevant regulations of the Chinese Ministry of Education, including the “Document No. 12 (2020) of Ministry ofEducation of the PRC" and etc.
3. Goodphysical and mental health, with no criminal record.
4.Applicants must be between the ages of 16 (inclusive) and 23 (exclusive).

Features
ThePre-University Program enables students to improve their academic level andadapt to university life. Meanwhile, enriching cultural experience, on-sitevisits, academic subject contests as well as artistic and athletic activitiesare arranged to help enhance students’ capability and competitiveness. Theprogram provides professional coaching for CSCA and HSK examinations, coupledwith dedicated undergraduate admissions counseling, offering end-to-end supportto guide international students through their successful entry into Chineseuniversities.
Faculty
Leveragingthe teaching and research advantages of Shanghai Jiao Tong University, theinstructors of the program have been meticulously chosen. They possess a wideinternational horizon, profound professional expertise, rich teachingexperience, high caliber, and strong competencies.

ApplicationMaterials
Allapplication materials and information provided by applicants must be authentic,accurate and fully complete. Otherwise, the applicant will be disqualified forapplication, enrollment or study.
1. Senior highschool diploma or equivalent qualification. Prospective graduates may provide anofficial high school transcript as proof of graduation.
2. High schoolprofile. The content should include the school's overall introduction,admission requirements, enrollment rate, curriculum system, grading system,university acceptance of graduates, etc..
3. Passportinformation page valid for more than 6 months. Once admitted, passportinformation cannot be changed until enrollment registration is completed.
4. A personalphotograph (color portrait, hat-free).
5. PersonalStatement (must be signed by the applicant; 600–1000 words) covering, butnot limited to: personal background, hobbies and strengths, reasons forapplying to the program, and study plans.
6. One recommendation letter, signedby the recommender and including their contact information (email address and phonenumber).
7. Academic certificates:
7.1 High school transcript(covering all courses completed during high school). Prospective graduates mustsubmit a transcript of all high school courses completed up to the applicationdate.
7.2 Standardized internationaltest scores or national/regional unified high school graduation or universityentrance exam results, such as SAT, AP, ACT, A-Level, IB, UEC, etc. If theabove results cannot be provided, a written explanation can be submitted.
8. Language certificate:
8.1 Chinese Program (1 year) applicants should provide HSKcertificate (HSK 4 score ≥ 180) or equivalentcertificate. Chinese Program (0.5 year) applicants should provide HSKcertificate (HSK 5 score ≥ 200, with Writing score ≥ 60) or equivalent certificate, such as documentation indicating Chineseas a native language.
8.2 English Program (1 year) applicants should provide oneof the following:
• TOEFL score report (totalscore ≥ 80)
• IELTS score report (totalscore ≥ 5.5)
• Proof of being a nativeEnglish speaker
• Certificate of at least twoyears of full-time academic study conducted in English
English Program (0.5 year) applicants should provideone of the following:
• TOEFL score report (totalscore ≥ 90)
• IELTS score report (total score ≥ 6.0)
• Proof of being a native English speaker
• Certificate of at least two years of full-timeacademic study conducted in English
9. Applicant's birth certificate.
10. The nationality or householdregistration of both the applicant's parents, such as passport or ID card.
11. Applicant whose one or bothparents are Chinese citizens should provide the following materials:
11.1 Certificate of theapplicant’s foreign nationality obtained at birth or afterward.
11.2 For applicants whoacquired foreign nationality at birth:
(a) If one or both parentswere Chinese citizens and had permanent residence abroad at the time of theapplicant's birth, provide the corresponding permanent residence proof of theparent(s).
(b) If one or both parentswere Chinese citizens and did not have permanent residence abroad at the timeof the applicant's birth, provide a certificate of the applicant's renunciationof Chinese nationality for at least four years by April 30 of the applicationyear.
11.3 For applicants who areoriginally Chinese citizens and later acquired foreign nationality, provideproof of cancellation of their Chinese residence registration and renunciationof Chinese nationality for at least four years by April 30 of the applicationyear.
11.4 All visas, residencepermits, and entry/exit records for China, starting from the applicant's firstpassport.
11.5 Statement of Integrity
Note: Applicants whopreviously held a "People's Republic of China Travel Document" or"People's Republic of China Exit and Entry Permit" must provide therelevant documentation as in section 11.3.
12. Applicants under the age of 18as of September 1, 2026 (inclusive) for the Fall intake, or as of February 1,2027 (inclusive) for the Spring intake, must submit a Guardian Guarantee Form.
13. Award certificates (if any).
14. Other supportive materialsrequired by SJTU.
Note:
① If any documents are not inChinese or English, notarized translations in either language are required.
② Hard copies of supportingmaterials are not necessary during the application process.
Application Procedures
1.Visit https://elearning.sjtu.edu.cn/register,register your account by following the instructions, and then enter the onlineapplication system;
2.Completethe application form as required, upload the electronic application materials.
3.Paythe application fee of CNY 800 and click to submit the application.
Interview
Theadmission decision is based on a comprehensive evaluation of all theapplication materials as well as integrative test (by case basis). Notificationsof interview (if applicable) arrangements will be sent via email.
Admission
1. Theadmission decision will be made based on a comprehensive evaluation. Applicantsmay check their pre-admission results in the application system https://elearning.sjtu.edu.cn/register.
2.  Upon receiving apre-admission notice, applicants are required to confirm theirenrollment via tuition fee payment in the applicationsystem. The payment shall be made within two weeks upon the releaseof the pre-admission results.
3. The"Admission Notice" for the Spring intake programs will be issued inbatches from the end of January, and those for the Fall intake programs will beissued in batches from the end of July.
4. Applicantsmay download the electronic admission notices through the applicationsystem. Physical copies of the admission notices will be provided duringregistration at the beginning of the semester.
Application Deadline
TheFall intake programs: 2026 July 31st (Students applying for Scholarshipshould submit their applications before 2026 July 1st )
TheSpring intake programs: 2027 January 15th
