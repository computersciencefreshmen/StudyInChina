Chinese Language Program

Published:2023-11-10
Chinese Language Learning

We offer different courses for international students with different Chinese levels.

1. Long-term Chinese Language Program (one semester or above)
We have three levels of Chinese language programs for international students with varing Chinese profiencies: Beginners, Intermediate and Advanced programs. There are several classes in each level. Students can choose the one that fits best with their learning.

Opening time: Every March and September.
