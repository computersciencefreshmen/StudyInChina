Chinese Language Student Enrollment Prospectus 2027

Published:2024-12-02

Chinese Language Program For Spring Semester, 2027



Ⅰ.Application Requirements

1.Non-Chinese citizens with a valid passport

2.Healthy, and abide by the laws and decrees of China

3.Academic record equivalent to a junior high school ,18-60 years of age


Ⅱ.Study period

March 2027---June 2027/ December 2027


Ⅲ. Application Deadline

November 30th, 2026


Ⅳ.Application Materials

1.Clear photocopy of passport’s front page

2.Highest diploma/degree certificate (in Chinese or English)

3.Personal Resume

Note: Students transferring from other Chinese universities must apply for a T visa in the city where their previous school is located, and provide the original certificate of completion and the original examination results from the previous school .


Ⅴ.Fees

1.Application fee:400RMB

2.Study fee:7000RMB /Semester

3.Dorm fee：留学费用-国际学院（新）


Ⅵ.Application procedure

1.Please log in the foreign student application system(Online Service Platform for International Students). Please choose the right major according to your study plan when you apply(one semester or one year).

2.After application passing pre-evaluation, please pay for the application fee(no refund for application fee).

3.We will send the Admission Letter and the form JW202 or DQ via email.
