2027 NPU Master Program Application Guide for International Students

Date of issue：2026-10-09 16:52:00 Hits：


Duration of Offered Program:

Duration: 2.5 years

Method of study: Full-time

Fall Semester Intake Only.


Major List for Master program:

2027 Master Major list: https://studyat.nwpu.edu.cn/info/1203/10691.htm


Application Period:

The first batch: November 1, 2026 - December 31, 2026, 17:00 (GMT+8).

Application submission after 17:00 (GMT+8) is automatically regarded as 2nd batch application.

The second batch: January 1, 2027-  February 28, 2027, 17:00 (GMT+8).

Application submission after 17:00 (GMT+8) is automatically regarded as 3rd batch application.

The third batch: March 1, 2027 - March 31, 2027, 17:00 (GMT+8).

Application submission after 17:00 (GMT+8) is automatically regarded as 4th batch application.

The fourth batch: April 1, 2027 - April 30, 2027, 17:00 (GMT+8).

Application submission after April 30, 2027, 17:00 (GMT+8) is automatically regarded as giving up.


  • CSC Type B Scholarship application deadline is February 28, 2027, 17:00 (GMT+8). Applicants are encouraged to apply early to have a higher chance of receiving a scholarship.


Admission Requirements:

Admission to NPU is based on academic merit and open competition. Before application, please ensure that you satisfy the admission requirements.

Depending on the competitiveness of each applicant cohort, admission criteria may vary from year to year.

1．Good physical and mental health (no infectious or contagious diseases and no physical or mental disorders that could interfere with regular study).

2．Applicants for master's program should be under 30 by September 1st, 2027.

3．Academic Requirement: By September 1st, 2027, applicants for master's program must hold a bachelor's degree.

4．Learning Ability: Applicants should have good learning ability and academic performance. The requirement for previous academic grade level is an average score of 75% and above. For CSC scholarship applicants, the requirement for previous academic grade level is an average score of 80% and above.

Applicants may provide other evidence of academic ability, such as the GRE and GMAT scores.

5．Language Requirement

Applicants should have Chinese or English language proficiency that meets the requirement.


Medium

HSK Level 5 (200 or above)

PTE Academic (58 or above)


6．Nationality Requirements: The applicants shall be non-Chinese citizens with valid passports.

For the applicants who used to be Chinese citizens but later immigrated to other countries, these applicants must hold the passports or citizenship certificates of their current countries for at least 4 years in addition to the 2-year “proof of residence” in recent 4 years.

Applicants whose one or both parents are Chinese citizens must provide one of the following supporting documents:

1) The certificate proving that the applicant obtained the foreign nationality at birth;

2) For applicants who used to be Chinese citizens but later immigrated to other countries, these applicants shall provide the certificates of cancellation of Chinese nationality.  


Application Documents

1. A copy of the personal information page of your valid passport.

  • The passport should be valid until December 31, 2027 at least. Applicants currently residing in Chinese mainland must also provide a scanned copy of the currently valid visa page or residence permit page.

  • If the applicant does not have a passport at the time for application, the national ID can be used instead. However the passport is one of the mandatory application documents. Applicants should apply for it and provide to Admissions Office as soon as possible.

2. A copy of undergraduate diploma.

  • Both degree and graduation certificate are asked to provide for applicants who finish the undergraduate program in China.

  • Graduating students can provide pre-graduation certificate, indicating the expected time for graduation. The original/notarized diploma undergraduate diploma must be provided for registration after admission.

3. Transcripts for each semester/academic year during undergraduate period.  

4. Recommendation letters from two professors ( associate professor or level above).

5. Study plan for Master Degree program (based on given template. Please check the template by clicking here).

6. Chinese (HSK)/English language certificate (IELTS or TOEFL or Duolingo or PTE).

Under these circumstances, the language certificate is not needed:

  • Applicants who are Chinese native speakers and applying for Chinese medium majors.

  • Applicants who are English native speakers and applying for English medium majors.

7. Non-criminal record (valid for six months).

8. Acceptance letter from a supervisor (Please check the template by clicking here).

  • The below schools require that the Master Program Applicants must have the acceptance letter from supervisors to complete the application:

  • For the applicants who apply for master majors in other schools, acceptance letter is not a must and will be a bonus point.

9. Officially recognized awards, scientific research achievements (if any).

10. Copy of "Physical Examination Form for Foreigners" (valid for six months), which shall be deemed invalid if it is incomplete, lacks the applicant’s photograph, has no cross-seal on the photograph, or is not signed and sealed by the examining physician and hospital.

11. Bank statement (with a closing balance no less than 5000USD).

12. Other supplementary materials required.

13. Students applying for architecture or design majors shall provide one’s own art/design portfolio.


Notes:

1) For the list of English-speaking countries, please refer to：https://studyat.nwpu.edu.cn/info/1192/6021.htm..

2)  Some schools have specific requirements on GPA or students’ language or Master program acceptance letter from professors:

  • School of Management requires GPA of 80% and above for master applicants.

  • School of Public Policy and Administration requires HSK level 6 (180 or above) for Chinese medium major applicants.

3)  All the above application materials must be in English or Chinese. Notarized English or Chinese translations are required if materials are in other languages.

4)  All academic documents submitted by an applicant electronically are checked to ensure they are legitimate before an offer is confirmed. Once students arrive at NPU, we will re-examine all the original and hard-copy documents.

5) Please do not renew your passport after application submission without special reasons. Otherwise, the information on the Visa Application Form (JW202 and JW201) will be inconsistent and it would affect your visa application.


Application and Selection Procedure:

Step 1 – Documents Preparation

Applicants must meet all requirements and prepare application materials accordingly.

Step 2 – Complete online application and psychological evaluation

Create an account and log into the NPU online application portal at (http://admission.nwpu.edu.cn/) to complete the application and psychological evaluation.

  • The NPU online application portal is the only acceptable application gate. After completing the application, a non-refundable fee (600 CNY/ about 90 USD) must be paid to validate the application.

  • A valid e-mail address should be used to register for the online application system. Applicants should make sure this e-mail address can be used until registration time. The Admissions Office will send application results and notifications to this email address.

  • Applicants must upload required documents before deadline. Incomplete applications, such as those that have not been submitted or lack application documents, will not be considered and processed.

Step 3 - Application Materials Review

1．The Admissions Office will review the authenticity of materials.

2．Academic schools will review the applicants’ academic competency within three days after each deadline. All qualified applicants will be invited for a follow-up interview or examination.

  • During application period, applicants need to promptly pay attention to the review status on the application system and their e-mail for process update and feedback as well.

  • Additionally, applicants are asked to reply related email on time. Applicants who fail to respond to the emails in a timely manner or fail to submit supplementary materials as required will be considered as forfeiting their applications.

Step 4 – Examination/ Interview

1．Interviews will be arranged in 7-10 working days after each deadline.

  • Qualified applicants will receive e-mail notifications regarding the examination and interview method, content and time. Applicants should attend the examination or interview according to specified instructions.

2．The Admissions office will announce the results via e-mail within two weeks after the examination or interview.

Step 5 – Initial Fee / Registration Deposit

Pre-admitted students must pay the initial fee / registration deposit. The amount is 3000 CNY for all the candidates. This fee will be refunded in a lump sum after the student completes registration (expected in December of the same year), on condition that the student maintains active student status at the time of refund.

Step 6 - Issuing of Admission Documents

1．CSC candidates: Please log in to your CSC account and download the “confirmation form for study in China” (JW201 ) and Admission Notice.

2．Others: Admission Notice and “confirmation form for study in China” (JW202 ) will be sent to the email address applicants used for NPU application portal registration. Please download the documents promptly.

  • Admitted candidates should go to Chinese embassies (consulates) abroad to apply for study (X1) visa with passport, Admission Notice and "confirmation form for study in China" and arrive at NPU for registration on time.

Step 7 - Registration

1. Online Registration: Candidates should complete their online registration at early August, 2027. Specific date will be announced in the official notification email.

2. On-site Registration: The time is around the end of August, 2027. Specific date will be announced in the admission notice and official notification email. Candidates who fail to register as requested without a valid reason will have their admission and scholarship canceled. Please note that early registration is not recommended.

  • Admitted candidates should bring all the required documents to Admissions Office for on-site registration, the documents are including:

Original Documents1. Passport
2. Bachelor degree diploma
3. Transcripts for each semester/academic year during undergraduate period
4. Physical Examination Form for Foreigners (Includes Blood Test Report and X-Ray Report)

Notes：

1.  All the aforementioned materials must be in English or Chinese. Materials in other languages must be accompanied by a notarized English or Chinese translation.

2. If your undergraduate studies were completed in China, the Bachelor graduation certificate must be provided.

3. Applicants who fail to provide the above materials, or whose original documents are inconsistent with the scanned copies submitted during the application, will have their admission qualification canceled.

4. Applicants whose physical examination records are inconsistent with their actual health status will have their admission qualification canceled.

5. For the rest of the documents required for registration, the International College will issue a registration guideline with all relevant information around July.

Scholarship Opportunities

NPU provides multi-type scholarships which covers all levels in different perspectives. We wish to improve students life and support more youth to strive for excellence and explore their potential.

1. Scholarship details and application method

CSC Scholarship: https://studyat.nwpu.edu.cn/SCHOLARSHIPS1/Chinese_Government_Scholarships.htm

NPU President Scholarship: https://studyat.nwpu.edu.cn/SCHOLARSHIPS1/NPU_President_Scholarship.htm

Xi’an Belt and Road Scholarship: https://studyat.nwpu.edu.cn/SCHOLARSHIPS1/Xi_an_City.htm

2. Scholarship Result Announcement

1) Scholarship review will be processed according to scholarship type, requirements and application batches. Applicants will receive the result announcement via registered email for NPU online application portal. Early applicants will be given merit-based priority.

2) No applicant may hold multiple scholarships. Only one scholarship, either from China Scholarship Council or NPU, will be granted to each eligible candidate.

3) After being admitted by NPU, applicants will not be able to change their programs, majors, learning language, or study period. Any special circumstances may require additional approvals from the university.


Process supervision

1. The entire selection and admission process will be strictly regulated by the university and will adhere to the principle of openness, fairness, and academic integrity.

2. NPU takes the possibility of admissions fraud seriously and the admissions team has expertise and experienced in fraud detection. The University takes robust action against applicants with fake or altered application documents, or other false or misleading information. If we find that fake documents or false information has been submitted, we will withdraw the application and any others made by the same person. If this is discovered after the individual has matriculated at the University, the University reserves the right to terminate their studies. When investigating potential fraud, we may disclose application data to third parties.

3. For admitted freshmen, NPU will have regular verification for eligibility of new students before registration. If any applicant with issues are identified, NPU reserves the right to cancel their applicant's admission qualification after verification and refuse to process, visa residence and other procedures.

4. Disposition of other violations will be in accordance with the relevant provisions issued by the Ministry of Education of China.
