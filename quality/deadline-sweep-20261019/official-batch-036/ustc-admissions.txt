The University currently offers 35 undergraduate programs across 13 disciplines for international students, including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Materials Science, Life Sciences, Engineering Sciences, Electronic Information, Computer Science, Geophysics, Environmental Science and Engineering, Management Science, Cyberspace Security, and Nuclear Engineering.

Master’s Programs
The University offers 91 master’s programs and 97 doctoral programs open to international students. Fields of study include Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Materials Science and Engineering, Electronic Information, Computer Science and Technology, Geophysics, and Environmental Science and Engineering, among others.

Doctoral Programs
The Suzhou Institute of Advanced Research is located in Suzhou Industrial Park (SIP). It currently offers English-taught master’s programs for international students, supported by a strong faculty team, innovative curriculum design, and a highly advantageous geographic location.
