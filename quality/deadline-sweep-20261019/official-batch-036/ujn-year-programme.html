Chinese Language and Culture (One-semester/One-year Program)

Spring Semester: March-July

Fall Semester: September-January

Beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels are available, including compulsory courses such as grammar, listening, speaking, reading, and writing, with 20 hours per week.Students also have the opportunity to experience traditional Chinese culture such as Peking Opera, Tai Chi, calligraphy, Chinese painting, paper-cutting, martial arts, etc..
