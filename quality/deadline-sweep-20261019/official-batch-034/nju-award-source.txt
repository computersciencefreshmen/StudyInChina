About Us
Overview
Nanjing University is one of the top nine universities (C9 League) in China, and also one of the first universities to enroll and cultivate international students in China. It has hosted more than 20,000 international students from over 120 countries since from 1955, with prominent international alumni in politics, business, academia and other fields.
