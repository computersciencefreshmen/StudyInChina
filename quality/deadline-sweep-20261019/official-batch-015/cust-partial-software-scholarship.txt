Program Categories, Duration and Language of Instruction
a. This scholarship only supports undergraduate programs are taught in English, include:
- Software engineering
b. Programs are taught in English.

Reminder:
1.Apply for ‘The Scholarship for English-taught Bachelor degree’: choose ‘The Scholarship for English-taught Bachelor degree’ in option of ‘Select program’ on ‘Select program’ page, choose ‘The Scholarship for English-taught Bachelor degree’ in option of ‘study type’ on ‘Please Select Study Type’ page.
2.The basic requirements for scholarships for current undergraduates: The attendance rate should be above 85%, and there should be no record of violation of laws and disciplines.
Application Deadline
Deadline of Applicants is December 20th for Spring intake, and May 31st for Autumn intake.
Application Procedure
a. Apply online:
Please visit http://cust.apply.cucas.cn to apply, choose ‘The Scholarship for English-taught Bachelor degree’ in option of ‘Select program’ on ‘Select program’ page, choose ‘The Scholarship for English-taught Bachelor degree’ in option of ‘study type’ on ‘Please Select Study Type’ page.
Eligibility
1. Non-Chinese Citizen, in good health and under the age of 30.
2. High school diploma or above.
3. Applicants from non-native English speaking must have IELTS 5.5, TOEFL (IBT) 72 or above, or pass the English test organized by the school (the test time is approximately June 2023, and the specific time will be notified separately)
Application Documents (in duplicate)
1. Application form.
2. A copy of a valid passport, including name, passport number, expiration date, and photo.
3. A photo consistent with the passport of the applicant.
4. A copy of high school graduation certificate: A notarized copy is also required if it is in a text other than Chinese or English.
5. High school transcript.
6. Certificate for English proficiency , such as IELTS or TOEFL scores, only for Non-English native speakers.
7. A copy of visa: Only for students who are already in China.
8. Applicants under the age of 18 must submit relevant legal documents from their legal guardians in China..
9. A copy of the Physical Examination Form for Foreigners within its validity period of 6 months .
10. A certificate of no criminal record within its validity period of 6 months.
11. A life photo of the full body taken within 3 months and a full-body video in 10 seconds.
12. Talents in art, sports, and other fields are preferred. Please provide certificates or performance videos.
