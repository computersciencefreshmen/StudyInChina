Liaoning University School of International Economics and Politics/Digital Economy Modern Industry College Master's Admissions Guide for Finance and International Business
2025-12-27 10:15
Ⅰ. The professional profile
The Master of Finance and the Master of International Business offered by School of International Economics and Politics/School of Modern Industry of Digital Economy of Liaoning University are all English-language teaching programs based on profound academic background. Relying on the national "double first-class" construction discipline of applied economics, inheriting the profound accumulation of related disciplines, we are committed to cultivating high-level applied talents with global vision, solid theoretical foundation and strong practical ability, which meets the development needs of the international financial market and the international business field under the trend of economic globalization, especially serving the talent development needs of Sino-Russian financial cooperation and cross-border business cooperation between China and Russia and Northeast Asia. Among them, the Master of Finance, as an important part of applied economics, inherits the excellent academic advantages of finance; The master of international business is an important carrier for cultivating high-quality professionals in the open economy system, and it inherits the profound accumulation of international economics and trade disciplines.
Ⅱ. The core advantages
1. Top academic foundation and strong discipline strength.
Relying on the discipline of applied economics of Liaoning University, this discipline was selected as the national "double-first-class" construction discipline for two consecutive rounds in 2017 and 2022. It is the only discipline in Liaoning provincial universities that won this honor, and it is also the only national "double-first-class" construction discipline among the economic disciplines in Northeast China. The discipline of finance has a long history. It was founded in the late 1970s. Under the guidance of Chen Jiasheng, Bai Qingxian, He Guosheng and other well-known domestic scholars, it has accumulated fruitful results. It has been awarded as an undergraduate demonstration major in ordinary colleges and universities in Liaoning Province and a key undergraduate support major in ordinary colleges and universities in Liaoning Province, and its academic influence and industry recognition have been continuously improved.
2. Excellent teaching staff and excellent resource platform.
The professional teaching team consists of 41 full-time teachers, including 13 professors, 15 associate professors and 9 doctoral supervisors. The team members cover top scholars such as national high-level talents, chief experts of major projects of the National Social Science Fund, and winners of special allowances from the State Council, and their teaching and research capabilities are outstanding. The college has deep cooperation with top institutions such as Peking University New jiegou Research Institute and the Institute of International Trade and Economic Cooperation of the Ministry of Commerce to build high-end research centers and think tanks; At the same time, there are characteristic platforms such as Northeast Asia Research Institute of Liaoning University and regional country research institutes to provide students with rich academic resources and practical opportunities.
3. Internationalized teaching mode, with close communication between China and Russia.
The whole course is taught in English, and the teachers have international academic background or rich practical experience in the industry, ensuring that the teaching content is in line with the forefront of the international financial field. The College has established extensive inter-institutional exchange and cooperation relations with universities in Russia, the United States, Australia, Japan, South Korea and other countries, paying special attention to academic interaction with Russian universities, providing students with opportunities to exchange and study in Russia and participate in Sino-Russian joint scientific research projects, and helping to expand their international horizons.
4. all-round talent training, giving consideration to theory and practice.
The curriculum system integrates solid economic and financial theories and practical applications. The core courses cover financial marketing, corporate finance, risk management, international finance and other fields, and special modules such as the dynamics of Sino-Russian financial cooperation and the financial market in Northeast Asia are added to meet the academic interests and career development needs of Russian students. Encourage students to participate in high-level competitions such as "internet plus University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition" and National Financial Professional Competition. At the same time, relying on the think tank resources and cooperation institutions of the college, provide students with internship positions to enhance their ability to solve practical financial problems.
Ⅲ. The enrollment requirements
1. non-China citizens, healthy in body and mind, under 40 years of age.
2. he has obtained or will soon obtain a bachelor's degree from a recognized university at home and abroad, with a professional background in economics, finance, management and related fields; Non-related majors can also apply if they have strong mathematical analysis ability and logical thinking ability.
3. applicants who choose English as the professional teaching language must have an English level of IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90, or have passed the interview of an English expert group organized by Liaoning University.
Ⅳ. The cost
Full-time graduate students majoring in finance are 25,000 yuan/student/year, with a schooling of 2 years, totaling 50,000 yuan.
V. Application materials
1. A notarized certificate of the highest academic qualifications.
A notarized certificate of undergraduate education is required. If the applicant is a student at school, a pre-graduation certificate issued by the school where he/she attends should be submitted separately. If it is in a language other than Chinese or English, it is necessary to provide another Chinese and English translation and notarize it.
2. Transcripts of undergraduate study.
The scanned transcripts should include the results of undergraduate studies up to the latest semester. Transcripts should be issued and sealed by the academic affairs office, graduate school or relevant student management department of the school. If it is in a language other than Chinese or English, it is necessary to provide another Chinese and English translation and notarize it.
3. Proof of study or employment.
For students who have not graduated, please provide proof of attendance or pre-graduation certificate, and indicate the pre-graduation date; If the applicant is employed, he/she must provide the employment certificate issued by the employment unit. If it is in a language other than Chinese or English, a Chinese-English translation is required.
4. Study and research plan in China.
Please write in English, more than 2500 words.
5. Physical Examination Form for Foreigners (see Annex).
The applicant shall conduct the examination in strict accordance with the items required in the Physical Examination Form for Foreigners. Requirements: Fill in the form in English, and the physical examination items should not be missing. Paste my photo and affix the seal of the physical examination unit on the photo. The physical examination form needs the signature of the doctor and the seal of the hospital. Physical examination records are valid for 6 months.
6. Letters of recommendation from two professors or associate professors.
The recommendation letter should be written in Chinese or English, and the recommender should be an associate professor or professor. The contents should include the applicant's study objectives in China, the evaluation of students' comprehensive ability and future development, etc.
7. A scanned copy of the first page of the applicant's passport and a blank page of the visa.
Applicants who have studied in China are required to submit a passport and visa page.
8. Proof of language ability.
English level must reach IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90 (the certificate must be within the validity period), or pass the interview of English expert group organized by Liaoning University.
9. No criminal record certificate.
No criminal record certificate, which needs to be issued by the public security or government and other relevant departments, and is valid for 6 months.
10. Other accessory materials.
List of published articles and scientific research achievements in professional fields, as well as representative works or abstracts, award certificates, etc.
VI. Application Process
1. Online application: Applicants who meet the enrollment requirements should carefully prepare the application materials, visit https://lnu.17gz.org/member/login.do, register by email and log in to the "Liaoning University International Student Online Service System", and upload the electronic application materials as required.
2. Material review: The Admissions Committee will strictly review the application materials and evaluate the applicant's academic background, professional potential and English ability.
3. Payment of registration fee: If the application is passed in the first instance, 800 yuan RMB registration fee shall be paid.
The payment method of registration fee will be sent to the applicant's mailbox through the only official email address of our school (studyinlnu@lnu.edu.cn).
4. Written examination and interview assessment: Liaoning University adopts written examination and interview assessment to examine the applicant's attitude towards China, academic performance, scientific research ability, language level, comprehensive quality, development potential, health status and no criminal record, and select and recommend candidates. The written test and interview will be conducted through the Internet, and the specific time will be notified to the personal contact email address submitted by the applicant through the official email address of the International Student Admissions Office (studyinlnu@lnu.edu.cn).
5. Issuance of admission notice: Applicants who pass the examination will receive formal admission notice and materials required for visa application.
Ⅶ. the academic system and graduation requirements
Full-time postgraduate program of international business: 2 years.
Ⅷ. Graduation requirements
You can get the master's degree certificate and graduate diploma issued by Liaoning University by completing the required courses, achieving qualified grades, completing the required credits, and passing the defense of master's degree thesis.
IX. Employment Prospects
1. Finance major
Graduates of this major have strong competitiveness in the financial field at home and abroad, and their main employment directions include:
International financial institutions, commercial banks, securities companies, insurance companies and other financial enterprises are engaged in investment analysis, risk management and cross-border financial business.
Economic and financial management departments of governments, international organizations (such as BRICS New Development Bank) and Sino-Russian joint ventures participate in international economic cooperation and cross-border financial projects.
Universities and scientific research institutions, engaged in teaching or research in the financial field, have advantages in the special fields such as Sino-Russian financial cooperation and economic research in Northeast Asia.
2. Major in International Business
Graduates of this major have strong competitiveness in the field of international business, and their main employment directions include:
Multinational companies, foreign trade enterprises, cross-border e-commerce platforms, international logistics enterprises, etc., engaged in cross-border trade operations, international business negotiations, overseas market expansion, etc.;
Commercial banks, international settlement institutions, foreign-related insurance companies and other financial enterprises engage in international financial business and cross-border payment and settlement;
Foreign-related economic management departments of governments, international organizations, Sino-Russian joint ventures, etc. participate in international economic cooperation and cross-border business policy implementation;
Universities and scientific research institutions are engaged in teaching or research in the field of international business, especially in the cross-border business cooperation between China and Russia and trade research in Northeast Asia.
