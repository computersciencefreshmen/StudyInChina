1.Brief Introduction of NCEPU

North China Electric Power University (NCEPU) is a national key university, directly under the leadership of the Ministry of Education. NCEPU has two campuses in Beijing and Baoding, and now has about 3,000 faculty members, over 20,000 full-time undergraduate students and nearly 10,000 post-graduate students. It covers an area of over 1,600 acres with floorage of over 1 million square meters. NCEPU was founded in 1958 in Beijing, jointly administrated by the Ministry of Education and the university Council. The Council involves 12 state owned companies, including State Grid Corporation of China, etc.

Over the past century, NCEPU has undertaken the historic mission of cultivating highly competent talents for the country's energy and power industry and promoting scientific and technological progress in this field. Upon the entry of the new century, NCEPU has upheld the principle of “establishing the university by disciplinary construction, strengthening the university by talents cultivation, thriving the university through scientific research, and developing the university with distinctive characteristics”. NCEPU has seized development opportunities and achieved great progress.

NCEPU has 15 schools, totally 67 undergraduate majors, including the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, School of Energy Power and Mechanical Engineering, School of Control and Computer Engineering, School of Economics and Management, School of Environmental Science and Engineering, School of New Energy, School of Hydraulic and Hydropower Engineering, etc. All these forms a complete system to train undergraduates, masters, and doctorate students.

Relying on the university council, NCEPU has constantly deepened the university-industry-research cooperation and reached strategic cooperation relations with about 100 large domestic and overseas energy and power enterprises to jointly undertake important researches and development projects to accelerate exploitation and industrialization of scientific and technological achievements. It has successively obtained two titles of Special Contribution Unit of Demonstration Project for Ultra-high Voltage Alternating and Direct Current Experiment of State Grid Corporation of China. NCEPU has constructed the multifaceted university-industry-research cooperation platform, and signed framework cooperation agreement with more than 20 local governments to deepen cooperation on strategic emerging industries and achieved great progress on promoting local economic development. NCEPU is an active participator in the promotion of interscholastic cooperation, and as the major sponsor it has participated in establishing the Beijing High-Tech Universities Alliance for complementary advantages and resource sharing and encourage cooperative innovation among universities.

The university has been engaged in the internationalized education, and developed substantive communication and cooperation with about 140 internationally renowned universities and research institutes. Meanwhile, it has established the credit transfer and recognition system with partner universities and set up programs of regular student exchange, scientific research cooperation and experts reciprocal visits. NCEPU has actively implemented the Belt and Road Initiative and foreign aid training programs. As the leading university of China in the energy science domain at the Shanghai Cooperation Organization University, NECPU has established a think-tank in energy sector for SCO University.

As the research-oriented leading university in the field of electric power, at a new historical juncture, NCEPU undertakes the honor and dream of the era and actively fulfills the historical task to cultivate top-level innovative talents, create high-level scientific research achievements, and provide first-class service for the country and society.

Undergraduate Applicants' University Entrance Examination Regulations

1. Overview of the University Entrance Examination

Starting from the 2026/2027 academic year, applicants who apply for undergraduate programs must take the examination before submitting their applications. The examination score shall be one of the essential materials for each institution to review and make admission decisions.

2. Subjects of the University Entrance Examination

The examination consists of 4 subjects: Professional Chinese, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.

- Students choosing the major of Business Administration must take a mathematics examination.

-Students choosing the majors of Electrical Engineering and Its Automation, Energy and Power Engineering, and New Energy Science and Engineering are required to take the mathematics and physics examinations.

3. Examination Information

For specific information about examination centers, please refer to the official announcements on the website (www.csca.cn).

In order to ensure that students can smoothly cover their study and living expenses during the school, the university requires all applicants to submit valid bank deposit proof along with the application materials. This proof should show that the applicant or his sponsor has sufficient financial means to support the applicant's estimated expenses throughout the study period, including but not limited to tuition, accommodation, living expenses and other expenses that may be incurred.

‌Specific requirements are as follows: ‌ :

‌1.Deposit certificate: It must be issued by a regular bank with the official seal of the bank and is valid within the last three months.

‌2.Amount required: The total deposit must reach RMB 70,000.

‌3.Account information: It is best to use your own account, if using a parent's account, you need to provide additional proof of relationship. The certificate should clearly show the account holder's name, account number, bank information and deposit balance and other key information.

‌4.Translation requirements: If the deposit certificate is not in Chinese or English, an officially recognized translation must be provided.

‌5.How to submit: Please upload this proof to the online application system together with other application materials.

Please note that failure to submit the required bank statement on time may affect the progress of your application or the outcome of your admission. We encourage applicants to prepare in advance and ensure the authenticity and validity of all financial documents.

8. Scholarship

Chinese Government Scholarship （CGS/CSC）

CGS/CSC only supports master and PHD students.

Applicants should submit application to CSC through related local governmental department. Students who need pre-admission should send all required application documents for CSC to icdfs@ncepu.edu.cn.

For more information, please check http://www.campuschina.org.

Chinese Government Scholarship—Chinese University Program (CGS/CSC-CUP)

CGS/CSC-CUP only supports master and PHD students.

For more information, please check http://www.campuschina.org and http://studyatncepu.ncepu.edu.cn.

Online Application: http://study.ncepu.edu.cn/

Beijing Government Scholarship（BGS）

BGS only supports bachelor students.

For more information, please check http://studyatncepu.ncepu.edu.cn.

International Education Institute Scholarship (IEIS)

IEIS only supports bachelor students.

For more information, please check http://studyatncepu.ncepu.edu.cn.

Confucius Institute Scholarship（CIS）

CIS is a full scholarship. This scholarship only supports Chinese Language students who are recommended by local Confucius Institute. Study period can be four weeks, one semester or one year.

The application time depends on the local Confucius Institute.

Applicants shall log on to the Confucius Institute Scholarship website at http://cis.chinese.cn, set up an individual account, complete the Confucius Institute Scholarship Application form online, print out the form and sign it.
