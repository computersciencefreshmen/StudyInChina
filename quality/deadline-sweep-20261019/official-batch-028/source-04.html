International Students

HOME > Admissions > International Students

Ningbo University offers a variety of programs, including degree programs (bachelor's, master’s and doctoral programs), non-degree programs (general scholars and senior scholars) and non-degree Chinese language programs.

Various scholarships are available for students such as Chinese Government Scholarship (Type A, Type B, Type C), Confucius Institute Scholarship, Zhejiang Government Scholarship, Ningbo Government Scholarship, Ningbo University Scholarship and etc..

In academic year 2019-2020, 20 bachelor’s programs, 28 postgraduate programs (including joint programs) will be offered for international students. Furthermore, students who want to learn Chinese and experience Chinese culture can undertake non-degree Chinese language program.

For more information, please visit international students admission websitehttp://is.nbu.edu.cn, where you can apply online.
