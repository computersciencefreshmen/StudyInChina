FOUNDATION PROGRAM

You are here：首页>English>ADMISSIONS>FOUNDATION PROGRAM

 

Tianjin University(TJU) is one of the first batch pilot universities to provide foundation program for international undergraduates awarded with Chinese Government Scholarships under the Ministry of Education of China. Since 2005, nearly 2000 international students have studied at and graduated from TJU with solid Chinese language skills, strong adaptability in degree program learning as well as high-level intercultural communication competence. The foundation program at TJU is designed for students who plan to pursue science and engineering for further study, as the aim of foundation program is to arm students with a systematic study of general Chinese, technical Chinese, mathematics, physics and chemistry to help students proceed to degree programs of various disciplines at TJU or other renowned higher education institutions. At the same time, relying on the advantageous disciplines of Tianjin University, the foundation program is created to combine the characteristics of economics, management and commerce, laying a solid foundation for cultivating professional economic and management-oriented talents, and assisting learners in their future progress in the disciplines of economics, management and commerce.

At present, nearly 200 international students from more than 30 countries supported by Chinese Government Scholarship or at their own expenses are studying foundation program at Tianjin University. For academic year 2027, TJU will continue to provide foundation program study for international students, and the specifics are as follows:

II. Features and Strengths 

1. Small class teaching, with teachers providing more support and attention.

2. The course is rich in content, helping to expand vision and future planning.

3. Applicants who meet the assessment requirements have priority for scholarship opportunities.

III. Qualification of Applicants 

1. Applicants shall be non-Chinese citizens with a valid passport, and the conditions set by the Document No. 12 (2020) of Ministry of Education of the PRC should be met. 

2. Applicants shall be in good physical and mental health and hold a high school diploma.  

3. Applicant should be between 18 and 24 years of age. Applicants who are still under the age of 18 as of September 1st, 2027 must submit the original guardian certificate issued by the Tianjin District Notary Office, and the Guardian must be a permanent resident of Tianjin.

V. Application Procedure 

Step 1: Register online 

Applicants can visit TJU International Student Service System (ISSS) and register an account to log in and start the application. 

Website of TJU ISSS：https://tju.at0086.cn/StuApplication/Login.aspx 

Step 2: Submit online application. 

Applicants should fill in the online application with true and accurate information and upload the necessary documents as required. Then applicants can submit application online. 

  

VI. Application Checklist 

1. Photocopy of passport. 

2. Academic Transcript. 

3. Highest Diploma. (Pre-graduation certificate for applicants who have not obtained the graduation certificate.) 

4. Study and working Experience Resume: The start and end time of each education experience must be uninterrupted, and education level should start from senior middle school. (Click to download the template)

5. Valid Foreigner Physical Examination Report. Click to download the template. (The medical examinations must cover all the items listed and should be conducted after October, 2026). 

6. Certificate of Non-Criminal Records for one-year program applicants. 

7. HSK 4 Certificate and above, or Chinese Learning Experience Certificate of one-year and more for one semester program; HSK 3 Certificate and above, or Chinese Learning Experience Certificate of one-year and more for one year program. 

8. Applicants who are currently studying in China must submit a copy of their passport visa page and a certificate of school performance issued by their current institution. Applicants must truthfully disclose any previous study experiences in China. If it is found that any information has been concealed or misrepresented, the university reserves the right to unconditionally cancel the applicant’s admission.

Note:  

1. All uploaded documents must be in Chinese/English; otherwise notarial translations in Chinese/English are required. Once translated, the original documents and certified translations should be submitted together to the application system.

2. TJU has the right to request the applicants to provide the original or notarized hard copies of application documents for further qualification checks if the uploaded documents are not sufficient. Applicants who provide fake materials will be disqualified from admission. 

VII. Admission 

Applicants can check admission status via International Student Service System (ISSS) after submitting the online application. TJU will issue Admission Notice and visa documents to those admitted.

5. Tuition Refund

(1) Applicants who have successfully prepaid tuition may apply for a refund under the following circumstances:

A. Applicants who confirm in writing within 60 days of successful payment that they are withdrawing their application will receive a refund in RMB, based on the actual amount received by the university, to a bank account in China. The applicant shall bear any exchange rate differences and transaction fees, settled at the exchange rate of the day.

B. Visa rejection due to reasons beyond personal control: Applicants may apply for a refund by submitting an official visa rejection certificate from the embassy/consulate and a written refund application. The refund amount and requirements are as stated above.

(2) Prepaid tuition shall NOT be refunded to applicants who have successfully paid under the following circumstances:

A. Applicants who confirm withdrawal more than 60 days from the date of successful payment (date of actual receipt by the university).

B. Admitted students who fail to register.

C. Visa rejection due to personal reasons.

D. No refunds will be issued if more than two weeks have passed after the new semester begins or if a student requests a transfer or withdrawal after the enrollment.

Note: The application fee is non-refundable once paid.
