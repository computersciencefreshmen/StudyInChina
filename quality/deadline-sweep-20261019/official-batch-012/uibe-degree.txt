Majors
Economics
International Economics and Trade
Finance
Business Administration
Marketing
International Politics
International Business
Artificial Intelligence
Economic Development Cooperation & Polymer Materials and Engineering 2+2 （UIBE+BUCT）
Application Period
Spring Semester: October 20 - December 31 each year
Fall Semester: March 20 - June 30 each year
(Please visit http://sie.uibe.edu.cn and apply online.)
Application Requirements
1. High school diploma, good health
2. Applicants under 18 need to submit notarized guardianship certificate upon the approval of UIBE (For detailed information, please refer to Admissions Office).
Application Materials
(Application materials must be prepared in either Chinese or English and will not be returned to candidates nomatter they are accepted or not.)
1. UIBE Application Form (Apply online)
2. A copy of high school graduation certificate and transcripts
3. A copy of guarantor’s passport or ID
4. CSCA Academic Proficiency Test Certificate for Undergraduate Admission to Study in China (Test subjects: Mathematics) (https://csca.cn/about/examintro)
5. A copy of passport or other identification
6. Two recent passport photos, white background, frontal view, no hat
7. A copy of TOEFL, or IELTS, or GRE, or Cambridge International AS, A Level, IGCSE, or IB scores report, or equivalent. The minimum score for TOEFL is 86 and 6.0 for IELTS, or BBC level or above for A Level, satisfactory GRE scores, or equivalent, (If high school courses are taught in English, please provide official certificate that the teaching language was fully in English).
8. Asset certificate(if needed,i.e. current deposit on the bank statement shall be able to cover program tuition)
9. Certificate of No Criminal Conviction
Application Fee: RMB 660 (Non-refundable)
Advance Payment: RMB 10,000 (Advance payment is not refundable and should be paid after the confirmation of enrollment. It will be counted into the total tuition fee after registration.)
