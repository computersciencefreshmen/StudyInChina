Beijing International Students Scholarship

 

Beijing International Students Scholarship is established by the university to encourage self-funded students to work hard on their study and research, so as to cultivate high-level talents with excellent conduct and academic performance.


Ⅰ.ELIGIBILITY

1.Be a non-Chinese citizen;

2.Be friendly to China, have no criminal record, abide by the laws and regulations ofChina and the rules and regulations of the Host Institution;

3.Be in good physical and mental health,be in good academic and behavioral standing;

4.In principle, the bachelor's degree should not exceed 30 years old, the master's degree should not exceed 35 years old, and the doctor's degree should not exceed 40 years old (as of September 1, 2026).

 

Ⅱ. Supporting Categories and Scholarship Coverage

This scholarship is open to applicants of Bachelor’s Degree, Master’s Degree and PhD programs.

This scholarship only covers the first year tuition fee and excludes other fees.

 

Ⅲ. Application Process

1. An applicant shall download “Beijing International Students Scholarship Form (Only for Freshmen)” from the website of Beijing language and culture university.

2. An applicant shall fill in the application information online via BLCU Application System (https://apply.blcu.edu.cn) and upload relevant materials according to the course you are applying for and the "Beijing International Students Scholarship form (Only for Freshmen)"; 

3. Only after the approval of International Student Scholarship Committee, the successful candidates will be awarded the scholarship, and “Admission Notice for Beijing International Students Scholarship” will be issued. Otherwise, the applicants shall study on their own expenses.

4. If the applicant decides not to study at BLCU, the application materials will not be returned and application fee will not be refunded.

 

Ⅳ. Application Dates and Admission Results

If enrolled in September 2026: Please submit your application before July 15, 2026, and the scholarship results will be announced in August 2026.

If enrolled in March 2027: Please submit your application before January 12, 2027, and the scholarship results will be announced in February 2027;

 

Ⅴ. Application Materials

1. Applicants for Bachelor’s Degree, please refer to: https://admission.blcu.edu.cn/en/39/list.htm

2. Applicants for Master’s Degree and PhD programs, please refer to: https://admission.blcu.edu.cn/en/376/list.htm
