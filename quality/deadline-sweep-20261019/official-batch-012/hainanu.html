2026-2027 Admission Guide of Chinese Language and Culture program
日期：2025.12.09 点击：

2026-2027 Admission Guide of

Chinese Language and Culture program 

I. A Brief Introduction of the University and Program

Hainan University is a university participating in China's "Double First-Class" Initiative and a university co-constructed by the Ministry of Education and Hainan Province. On April 13, 2018, General Secretary Xi Jinping pointed out in his important speech at the Grand Meeting Celebrating the 30th Anniversary of Hainan becoming a Province and establishing a Special Economic Zone that "efforts should be made to support Hainan University in building world-class disciplines", which has provided fundamental guidance for the development of the university.


The University adopts a strategy of openness in education and has established all-round, multi-level and extensive exchanges and cooperation. It is the first institute in Hainan Province that is qualified to receive Chinese government scholarships for international students to study in China, it has been selected as the Demonstration Base for Studying in China by the Ministry of Education and the Chinese Language Education Base by the Overseas Chinese Affairs Office of the State Council, and has won the honorary title of “Advanced Group of Education for International Students in China”.


At present, there are six different levels (A-F) for Chinese Language and Culture program learners according to their Chinese proficiency. Teachers in Hainan University are friendly, responsible and experienced. The School aims at not only improving the students’ language ability but also deepening their understanding of Chinese culture and enhancing their capacity of the practical use of Chinese language by organizing various activities, to help create a better study environment for international students. Students who have good class attendance and pass the final examination shall be awarded with a Certificate of Completion.

III. Application Requirements

Applicants should be in good health, between 18 and 60 years old. Well-behaved and capable of living independently, fulfill all the conditions of Chinese Entry-exit, observe Chinese laws and university’s regulations, and respect Chinese culture and customs.

IV. Application Deadline

Fall semester: July 10,  2026 (Semester starts from the beginning of September 2026)

Spring semester: December 31, 2026 (Semester starts from the beginning of March 2027)

V. Application Method

Applicants should register and complete the online application system at https://hainanu.at0086.cn/StuApplication/Login.aspx , upload the required materials below and pay the application fee (can be paid online) before the application deadline.

(1) Valid Passport Copy (the expiry date of the passport should be more than 1 year);

(2) Highest academic diploma and transcript（in Chinese or English);

(3) The Foreigner Physical Examination Form;

(4) The Certificate of Non-Criminal Record;

(5) Bank Statement (30,000 yuan/RMB for one semester and 50,000 yuan/RMB for one academic year).

(6) If the applicant is an international student in China, a valid Visa or study type residence permit and an official study proof such as the "Certificate of End of Study" or "Transfer Certificate" issued by the aforementioned school/ University must be submitted with the application. If the applicant is employed, an official employment proof must be submitted.

Note：

● The applicant should affirm that all the information and documents provided are true and correct. Any conduct of falsification, fraud will result in cancellation of your admission qualification.

● Please scan the original or notarized application documents in color using a scanner. Images captured by mobile phone or camera are not acceptable.

● All uploaded documents should be in Chinese or in English; otherwise notarial translations in Chinese or English are required. Once translated, the original documents and certified translations are to be submitted together.

● Incomplete application documents will delay the process of your application.  

VI. Qualification Examination

Hainan University will check the application documents and issue the Electronical Letter of Admission, Visa application form for Study in China to the qualified students after the applicants are approved.

VII. Registration

Admitted students should take the Passport, Letter of Admission, Visa application form for Study in China, the Foreigner Physical Examination Form and other required documents to the Chinese Embassy or Consulate in their country to apply for Student (X1 or X2) visa.

VIII. Fees and Payment

1. Application fee: 400 Yuan（Application fee should be paid when you submit your application materials; it is non-refundable.）

2.Tuition fee of Chinese Language and Culture program: 8,050 yuan/semester; 16,100 yuan/academic year.

Hainan University provides preferential tuition fee for the offspring of overseas Hainanese, who only pay 50% of the tuition fee. For exchange students of this program, the fee is based on the agreement between Universities.

IX. Scholarships

For Chinese Language and Culture program, Hainan University provides excellent students with Chinese Government Scholarship（Bilateral Program), International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship, Hainan Provincial Overseas Chinese Teachers Scholarship, etc. Prospective students and scholars from all over the world are welcome to apply for scholarship to study Chinese Language and Culture at Hainan University. With regard to specific information about the Scholarship application, please check the Application Guide for those Scholarship Programs（https://hd.hainanu.edu.cn/gjjyen/Scholarships/Confucius_Institute_Scholarship.htm）.
