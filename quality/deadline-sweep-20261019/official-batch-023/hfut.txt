I Introduction to HFUT
Hefei University of Technology (HFUT), located in Hefei, the capital city of Anhui Province, is a national key university administrated directly by the Ministry of Education of China. Founded in 1945, HFUT has a long history of providing high-quality education and conducting cutting-edge research in various fields. HFUT is one of the universities of Project 211 and Program 985 innovation platform in China, and also one of universities included in the list of “Double First Class” plan, the country’s construction plan of world-class universities and first-class disciplines.
Tw
elve disciplines of the university rank among the top 1% of the global ESI, and its engineering science disciplines have entered the top 0.4‰ of the global ESI ranking.

III Eligibility
1. Applicants
shall
be non-Chinese citizens, physically and mentally healthy;
2.
Applicants shall hold a high school diploma before September 1, 2026;
3. Applicants should not be older than 25;
4
. Applicants’ academic grade in the previous stage should not be less than 75%;
5
.
Applicants shall complete the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (
CSCA
) prior to the application deadline.
Relevant test subjects are specified in
Appendix 1
, and detailed test arrangements may be accessed via the official CSCA website (
www.csca.cn
).
6. Applicants for Chinese-taught Programs should have the Chinese proficiency of HSK 4 or above
;
Applicants for English-taught Programs should have the English proficiency of IELTS 6.0/TOEFL 85/TOEFL Essentials 9/Duolingo 100 or above.
7. Applicants who previously held Chinese citizenship shall have obtained foreign nationality and renounced their Chinese nationality before April 30, 2022. Additionally, they shall have resided abroad for more than 2 years between April 30, 2022 to April 30, 2026. (Record of actual foreign residency for 9 months in a year can be counted as one year, referring to the entry and exit date signatures)

VIII Application Process
Step 1:
Materials Preparation
Note
:
The CSCA test result is one of the essential documents for applying to undergraduate programs at HFUT. It serves as an important quantitative indicator to assess applicants' academic competence. Please refer to
Appendix 1
for specific test subjects. Applicants can visit the official website (www.csca.cn) to check the registration method and test arrangements. To ensure a smooth application process, it is recommended to take the CSCA test and obtain the transcript as early as possible to reserve sufficient time for subsequent application and review.
Step 2:
Apply Online
Applicants log in to HFUT International Student Online Service System (
http://admission.hfut.edu.cn
) to register and
submit
application.
1. Each applicant can only apply for one degree program. Please do not apply repeatedly;
2. The email address used in the application system must be valid and the applicant should ensure that the email address can be used before registration;
3. Applicants
shall
submit all application materials that meet the requirements before the deadline.
Step 3:
Initial Qualification Review
1. The Admissions Office will review the completeness and authenticity of the materials in 5-7 work days.
2. If the applicant receives a notice regarding supplementary materials, please submit the relevant materials before the application deadline.
3. Applicants who pass the initial review must pay a
non-refundable
application fee of 400 RMB.
Step 4:
Comprehensive Interview
After confirming the payment of application fee, applicants will receive an email notification of the comprehensive interview within 7-10 working days. The interview results will be sent via system message or email.
Step 5:
Pre-admission &
F
ee
P
ayment
Applicants who pass all the reviews will receive
HFUT
Pre-admission Notice.
Pre-admitted students are required to confirm their admissions and pay their tuition fees within the prescribed period stated in the pre-admission notice. Those who don’t give feedback as required will be deemed as giv
ing
up the admission.
Step 6:
Issuance of Admission Materials
1.
After the tuition payment is confirmed,
HFUT
will issue the
A
dmission
N
otice and
the
Confirmation Form for Study in China via email.
2
.
For students admitted to CSC Bilateral Program,
the Admission Notice and the Confirmation Form for Study in China will be forwarded to the dispatching departments or the students themselves via the Chinese Embassy or Consulate in their respective countries.
3.Students should bring their passport, Admission Notice, and Confirmation Form for study in China to the Chinese embassy or consulate abroad to apply for an entry study (X1) visa.
Step 7:
Registration
1. New students will start studying at HFUT in early September 2026. Please refer to the Admission Notice for the specific registration date. Those who fail to report on time without approval shall be deemed to have forfeited their admission.
2.
New students shall bring the Admission Notice, the Confirmation Form for study in China, and the original copies of all application materials for registration verification. If the original copies are in languages other than Chinese or English, notarized English translations will need to be submitted. Those who fail the verification will have their admission qualification revoked.
