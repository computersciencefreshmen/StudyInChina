2026/2027 Henan University Admissions Guide for “Chinese Government Scholarship – High-Level Postgraduate Program”
Time:2025年11月14日 21:24       Source:
The Chinese Government Scholarship- High Level Postgraduate Program adopts the principle of "individual application, school recommendation, expert review and merit-based admission" to select international students with excellent academic background, professional ability and future development potential. Henan University will select and recruit outstanding young international students to study for postgraduate degrees, and cultivate high-level international talents.
Ⅰ
、
Scholarship category, duration and admission majors
1. Scholarship Category: full-time postgraduates (master degree students, doctoral degree students);
2
. The language of instruction is generally Chinese, though some programs are offered in English.
Admission majors: Please refer to Annex 5 (English Taught Programs) and Annex 6 (Chinese Taught Programs) for details.
3
. The scholarship covers the study duration determined at the time of admission, including the duration of professional study and Chinese language (preparatory) study, which cannot be extended in principle. The subsidy coverage for Chinese language (preparatory) study is generally 1 year; the subsidy coverage for master students is generally 3 years; the subsidy coverage for doctoral students is generally 4 years.
Ⅱ
、
Scholarship content
1. Provide tuition fees;
2.
Provide free on-campus accommodation;
3. Provide living expenses;
4. Provide comprehensive medical insurance for international students in China.
See Annex 4 for details.
Ⅲ
、
Application channels and application deadlines
Applicants should apply directly to Henan university; the application deadline is
January
16th, 202
6
.
Ⅳ
、
Application conditions
1. Non-Chinese citizens, in good health;
2. Education and age requirements:
Those who come to China to study for a master's degree must have a bachelor's degree and excellent academic performance, and be under the age of 35;
Those who come to China to study for a doctoral degree must have a master's degree or above and be under the age of 40.
In principle, applicants should be overseas students. Outstanding current final-year undergraduate or master's students are also eligible for recommendation.
3. Language proficiency requirements:
Language proficiency requirements for majors taught in Chinese: At least
180 points in the HSK Level 5
.
Applicants who have obtained a degree from a Chinese university with Chinese as the medium of instruction are exempt from providing HSK certificates.
Language proficiency requirements for English-taught majors:
(1) The mother tongue or official language is English;
(2) Applicants whose native language is not English:
IELTS 6.5 or new TOEFL 86 or above
Ⅴ
、
Application process
Step1: apply online
Applicants must submit materials before
January
16th, 202
6
. Applicants must register an account in the Chinese Government Scholarship Management Information System for Studying in China (https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn
，
Type : Category B; Acceptance institution code: 10475) and in Henan University International Student Online Service System (https://henu.17gz.org), fill in the personal information accurately, upload documents that meet the requirements, and click "Submit" to complete the online application. This year's admissions are submitted online. Applicants should ensure that the materials submitted online are consistent with the originals. Complete application materials will be given priority to the next round of review.
See Annex 1 to Annex 3 for the list of submissions and specific requirements.
Attention: Personal information, such as passport name, passport number, nationality, date of birth, etc., must be filled in truthfully, otherwise the application may fail; contact numbers, email address, contacts in China, etc. must be accurate and effective.
Step2: Preliminary review by School of International Education(SIE), Henan University
According to the admission requirements, SIE will review the application qualifications item by item, focusing on reviewing the academic background, language level, identity qualification, health status, etc..
Step3: Academic review by educational department of Henan University
The educational department will organize an expert review, focusing on reviewing the applicant's academic performance, scientific research ability, language level, academic background, comprehensive quality and development potential, and select the best candidates for a remote interview or written test, and report the results to SIE.
Step4:Recommend candidates to the China Scholarship Council
SIE reports the recommendation list to the China Scholarship Council.
Step5: Final review by experts organized by the China Scholarship Council
The China Scholarship Council will organize experts from relevant disciplines to conduct final review on the applicant's academic performance, comprehensive quality, development potential, etc., and select the best candidates for admission.
Scholarship applicants who are not admitted and willing to apply for self-funded projects of the university will be given priority for admission.
Step6: Notify students of admission results
SIE will inform the students of the admission results, transfer or notify the students of the “Admission Notice” and “Visa Application for Study in China” (JW201 Form) and other documents before the end of July
202
6
.
Ⅵ
、
Other Matters
1. Chinese government scholarship students are not allowed to enjoy other scholarships (excluding all kinds of one-time incentives) established by the Chinese government at all levels and the admitted institutions at the same time. Those who deliberately conceal the funding situation will not be allowed to apply for Chinese government scholarships within three years, except for disqualification.
2. Applicants who have applied to other universities in the Chinese Government Scholarship Management Information System to study in China should not apply to Henan University repeatedly.
3.
Once admitted, scholarship recipients are not permitted to change their major, study duration, or language of instruction.
4
. Admitted students must complete the registration and registration procedures in strict accordance with the time specified by the university. Those who fail to register on time as required will not be eligible for scholarships.
5
. Those who fail to obtain the corresponding graduation or completion certificate on time or fail the physical examination will be disqualified from admission.
6
. Scholarship students must participate in the annual review after entering university to confirm whether the scholarship qualification is sustained, suspended or canceled for the next academic year based on the review results.
