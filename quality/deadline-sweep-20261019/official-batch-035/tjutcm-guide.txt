天津中医药大学国际学生招生简章
2025-12-11 15:21
审核人：
天津中医药大学始建于1958年，入选国家“双一流”建设高校名单，中药学科入选国家“双一流”建设学科，入围教育部、国家中医药管理局、天津市政府三方共建高校之列，是原国家教委批准的唯一一所中国传统医药国际学院，是世界中医药学会联合会教育指导委员会和世界针灸学会联合会教学指导委员会主任委员单位。学校秉承“进德修业，继承创新”的校训，以中医药为主体，医学、理学、文学、管理学、工学、教育学多学科协调发展，以“做精医学，做强药学，做实健康相关专业，做大社会服务”为发展战略，努力建设中国特色、世界一流的中医药大学。学校拥有中药学国家“双一流”建设学科，有针灸推拿学和中医内科学2个国家级重点学科，是教育部中医学卓越人才(本博贯通)培养试点单位(全国共6所)和中药学基础学科拔尖学生培养计划2.0基地(全国共2所)。学校拥有附属医院10所，教学与实习医院41所。
学校广泛开展国际交流与合作，注重培养理论知识扎实、临床操作能力强的国际应用型临床人才，在国际上享有较高的声誉，现已发展成为本科教育为主体，涵盖硕士、博士等高层次教育及长短期进修的传统医药国际化人才培养体系。学校与世界50多个国家和地区的高等教育院校、科研院所、医疗机构建立了友好合作关系。组织研究制定全球第一个中医药教育国际标准——《世界中医学本科(CD前)教育标准》，总主编世界中医学专业核心课程教材13部，建有“世界中联‘一带一路’中医药教育师资培训基地(天津)”。学校现有全球首家中医孔子课堂——日本神户东洋医疗学院孔子课堂，泰国首家中医孔子学院，马来西亚南方大学学院中医孔子学院。与英国诺丁汉大学合作举办临床药学本科专业，荣获“英国泰唔士报高等教育奖”最佳国际合作项目提名。
学校设有静海团泊校区、南开玉泉路校区。主校区坐落于天津市静海区团泊湖畔，占地2100余亩，建有天津中药植物园、天津市中医药博物馆、中医药科技园及在国际上具有影响的中医药国际教育中心。
学历生
一、申请入学条件
·非中国籍公民；
·身体健康；
·申报汉语授课的专业课程者，汉语水平须达到HSK4级水平；
·申报英语授课的专业课程者，需英语流利，具备用英语学习的能力；
（一）本科生入学条件：
·具有高中毕业或具有同等以上学历；
·报读中药学专业学生要求具有较好的化学学习基础。
备注：1.外国籍华裔申请本科课程，应持有有效的外国护照或国籍证明文件4年（含）以上，且最近4年（截至入学年度的4月30日前）之内有在外国实际居住2年以上的记录（一年中实际在外国居住满9个月可按一年计算，以入境和出境签章为准）。
2.未满18周岁申请者须办理在津监护人公证手续。
·自2026/2027学年起，申请就读本科学历教育的申请人，请在提交申请前参加来华留学本科入学学业水平测试（China Scholastic Competency Assessment，简称 CSCA），并提交测试成绩。（测试详情见附件）
（二）学术型硕士研究生入学条件：
报考攻读硕士学位研究生须具有与中国学士学位相当的学位。
1.医学类：
（1）基础学科：具有本科学历者。
（2）临床学科（具备以下一项）：
·具备中医学类、中西医临床医学专业本科学历；
·具备临床医学类、基础医学类专业或非医专业本科学历，申请中医学类专业、中西医结合专业需完成规定中医课程学习或具有所在地区中医或针灸师资格；
（3）中药学：具有中药学类、药学类或相关本科学历者。
2. 非医学类：具有相关专业本科学历。
（三）学术型博士研究生入学条件（具备以下一项）：
报考攻读博士学位研究生须具有与中国硕士学位相当的学位。
1.具备中医学、中西医结合学科硕士学历；
2.具备临床医学、基础医学硕士学历，并且完成规定中医课程学习者或具有所在地区中医或针灸师资格者。
3.报考中药学专业者，需具有中药学、药学或相关学科硕士学历。
备注：英文授课针灸推拿学硕士或博士学位申请者，应具备医学或相关专业学士或硕士学位。
二、可颁发证书：博士、硕士、学士学位证书和毕业证书及学位证书英文副本。

7.护理学：护理学

来华留学本科入学学业水平测试介绍
一、测试及执行机构
来华留学本科入学学业水平测试（China Scholastic Competency Assessment，简称CSCA）是在教育部指导下，国家留学基金管理委员会授权北京留金东方国际教育科技有限公司作为测试服务执行机构，全面负责考点建设、考务组织、技术支持等工作。考生可登录CSCA官方网站（www.csca.cn）了解更多信息。

（一）理科中文
申请全英文授课本科学历教育的申请人无需参加专业中文测试；申请攻读汉语言本科专业的申请人如能提供有效期内HSK四级成绩报告，可免考专业中文。
（二）数学
数学是考察申请人基础学业能力和思维逻辑能力的重要依据，无论申请何种专业，数学均为必考科目。
三、测试形式
考试初期以居家网考为主，逐步在重点生源国家和地区设立线下考点。具体考点信息以官方网站(www.csca.cn)实际发布为准。
四、测试报名
1.报名时间
拟定于2025年11月初至下旬开放报名，具体时间安排及操作流程将通过CSCA官方网站（www.csca.cn）另行发布。
2.报名方式
（1）线上报名（主要方式）：通过CSCA官方网站（www.csca.cn）完成在线报名，平台支持中、英文双语操作。
（2）线下辅助（特殊渠道）：部分海外合作考点为网络或技术条件受限地区的考生提供线下报名协助服务，具体考点名单及服务细则另行公布。
五、测试时间
首次测试定于2025年12月21日进行，将于2025年11月开放报名，具体时间将通过官方网站(www.csca.cn)发布。
测试具体时间安排如下（北京时间）：
理科中文：13:00-14:30
数学：15:30-16:30
自2026年起将常态化提供测试服务，每年5次，分别于1月、3月、4月、6月和12月进行。具体测试时间及安排将通过官方网站(www.csca.cn)发布。
六、测试费用
报考单科测试费用为450元人民币，报考两科及以上为700元人民币。报名支付系统支持境内外主流支付方式，包括支付宝、微信、银联及VISA、MasterCard等。
七、成绩发布
成绩发布时间将根据考试形式有所不同，居家网考和线下集中机考将在考后7个工作日内公布成绩，纸笔考试则将在考后14个工作日内公布。
Tianjin University of Traditional Chinese Medicine
Admissions for International Students
Established in 1958,Tianjin University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TUTCM)has been selected into the list of national“Double First-Class”constructionuniversities, with the subject of ChineseMateria Medicabeing included in the list of national“Double First-Class”constructionprogram. It is also among the universities jointly constructed by the Ministry of Education(MOE), the State Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine, and TianjinMunicipalPeople’s Government.It is the only former State Education Commission (Present MOE) approved International University of Traditional Chinese Medicine and also the hosting institution of Educational Instruction CommitteeofWorld Federation of Chinese Medicine Societies (EIC-WFCMS)and Teaching Guidance Committee of the World Federation of Acupuncture-Moxibustion Societies.TUTCMhas always adheredtothe mottoof“Strengthening moral education and pursuing academic studies,inheriting and innovating”, focused on Traditional Chinese Medicine and has six categories including Medicine, Science, Art, Management, Engineering, and Education, with the development strategy of“refining medical disciplines, strengthening pharmaceutical disciplines, consolidating health-related majors, and expanding social services”,andstroveto build a world-class university of traditional medicine with Chinese characteristics. It has a national“Double First-Class”construction program of Chinese Materia Medica,and two national key programs ofTCM Internal Medicine and Acupuncture & Moxibustion and Tuina.It is one of the six pilot units for the cultivation of outstanding talents in TCM(straight to Ph.D)by MOE and one of the two Top Students Training Centers 2.0 for Basic Programs in Chinese Materia Meidica in the country.The university has 10 affiliated hospitals and 41teaching hospitals.
The University has extensive international exchanges and cooperation. It aims to cultivate international applied talents in clinic with solid theoretical foundation and excellent clinical skills which make it enjoy a high reputation worldwide. It has formed TCM internationalization talents training system where the bachelor education is the main part.The system also covers master and doctoral education and short and long term certificate studies.The university has established friendly cooperative relations with institutions of higher education, scientific research institutes and medical institutions in more than 50 countries and regions around the world.It has formulated the first standard for TCM international educationWorld Standard of Chinese Medicine Undergraduate (Pre-CMD) Education, servedas the chief editor of 13 textbooksof World Textbook Series forChinese medicineCore Curriculum, and established the“WFCMS‘Belt and Road’Chinese Medicine Education Teacher Training Base (Tianjin)”.The university now hosts the world’s first Confucius Classroom for Traditional Chinese Medicine–Confucius Classroom at Kobe Toyo Medical School, the first Confucius Institute for Traditional Chinese Medicine in Thailand, as well as Confucius Institute of Traditional Chinese Medicine at Southern University Collegein Malaysia.It has jointly offered the undergraduate program in clinical pharmacy with the University of Nottingham in the UK, and been nominated for the“Times Higher Education Award”for the Best International Collaboration Project.
TUTCMhas two campuses:the Tuanbo Campus in Jinghai District and the Yuquan Road Campus in Nankai District, with its main campus in the picturesque area around Tuanbo Lake in Jinghai District,Tianjin, takingan area of 1.4km2.It has established the Tianjin Botanical Garden of Chinese Medicinal Herbs, the Tianjin Museum of Traditional Chinese Medicine, the Science and Technology Park of Traditional Chinese Medicine, as well as an internationally influential International Education Center for Traditional Chinese Medicine.

Appendix
China Scholastic Competency Assessment
I.Test and Implementation Organization
The China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) for international undergraduate applicants to study in China is guided by the Ministry of Education of the People’s Republic of China. The China Scholarship Council has authorized Beijing Liujin Oriental International Education & Technology Co., Ltd. as the official test service implementation organization, which is fully responsible for test center construction, examination affairs organization, technical support, and other related work. Candidates can visit the official CSCA website at www.csca.cn for more information.

(ii) Mathematics
Mathematics serves as a crucial criterion for assessing applicants’basic academic competence and logical thinking ability. Regardless of the program they apply for, Mathematics is a compulsory subject.
III.Test Format
In the initial stage of the test, online proctored exams taken at home will be the primary delivery method. Offline test centers will be gradually established in key source countries and regions. For specific test center information, please refer to the official announcements released on the official website (www.csca.cn).
IV. Test Registration
1.Registration Period
Registration is tentatively scheduled to open from early to late November 2025. The specific schedule and operation procedures will be separately announced via the official CSCA website (www.csca.cn).
2. Registration Method
(1) Online Registration (Primary Method):Complete the online registration via the official CSCA website (www.csca.cn). The platform supports both Chinese and English operation interfaces.
(2) Offline Assistance (Special Channel):Some overseas cooperative test centers provide offline registration assistance services for candidates in regions with limited internet or technical access. The specific list of test centers and service details will be announced separately.
V. Test Time
The first test is scheduled to be held on December 21, 2025, with registration opening in November 2025. The specific schedule will be published via the official website (www.csca.cn).
The detailed test schedule (Beijing Time) is as follows:
Chinese(forScienceSubject): 13:00–14:30
Mathematics: 15:30–16:30
Starting from 2026, regular test services will be provided five times a year, held in January, March, April, June and December respectively. The specific test time and arrangements will be released on the official website (www.csca.cn).
VI. Test Fees
The fee for a single subject test is 450 RMB. For two or more subjects, the total fee is 700 RMB. The online registration and payment system supports major payment methods both in China and overseas, including Alipay, WeChat Pay, China UnionPay, VISA, and MasterCard.
VII. Score Release
The score release time varies depending on the test format. For online proctored home exams and offline computer-based centralized exams, scores will be published within 7 working days after the test. For paper-and-pencil exams, scores will be released within 14 working days after the test.
