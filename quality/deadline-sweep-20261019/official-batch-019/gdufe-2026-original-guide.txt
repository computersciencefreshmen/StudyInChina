广东财经大学 2026 年国际学生
本科生招生简章
一、学校简介
广东财经大学始建于 1983 年，为博士学位授予单位。学校
现有广州和佛山两个校区，位于粤港澳大湾区核心城市—广州市
和佛山市，占地面积 2,275 亩。广州校区地处琶洲国家级人工智
能与数字经济试验区核心区，毗邻广州国际会展中心；佛山校区
位于风景秀丽的佛山市云东海旅游经济区。学校坚持“商法融合、
实践创业、多元协同”办学特色，经过 42 年发展，已经建设成
为一所学科特色鲜明、师资力量雄厚、办学效益显著、社会影响
力较高的综合性财经大学。
学校以经济学、管理学、法学为主体，文学、理学、工学、
艺术学、教育学等多学科协同发展，62 个本科专业，其中包括
18 个国家一流本科专业建设点、16 个省级一流本科专业建设点；
有 2 个博士学位授权一级学科点、2 个博士学位授权二级学科点、
11 个硕士学位授权一级学科点、1 个硕士学位授权二级学科点、
23 个硕士专业学位授权点。现有全日制本科生、研究生 2.9 万人

I. About GDUFE
Guangdong University of Finance and Economics (GDUFE) was founded in 1983 and
it was authorized to confer doctor’s degree since 2021. It consists of two campuses with a
total land area of about 150 hectares, one in Guangzhou city and one in Foshan city, both of
which are core cities of Guangdong-Hongkong-Macao Greater Bay Area. Guangzhou
Campus is located in the core area of Pazhou National AI and Digital Economy
Demonstration Center, with walking distance to Guangzhou International Convention and
Exhibition Center where Canton Fair is annually held, and Foshan campus is located within
the scenic Yundonghai Tourism Economic District in Foshan City. It has adhered to the
distinct characteristics of "integration of business and law, practical entrepreneurship, and
diverse collaboration". After 42 years of development, GDUFE has become a
comprehensive university of finance and economics with distinct disciplinary
characteristics, strong teaching faculty, significant educational achievement, and high social
influence and reputation.
GDUFE has developed a multi-disciplinary education system in economics,
management, law, humanities, science, engineering, art and education. It offers 62 bachelor
programs, including 18 national first-level programs and 16 provincial first-level programs.
And it offers 2 first-level disciplines conferring doctoral degrees, 2 second-level disciplines
conferring doctoral degrees, 11 first-level disciplines conferring master degrees, 1
second-level discipline conferring master degrees, and 23 professional master programs.
The total enrollment of full-time undergraduate and postgraduate students is about 29,000.
It has established partnership with more than 90 universities and scientific research
institutions from over 30 countries and regions in the world.

IX. Application Procedures
1. Apply and submit materials. By June 1, applicants should apply and submit
documents via our application website: gdufe.at0086.cn/student.

nationality, highest degree, recommendation letters, academic transcripts, language
certificates, and other supporting materials and finalize candidates for interview.
3. Interview. Eligible applicants attend an online interview in which their
comprehensive abilities, language proficiency, etc. will be assessed. Details regarding time,
way of interview will be notified through emails by GDUFE.

XII. Information for Registration
1. International students shall finish registration according to the notice specified in

18
the Admission Letter. Early registration is not accepted. Those who fail to register within

of GDUFE foreign affairs staff, do a physical examination at Guangzhou Health Care

diseases that are likely to cause serious harm to public health, as stipulated in Exit and
Entry Administration Law of the People’s Republic of China, shall be handled by the public
security authorities in accordance with relevant Chinese laws.
5. International students are required to buy insurance at their own expense. Those
who fail to obtain insurance in accordance with related regulations will not be admitted.

withdraw or denied registration.

Chinese ethics and customs, observe the rules and regulations of GDUFE, and complete
study within standard 4 academic years, or 7 years at most.
7. International students enjoy the same facilities and medical services as the Chinese
student, but does not enjoy any scholarship or grant provided for Chinese students only.
8. International students are encouraged to take part in the cultural and sport activities
which are beneficial to their mental and physical health. They can also volunteer to
