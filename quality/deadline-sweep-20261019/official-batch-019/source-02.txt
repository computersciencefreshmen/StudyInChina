2026 Admission Brochure of Fuzhou University for International Students
作者：  发布时间：2026-03-25  阅读次数:
(Bachelor of Chinese Language)
Fuzhou University is a nationally recognized “Double First-Rate” university, a key university under the “Project 211”, and a university jointly built by the People’s Government of Fujian Province and the Ministry of Education of the PRC. Founded in 1958, the university has grown into a primarily engineering-focused university with a combination of science and engineering and a key university emphasizing the coordinated development of multiple disciplines including science, engineering, medicine,
economics, management, humanities, law, and arts.
The university has 27 colleges and one affiliated provincial hospital, with 13 disciplines in the top 1% of ESI global rankings; Chemistry, Engineering, and Materials Science are in the top 1‰. There are currently over 40,000 undergraduate students and more than 19,000 master's and doctoral students. The university actively engages in international cooperation and exchange, having established partnerships with over 140 universities, research institutions, and renowned enterprises in more than 50 countries and regions, and co-established the Joint Institute for Future Technologies with Universit
i
Tunku Abdul Rahman (Malaysia)
.
It recruits international students from over 30 countries and is an institution qualified for the Chinese Government Scholarship and the Fujian Provincial Government Scholarship for international students.

VII
. Study-Related Expenses
(
I
) No enrollment fee will be charged, but applicants will need to cover the textbook costs.
(II)
Tuition: The tuition will be charged for each academic year in RMB: 14000 yuan/academic year. Tuition for the duration of study for scholarship recipients will be covered by the university. Self-funded students will need to pay their tuition.
(III) Other
E
xpenses
1. Accommodation
F
ee:
The university arranges on-campus dormitories for international students, primarily double rooms at RMB 3,500 per year
at the beginning of the academic year. Accommodation fees for the standard duration of study for scholarship recipients are covered by the university. Self-funded students pay their own accommodation fees.
2. Living
A
llowance
for Scholarship Recipients:
Scholarship recipients receive a monthly living allowance according to their scholarship type. Self-funded students cover all living expenses.
Considering the consumption level in Fuzhou, the monthly basic cost is about
RMB
1000.
I
t is recommended that all new students exchange the equivalent of RMB 3,000 before arrival to cover initial living expenses in China.
3. Insurance
Fee:
To ensure the personal safety of international students in China, according to the regulations of the Ministry of Education, all international students must purchase the Comprehensive Group Insurance for International Students in China. The insurance fee for scholarship recipients is covered by the university. Self-funded students pay their own insurance fee. Rate: RMB 800 per person per year.
4. Residence
P
ermit
F
ee:
International students must undergo a medical examination according to Chinese entry and exit regulations and apply for a residence permit to legally reside in China. The costs for the medical examination and the residence permit are approximately RMB 2000, which shall be borne by students themselves (including scholarship recipients).
VIII. Scholarship Application and Evaluation
Applicants may simultaneously apply for the Fujian Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students and the Fuzhou University Scholarship for International Students. No additional materials are needed.
The
university will select scholarship candidates from the admitted students
based on merit
and submit the list to the university and the relevant scholarship authorities for approval.
These two scholarships offer identical benefits: tuition, accommodation fee, and insurance premium are covered by the scholarship funds. Students also receive a living allowance
.
The funding duration is one year. Scholarship recipients are required to participate in an annual review. Those who pass the review may continue to receive the scholarship for the next academic year. Those who fail will have their scholarship suspended or canceled.
Those who
are
not awarded the
s
cholarship
can
study at the university by
self-funded. Upon securing admission
,
they must submit additional financial documentation
,
including proof of sufficient funds and a financial guarantor in China, to demonstrate their ability to cover all reasonable expenses related to tuition, accommodation, daily living, and international travel throughout their study period in China.
The financial guarantee for studying in China refers to the scholarship amount
provided to international
students
by
Fujian Provincial Government (equivalent to
RMB
30000)
. A
scanned copy of the fixed deposit certificate
by
a
bank shall prevail. The
financial
guarantor in China
must be
limited to the applicant’s parents or relatives, who are required to provide a corresponding passport or a scanned copy of their identi
fication
certificate.
IX
. Enrollment and Registration
Officially admitted applicants shall enter China with a student visa (X VISA). For the registration date, the time specified in the admission letter shall prevail.
When registering for enrollment, students must submit the original copies of their high school diploma, transcripts, and
physical
examination report for qualification review. For those who cannot provide the original certificates for verification, their admission qualifications will be canceled. If any fraudulent application materials are discovered, the admission qualification will be canceled immediately.
If a student is found to be pregnant or has a critical illness that prevents normal study in the medical examination by the Chinese inspection and quarantine department, the student may apply to leave China and return home for recuperation. If there are special reasons for not being able to register for enrollment on time, students must apply for leave in writing to the university in advance; otherwise the admission qualification would be canceled.
X.

The university will, in accordance with its operational conditions, organize international Chinese preparatory courses and training programs at appropriate times. Specific details will be announced separately.
