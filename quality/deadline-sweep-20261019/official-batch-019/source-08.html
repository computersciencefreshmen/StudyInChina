II. Language: II. Language: Chinese 

III. Enrollment quota: 30

IV. Application requirements

Nationality

Non-Chinese citizen with the foreign passport

Age

Age of 18 to 30

Education

Senior high school diploma or with equivalent qualification

Language

Chinese proficiency must reach HSK Level 4 or above or the equivalent

Others

Healthy and physically fit for related professional study;

Law abiding&No criminal record;

Enough financial capacity to pay full tuition, accommodation, etc.

accept Chinese as the teaching and examination language

V. Application Deadline: 10th July

VI. Tuition and Fees

1. Registration fee: 500 RMB/ person, non-refundable

2. Tuition fee: 20,000 RMB for one academic year; Pay before or on arrival

3. Accommodation fee: 6000 RMB//double room/one academic year；Pay before or on arrival

Other expenses: Fees for books, physical examination, medical treatment, comprehensive insurance, residence permit, water and electricity, network, etc. shall be paid on time as requested.

VII. Scholarship

Guangdong Medical University provides scholarships to support outstanding students to study at GDMU. Please refer to Appendix for more details.
