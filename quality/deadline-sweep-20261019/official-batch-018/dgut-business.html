025400 国际商务专业介绍（仅面向2026年国际硕士） Introduction to International Business

发布时间：2026-04-30 点击数：

东莞理工学院国际商务专业硕士（专业代码：025400）为全日制专业学位硕士，立足全球经济一体化发展趋势，着力培养应用型、复合型、创新型高层次国际化商务人才。

本专业紧扣国际商务领域的行业需求与发展痛点，以培育兼具全球视野的人才为核心目标，要求学生熟练掌握国际商务理论、跨国经营技能与现代信息技术，通晓国际商务规则与跨文化沟通技巧，具备国际商务分析决策、风险管控和实务运作能力，可胜任涉外企事业单位、政府部门、社会组织的国际商务经营与管理工作。

专业开设国际物流与供应链管理、国际贸易与国际投资管理、数字经济与全球贸易一体化三大特色培养方向，精准贴合行业前沿。采用全日制两年学制，课程体系涵盖国际金融、国际商法、数字贸易、跨境电商等核心内容，同步融入人工智能、大模型应用等数字技术课程。

学生完成全部培养要求、达到学位成果标准后，可获得毕业证书与硕士学位，成为适配新时代开放型经济发展的国际商务专业人才。

Master of International Business (Major Code: 025400) at Dongguan University of Technology is a full-time professional master’s program. Based on the trend of global economic integration, it dedicates to cultivating application-oriented, interdisciplinary and innovative high-level international business professionals.

This major closely aligns with the industry needs and development challenges in the field of international business. Its core objective is to nurture talents with both a global perspective. Students are required to proficiently master international business theories, cross-border operation skills and modern information technology, be well-versed in international business rules and cross-cultural communication techniques, and possess capabilities in international business analysis, decision-making, risk management and practical operation. Graduates of this specialty are qualified for international business operation and management positions in foreign-related enterprises, public institutions, government departments and social organizations.

The program offers three specialized tracks: International Logistics and Supply Chain Management, International Trade and International Investment Management, and Digital Economy and Global Trade Integration, keeping pace with frontier industry trends. Adopting a full-time two-year academic schedule, the curriculum system covers core courses including international finance, international commercial law, digital trade and cross-border e-commerce, as well as digital technology courses such as artificial intelligence and large model applications.

Upon completing and satisfying all requirements and meeting the degree standards, students will be awarded a graduation certificate and a master’s degree, becoming professional international business talents for the new era of open economy.
