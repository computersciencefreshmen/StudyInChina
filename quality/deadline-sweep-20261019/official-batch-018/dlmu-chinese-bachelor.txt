ADMISSION
Prospective students should carefully review all the information in the admission section of our website in order to thoroughly understand our application process.To successfully apply, the student should submit the online application at https://dlmu.17gz.org
and pay the application fee before the application deadline. The formal notification of your admission or denial will be announced via online application system. You will be required to send us all the application documents and pay deposit to confirm your place at DMU if your application is accepted. Once you have confirmed your place on the program, we will issue an official enrollment letter and additional important documentation, including Visa Application for Study in China.

Application Qualification
◆foreign citizen with a valid foreign passport
--for the applicants whose parents are both or one of whom is a Chinese citizen and settled down abroad, and the applicants were born with a foreign nationality, who does not have Chinese nationality, as well as the residents of the mainland of China, Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan who have immigrated and obtained foreign nationality, their applications to enter DMU at the undergraduate level as international students, in addition to suiting the other admission requirements of DMU, should also provide a valid foreign passport or proof of foreign nationality for 4 years (including) or more, and have a record of actual residence abroad for 2 years or more within the past 4 years (a year of actual residence abroad of 9 months or more can be counted as one year, based on the entry and exit seal on the passport)
◆in good health, both mentally and physically, aged between 18 and 35
◆graduated from an institution equal to Chinese senior high school and eligible for admission
Application Materials
◆ Application Form for International Students to Study at Dalian Maritime University:the form shall be filled in the form and submitted online
◆ Photocopy of the personal information page and blank page of passport: If the applicant has been to China before, the visa page with entry stamp and exit stamp should be provided continuously
◆Notarization of high school diploma
◆Transcript during the whole high school
◆Language Proficiency Certificate:HSK Certificate (4th level or above)
Applicants without an HSK certificate (4th level or above) should attend the Mandarin level test organized by DMU. Those who get a result equivalent to HSK 4th level or above and signed the “Agreement Probation” shall be considered.
◆Valid Physical Exam Record issued within 6 months: if the one submitted online is not satisfied with the requirement, it may be required to re-check in the designated agency after entry and the related fees should be covered by yourself
◆Blood Test Report:The Blood Test Report should include results of HIV, V.D.R.L, HbsAg and HCV
◆Valid Non-Criminal Record Certificate: the valid period is 6 months
◆Transcript of China Scholastic Competency Assessment(CSCA)
◆Copy of Photograph: the size should be 3cm*4cm, with white background and not bigger than 2M)
◆Financial Certification（The deposit certificate issued by the bank in the applicant's name of not less than RMB 50,000 or equivalent in foreign currency,for self-funded applicants only)
Note:
1. The application deadline for applying for a degree program is each June 30th basically
2. Documents issued other than in Chinese or English must be attached with notarized translations in Chineseor English
3. Applicants who are still studying are also required to provide a study certificate and transcript issued by their institutions
Application Procedures
◆ Submit the online application through the website of https://dlmu.17gz.org
◆ The application online shall be reviewed by the admission staff after submitted
◆ The formal notification of your admission will be announced via online application system if your application is accepted. You will be required to pay application fees and tuition fees to confirm your admissions
◆ Once the related fees received, your admission materials, including the Admission Letter and JW202Form shall be issued
