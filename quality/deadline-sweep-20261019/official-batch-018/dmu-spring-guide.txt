Admission Guide for DMU 2026 (Spring) International Student Programs
Date：2025/11/03Clicks：
I. Programs
1.Pre-medical Program
2. Undergraduate Program: Clinical Medicine (MBBS)
II. Application Time
The system will be open on November 5, 2025 at 09:00 Beijing time.
III. Application Procedure
Fill in the online application form (http://dmu.17gz.org), and upload required documents.
Note:
1.High school diploma and transcripts must be certified by the Chinese Embassy (Consulate) in your country or notarized by a notary office in China. The original will be submitted without being returned.
2. Family financial proof certificate (or one year bank statement), medical check-up, passport size E-photo and other documents can be uploaded together. More completed documents will facilitate the evaluation process.
Admission will be determined according to the applicant's materials. The eligible candidate will get an Acceptance Letter (LOA) through the online application system. The candidate has to transfer the fee to DMU account first according to LOA to get the seat confirmed. Admission letter and JW202 form will be issued after receiving the fee.
