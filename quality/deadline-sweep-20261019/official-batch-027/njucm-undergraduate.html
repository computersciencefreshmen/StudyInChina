2026 NJUCM Guidebook for International Undergraduate Program Applicants
2025-11-24

I. APPLICATION

(1) Eligibility

a. Applicants must be foreign citizens holding a foreign passport, with excellent academic records, good conduct, no criminal record, and good health.

b. The applicant must possess education background equivalent to or above Chinese high school education.

c. Applicants' Chinese language proficiency must meet the requirement of a score of 180 or above in the New HSK Level 4.

d. For applicants who were born with foreign nationality and had both or one parent as Chinese citizens at the time of their birth, or who were originally residents of Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan and later immigrated abroad and acquired foreign nationality, they must meet the requirements of the Chinese Ministry of Education Document No. 12 【2020】 and provide the relevant supporting documents in accordance with the university's admission requirements.

e. The applicant shall take the "China Scholastic Competency Assessment" (hereinafter referred to as the "CSCA Test"), organized by the China Scholarship Council of the Ministry of Education of China, and submit a valid score report. For detailed information and registration methods, please visit the official website (www.csca.cn).  Specific test subjects are detailed in the attachment.

(2) Application Materials

a. "NJUCM Application Form for International Students".

b. Scanned copy of passport (passport must be valid within 6 months from the date of submitting the application).

c. Scanned copy of the highest academic qualification and/or degree certificate: please submit scanned copy of the graduation certificate and (or) degree certificate; If the applicant is an in-school student, he/she must submit a scanned copy of enrollment certificate issued by the school.

d. Copy of the HSK Level Certificate.

e. Digital Photo: 2-inch color photo.

f. Financial proof.

g. Non-criminal Record Certificate.

h. Certificate of health check.

i. CSCA Test Score Report

(3) Learning method

Full-time study.

(4) Application Period

December 1, 2025 to May 15, 2026.

Note: Due to limited scholarship quotas, applicants for scholarships are advised to submit their applications by May 10, 2026. For details, please refer to the “Scholarship” section.

II. Assessment Method

Application Assessment Evaluation.

III. Application and Admission Procedure

Step 1: Take the "China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA)"

All students applying for undergraduate programs are required to take the CSCA Test, organized by the China Scholarship Council of the Ministry of Education of China, and submit a valid score report. For detailed information and registration methods, please visit the official website (www.csca.cn).

Important Note: To ensure a smooth application process, applicants are strongly advised to take the CSCA test early and submit their scores promptly, allowing sufficient time for subsequent review procedures. Specific test subjects are detailed in the 2026 NJUCM Undergraduate Programs for International Students. The CSCA test score serves as a key quantitative basis for the university’s evaluation of an applicant’s academic competency. Diligent preparation is essential.

Step 2: Complete and Submit the Online Application

Applicants must log in to the Nanjing University of Chinese Medicine International Student Management System (http://apply.njucm.cucas.cn/) within the specified application period, register, fill in the application information, and upload all required documents. 

Step 3: Participate in an Online Interview

Eligible applicants who pass the initial document review will be shortlisted for an online interview. The interview will focus on evaluating the applicant’s comprehensive qualities and Chinese language proficiency. Specific interview arrangements and schedules will be notified via the applicant’s registered email.

The Admission Notice and other relevant documents will be emailed by the university in mid-to-late June 2026.

IV. Visa Application and Enrollment Registration

The specific enrollment schedule and other related arrangements shall be subject to the announcements made by International Education College.

Visa Application: Admitted students must present their original passport, the Admission Notice, and the Confirmation Form for Study in China (JW202) at the Chinese embassy or consulate in their home country to apply for a Student (X1) Visa. They are required to register at Nanjing University of Chinese Medicine on the date specified in the Admission Notice, bringing all the aforementioned documents.

Students who submitted a Certificate of Enrollment during the application process must supplement their application by submitting the original or notarized copy of their academic qualification certificate upon enrollment. Failure to provide the required document will result in the revocation of admission eligibility.

If an international student fails to register as stipulated without having obtained prior leave, their admission eligibility will be revoked. Additionally, the original Foreigner Physical Examination Form must be submitted for verification during registration.

V. Degree

Students who have completed the course, passed the thesis defense and passed relevant deliberation, and comply with the provisions of the “Regulations on Academic Degrees of the People’s Republic of China”, can obtain the corresponding degree certificate and graduation certificate.

VII. Scholarships

a. Chinese Government Scholarship

The scholarship covers tuition fees, accommodation, living stipend, and comprehensive medical insurance. Students applying for the Chinese Government Scholarship — Type A (Bilateral Program) through Chinese embassies abroad who list our university as their first choice may contact the Admissions Office of International Education College to obtain a Pre-admission Letter.

Students recommended directly by our university to apply for the Chinese Government Scholarship — Type B (Silk Road Program) must log in to the "Chinese Government Scholarship Information System for International Students" (https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn) to apply for the "Silk Road" Program (Type B). The institutional code of our university is "10315". The final admission result for the Chinese Government Scholarship is subject to the announcement by the China Scholarship Council.

For details, please visit: https://www.campuschina.org/

b. Study in Jiangsu Provincial Government Scholarship

The scholarship amount is RMB 18,000 yuan per person.

For details, please visit: http://iec.njucm.edu.cn/detail/1015.html

c. Nanjing Municipal Government Scholarship for International Students

The award standard for undergraduate students is RMB 10,000 yuan per person.

For details, please visit: http://iec.njucm.edu.cn/detail/71.html

d. NJUCM Outstanding International Student Scholarship

The award standards for undergraduate students are:

First Class: RMB 3,000 yuan Second Class: RMB 2,000 yuan Third Class: RMB 1,000 yuan per academic year

For details, please visit: http://iec.njucm.edu.cn/inside/5/38.html
