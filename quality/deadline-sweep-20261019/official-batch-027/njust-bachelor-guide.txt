NJUST Rules on Residence and Visa for 
International Students 
第一条 凡来我校学习 6 个月（含 6 个月）以上者，来
华前应申请 X1 签证。 持其它类型签证将无法办理居留许可。 
Article 1 Students whose study duration is six month and 
above should apply for X1 type visa before entering China. 
Students holding visas with other types are unable to get 
residence permits. 
第二条 留学生入境后入住或变更住宿地点均须在入住
后 24 小时内前往住地公安派出所申报住宿登记，填写临时
住宿登记表。 
Article 2 Students shall go to the local police station and 
fill in Registration Form of Temporary Residence within 24 
hours after accommodating in a new place. 
第三条 持 X1 签证的留学生自入境起 30 天内必须申请
办理居留许可。 
Article 3 Students holding X1 visas shall apply for 
residence permits within 30 days upon arriving in China. 
第四条 中国政府奖学金生的居留许可按照学习期限办
理；江苏省政府茉莉花奖学金生、南京市政府—南京理工大
学合作奖学金生的居留许可按照学年每年办理；自费留学生
根据交纳学费和住宿费的期限办理居留许可，其有效期不得
超过在我校注册的学习时间；委托培养留学生的居留许可按
照委托培养协议之约定期限办理。 
Article 4 The duration of residence permit of Chinese 
Government Scholarship students corresponds to their study 
duration in the university ； Jasmine Jiangsu Government 
Scholarship and Nanj ing Municipal Government -NJUST Joint 
Scholarship students should apply for residence permits yearly;

Article 5 Students should hold their passports and student

formalities including temporary lodging registration, residence 
permit extension or alteration, residence pe rmit application, etc. 
Students shall pay formality fees to the Exit & Entry 
Administration of Nanjing Municipal Public Security Bureau in 
accordance with relevant regulations.

第八条 留学生的护照号码、住址、就读院校、偕行人
等情况发生变化的，须在 10 日内向南京市公安局出入境管
理支队申请变更。 
Article 8 In case of any change of pas sport number ，
residential address, place of study, accompanying family 
members or other registered items in residence permit, students 
shall，within 10 days from the date of change, apply to the Exit 
& Entry Administration of Nanjing Municipal Public Securit y 
Bureau for alteration. 
第九条 留学生的居留许可事由为 “学习”，留学生不得
在华任职或就业。如有任职或就业行为，公安机关将按有关
规定予以处罚。 
Article 9 The reason for students stay is study. Students 
holding study residence permit are NOT allowed to work in 
China. Otherwise they will receive penalties from the p ublic 
security bureau in accordance with relevant regulations. 
第十条 留学生需要在校外勤工助学或者实习的，应当

Article 10 Students intend to engage in off -campus

第十一条  留学生在居留许可到期前必须离境，继续学
习者在到期前应办理延期手续。居留许可延期或变更，留学
生需至少提前 10 个工作日向国际教育学院提出申请，以便
开具相关证明材料。留学生应当密切注意护照、签证、居留
许可的有效期，及时办理延期手续。根据中国法律，外国人
非法居留的，给予警告；情节严重的，处每非法居留一日五
百元，总额不超过一万元的罚款或者五日以上十五日以下拘
留。 
Article 11 Students must leave China before their residence 
permits expire and students who continue to study in China shall 
go through  the extension formalities before the expired date. 
Students shall, at least 10 working days before the expiry date, 
apply to the SIE for the residence permit extension or alteration 
in order to get relevant supporting documents issued by the SIE. 
Students shall pay close attention to the expired date of their 
passports, visas and residence permits and go through the 
extension procedures in time. According to relevant laws and 
regulations, foreigners who reside in China illegally shall be 
given a warning; i f circumstances are serious, they shall be 
imposed with a fine of 500 Yuan per day, with a cap of 10,000 
Yuan in total, or be detained for not less than five days but not

第十二条  我校留学生家属可以向我驻外使（领）馆申
请 S1 签证来华陪读。南京市公安局出入境管理支队凭学校
公函、经我驻外使馆认证的亲属关系证明为来华留学生陪读

学生居留许可的有效期限。 
Article 12 Family members of students may apply to the 
Chinese Embassy/ Consulate in foreign countries for S1 visa to 
visit students. The Exit & Entry Administration of Nanjing 
Municipal Public Security Bure au may transact the extension of 
S1 visa for family members when presented the family 
relationship certificate verified by the Chinese Embassy and the 
official letter from NJUST. The duration of stay for family 
members should not exceed the expiry date of the students 
residence permits.  
第十三条  留学生在学习期间因病、因事回国或去港澳
地区及其它国家旅行，须到国际教育学院登记，经学校同意
后方能办理出境手续。 
Article 13 If students want to go home due to illness or 
other important business, or leave mainland China and travel to 
regions like Hong Kong and Macao or other  countries within 
their study period, they shall report to the SIE for approval first 
and then transact exit formalities.   
第十四条  留学生转学至中国其它学校，须到国际教育
学院办理相关手续，由学校出具转学证明。到达迁入地后须
在 10 天内（且在居留许可有效期内）到迁入地公安局出入
境管理处办理居留许可变更手续。 
Article 14 Students who transfer to othe r Chinese 
universities shall get the transfer approval issued by the SIE. 
Then students shall go through the formalities of residence 
permit change at the Exit & Entry Administration of the local 
Public Security Bureau within 10 days (within the validity o f 
residence permit) since the date of arrival at new universities.

第十五条 护照、签证、居留许可应妥善保管，不得涂
改、损坏。居留许可如有遗失，必须持学校的证明到南京市
公安局出入境管理支队申请补领。护照如有遗失，应及时向
公安局报失，由南京市公安局出入境管理支队出具证明，再
向本国驻华使馆申请补发。补发新护照后，要及时到南京市
公安局出入境管理支队申请新的居留许可。 
Article 15 Passport, visa and residence p ermit shall be 
properly kept and shall not be smeared or damaged. In the event 
that students lose the residence permit, they must apply for new 
residence permit with the certificate from the SIE to the Exit & 
Entry Administration of Nanjing Municipal Publi c Security 
Bureau. In the event that Students lose the passport, they shall 
go through the lost declaration timely to the public security 
bureau. With the certificate issued by the Exit & Entry 
Administration of Nanjing Municipal Public Security Bureau, 
they can apply to their embassies in China for a new passport. 
They shall apply for a new residence permit at the Exit & Entry 
Administration of Nanjing Municipal Public Security Bureau 
once they get the new passport. 
第十六条  凡涉及到留学生签证和居留管理的具体事宜，
根据《中华人民共和国出境入境管理法》具体条款执行。 
Article 16 Details regarding the management of visa and 
residence permit of students, please refer to the relevant 
regulations of Exit and Entry Administration Law of the 
People’s Republic of China.
