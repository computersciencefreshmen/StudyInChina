Scholarship System & Tuition Fee Standard
Date:2022-02-23Author:click:
NUFE SCHOLARSHIP SYSTEM
NUFE offers a scholarship system for international students studying on campus for over a year. This scholarship system is performance-based. Therefore, most newly admitted or non-NUFE students are not eligible to participate in the scholarship competition during their first year because they do not have any performance record at NUFE. The only exception to this policy applies to new Ph.D. students, as outstanding admitted doctoral students will receive the Academic Scholarship of NUFE or the Nanjing Government Scholarship upon admission.
NUFE receives dozens of government scholarship seats annually, including the Jiangsu and Nanjing Government scholarships. In 2023, NUFE established the NUFE Scholarship, which offers various awards to outstanding students who will enter NUFE in the autumn semester of 2023. The previous tuition fee waiver system still applies to students who enrolled in NUFE before 2023.
NUFE's financial policy states that tuition fee payment and scholarship disbursement are separate processes. Students awarded scholarships must pay their tuition fees at the beginning of the academic year before becoming eligible to receive scholarship funds.
NUFE UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIP
The NUFE University Scholarship consists of three types of awards: Freshmen Scholarship, Academic Scholarship, and Special Scholarship. Each of these scholarship competitions is held annually.
The Freshmen Scholarship is a one-time award given to exceptional first-year students who have completed the language preparatory program at NUFE. The first-rank winner will receive 17000 CNY, and the second-rank winner will receive 8500 CNY. The main requirement for this award is the applicant's overall performance in the language preparatory program during the previous academic year. Applicants who have achieved a score of more than 70 in the Comprehensive Chinese Course in both semesters, passed the required HSK test, and have completed NUFE's interview can apply for the first rank. Those who have achieved a score of more than 70 in the Comprehensive Chinese Course in just one semester, passed the required HSK test, and have completed NUFE's interview can apply for the second rank.
The Academic Scholarship is a one-time award given to outstanding undergraduates and postgraduates. For undergraduates, the first-rank award is 17000 CNY, and the second-rank award is 8500 CNY. The primary requirement for this award is the applicant's academic performance in the degree program during the previous academic year at NUFE, and this scholarship competition takes place every academic year. Applicants who have achieved a score of more than 70 in every course can apply for the first rank, whereas those who have achieved a score of more than 70 in at least eight courses can apply for the second rank. Postgraduate students follow identical academic requirements in the yearly scholarship competition, and their awards increase to 23000 / 11500 CNY. Doctoral students also follow identical academic requirements, increasing their awards to 30000 / 15000 CNY.
Special Scholarship for Bachelor's Degree Program Graduates of NUFE
Bachelor's Degree Program graduates from NUFE with HSK-5 Chinese competency can apply for the special scholarship for the 1st year in Master's Degree Programs at NUFE. NUFE graduates who achieved an average score over 70 throughout Bachelor's Degree Programs can apply for the first award of 23000 CNY, and those who achieved an average score over 65 for the second rank of 11500 CNY. Later they will join the Academic Scholarship competition in their second year of study in Master's Degree programs.
Special Scholarship for 专接本项目/Zhuanjieben Project Students
Newly admitted 专接本项目/Zhuanjieben Project students with HSK-4 Chinese competency can apply for the special scholarship for the junior year in Bachelor's Degree programs. The students who achieved an average score over 75 throughout college study can apply for the first award of 17000 CNY, and those who achieved an average score over 70 for the second rank of 8500 CNY. Later they will join the Academic Scholarship competition as seniors in Bachelor's Degree programs.
GOVERNMENT SCHOLARSHIP COMPETITION
The Jiangsu Government Scholarship provides a one-off fund of 18000 CNY. Meanwhile, the Nanjing Government Scholarship offers a one-off scholarship ranging from 5000 CNY (for non-degree program students) to 10000 CNY (for undergraduate students) and 20000 CNY (for postgraduate students).
NUFE announces the scholarship competition notice every fall semester to all eligible international students on campus. After the application deadline, qualified applicants with higher average scores in the previous academic year will be invited to scholarship defense interviews. The final scores are calculated based on the average scores during the last academic year (50%) and interview scores (50%). The allocation of government scholarship seats will be based on the final scores.
