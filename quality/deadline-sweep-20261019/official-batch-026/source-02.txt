Kunming Medical University 
2026 Admission Brochure for International Students  
 
I. Admission Programs 
1. Degree Programs 
Full-time undergraduate, master's, and doctoral students in medicine -
related majors. 
2. Non-degree Programs 
Medical advanced training students and Chinese language advanced 
training students. 
 
II. Majors Offered 
For specific majors, please refer to the "Kunming Medical University 
2026 International Student Majors Catalog" published by the university. 
Inquiry website: https://www.kmmc.cn/list1924.aspx. 
 
III. Application Eligibility 
1 ．  Applicants must be foreign citizens with good moral character, 
physical and mental health, excellent academic performance, friendly 
attitudes toward China, and the ability to abide by the laws and 
regulations of the People's Republic of China and the rules of Kunming 
Medical University. Chinese citizens who have immigrated abroad and

apply for undergraduate programs as international students must hold a 
valid foreign passport or nationality certificate for at least four years 
(inclusive) and have resided abroad for more than two years in the past 
four years (as of April 30 of the enrol lment year). Residence of at least 
nine months in a year counts as one year (based on entry and exit stamps). 
Applicants meeting these conditions must provide relevant proof and 
undergo university review. 
2. Academic Background and Age Requirements 
(1) Bachelor's degree applicants: High school graduation or equivalent 
academic qualification, age 18–25. 
(2) Master's degree applicants: Bachelor's degree or equivalent, major 
closely related to undergraduate studies. For non -Chinese university 
graduates, the institution must be officially recognized by the Chinese 
Ministry of Education, age 18–35. 
(3) Doctoral degree applicants: Master's degree or equivalent, major 
closely related to master's or undergraduate studies. For non -Chinese 
university graduates, the institution must be officially recognized by the 
Chinese Ministry of Education, age 20–45. 
(4) Medical advanced training applicants: Bachelor's degree or higher in 
a medicine-related major, age 18–50. 
(5) Chinese language advanced training applicants: High school 
graduation or equivalent academic qualification, age 18–50.

3. Language Proficiency Requirements 
(1) Chinese-taught programs: Ability to study in Chinese, with Chinese 
proficiency at least at Level 4 of the International Chinese Language 
Proficiency Standard (HSK4). 
(2) English-taught programs: Applicants must have strong English 
language skills and solid foundational science knowledge. Non -native 
English speakers must provide IELTS Academic (minimum 6.0, no band 
below 5.0), TOEFL (Internet-based test minimum 70, no section below 
15), or equivalent English test scores. Native English speakers or those 
who completed secondary education entirely in English are exempt from 
English test scores but must submit proof of English grades and English-
medium instruction from their secondary school. 
4. China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) 
Mathematics and physics are mandatory subjects . All applicants of 
undergraduate programs must hold a valid " China Scholastic 
Competency Assessment (CSCA) " score report from within the past 
year. For exam methods, dates, and locations, please check the official 
websites (www.csca.cn or www.studyinchina.edu.cn) for 
announcements.

V. Application Process 
1. Online Application 
Applicants must apply online via the Kunming Medical University 
International Student Admission Platform (http://study.kmmc.cn or 
https://kmmc.17gz.org/). Scholarship applicants should refer to the 
"Kunming Medical University 2026 International Student Sch olarship 
Program Admission Brochure." The following documents must be 
uploaded during online application: 
(1) Passport photo page (must be valid); 
(2) "Foreigner Physical Examination Form" and blood test report; 
(3) Notarized highest diploma, degree certificate, and academic 
transcripts in Chinese or English; 
(4) HSK Level 4 or above certificate (mandatory for Chinese -taught 
programs or English -taught clinical medicine master's degree 
applicants); 
(5) English proficiency test scores (mandatory for English -taught

2. Review and Admission Process 
The university will review application materials and conduct interviews 
for qualified candidates (notifications sent via email from official 
university email addresses ending with "@kmmu.edu.cn"). Admissions 
are merit-based. 
(1) Preliminary review: The university will review information and 
attachments. Results are only provided on the "Kunming Medical 
University International Student Admission Platform" 
(http://study.kmmc.cn or https://kmmc.17gz.org/). Ensure email 
accuracy. 
(2) Pre -admission: Qualified applicants proceed to online video 
interviews. Notifications and interview methods are sent via email. 
Check emails promptly to avoid missing interviews (missed interviews 
are considered voluntary withdrawal). 
(3) Provisional admission: Applicants passing interviews enter the 
"provisional admission" stage. The university organizes an expert panel 
to review all provisional admissions. 
(4) Final admission: Self -funded students are selected by the expert 
panel based on merit. Scholarship recommendations are submitted to

email/WeChat. 
 
 
VI. Registration 
1. Per the Chinese Ministry of Education's "paperless office" requirement, 
the university issues electronic admission notices and JW202/JW201 forms 
via email, not paper copies. 
2. Admitted students must apply for an X1 visa within the timeframe 
specified in the admission notice and report to the university with all 
original application and admission materials. 
3. Those unable to register must request leave in writing in advance. 
Unauthorized late registration results in canceled admission.
