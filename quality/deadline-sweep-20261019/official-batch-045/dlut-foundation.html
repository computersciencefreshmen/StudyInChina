DUT Foundation Program


Program Introduction

As a non-degree program for undergraduate preparation, this foundation program is intended for the graduates from senior high schools who want to study the undergraduate degree in DUT. The foundation program is divided into two directions, Science & Engineering and Chinese Language & Arts. Relying on the strong faculty resources of Dalian University of Technology, it provides intensive instruction in Chinese language, mathematics, physics, and chemistry. This comprehensive approach enhances students' Chinese proficiency, foundational knowledge in mathematics and science, and professional application skills, laying a solid academic foundation for their undergraduate studies.

Enrollment Target

            1. Non-Chinese citizens

            2. Aged 17-34 with good physical and mental health

            3. Qualified for university application with the graduate diploma of a senior high school

Enrollment Time

        Fall semester (one year) : September intake with zero-based Chinese language

         Spring semester (one semester) : March intake with HSK-3 or above

Program Advantages

1.Students who pass the foundation program final exam, meet the required scores on the Academic Proficiency Assessment (CSCA), and demonstrate good conduct during their time at the school will be given priority for admission to our undergraduate programs.

2. Foundation program students who continue to apply for an undergraduate program are exempt from the 800 yuan application fee.

3. Students with excellent scores in the test may be entitled to the preferred recommendation to the DUT undergraduate scholarship program.

Application Documents  

        1. Copy of passport photo page, blank visa page

        2. The Certified/Notarized Copy of High School Diploma (Chinese or English Version);

        3. Certificate of No-Criminal Conviction;

        4. Physical Examination Form (application for one year);

        5. Certificate of HSK-3 and above (application for spring semester)

        6. Supplementary Documents for Minor Applicants：Applicants under the age of 18 are required to submit the             documents listed in the link: https://sie.dlut.edu.cn/info/1263/29262.htm




Application Procedures

        1. Submit online application：http://iso.dlut.edu.cn

        2. Comprehensive Interview (March intake)        

        3. Pay Application fee       

        4. Admitted

    

Tuition Fees (CNY)

Enrollment in September（one year program）: Tuition fees CNY 28,000 yuan; application fee CNY 800 yuan*

Enrollment in March（one semester program）: Tuition fee CNY 15,000 yuan; application fee CNY 800 yuan*

* The application fee will not be returned; applicants who continue with the undergraduate program will be exempted from the application fee for the undergraduate program

Graduation Requirements

Passing HSK-4 and graduation exams of the foundation program
