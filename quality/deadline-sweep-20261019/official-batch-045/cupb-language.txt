1. Application Qualifications
1. Physically and mentally healthy, law-abiding, and non-criminal record, friendly towards
China, able to meet the normal study and life at school.
2. Applicants should be a non-Chinese citizen who can study and live in the school in good
health and with a sound mind. If both or one of the applicant’s parents is a Chinese citizen
and resides in a foreign country, the applicant has foreign nationality at birth. In that case,
they do not have Chinese nationality and must have a record of actually living abroad for
more than 2 years within the last 4 years. (Nine (9) months living abroad are equivalent to
one year, subject to entry and exit visas).
Chinese emigrants who apply to come to China for undergraduate study as international
students must hold a valid foreign passport or nationality certificate for more than 4 years.
Within the 4 years, they must have a record of living abroad for more than 2 years (9 months
abroad in a year can be calculated as one year, subject to entry and exit visas).
3. High school graduate with high school diploma or above.
2. Required application materials
The following documents are required:
1. Passport information page
2. Visa pages (scanned copies of all China visa pages and entry-exit records)
3. Original highest education certificate, should be translated to Chinese or English if needed (If
you have not graduated, please provide a "Pre-graduation certificate")
4. Original highest education transcript. (translated to Chinese or English)
5. Language proficiency certificate
6. Guardian authorization certificate (Only required by applicants under the age of 18)
7. Certificate of Non-criminal record
8. Physical examination form for foreigners
9. Accommodation registration certificate (Only for the applicants who are in China now)

3. Specific Requirements:
Training requirements:
1.Adapted to all the four Chinese language abilities used in Chinese teaching environment,
including listening, speaking, reading and writing skills
2.Master some basic professional knowledge
3.Gradually adapt to the teaching methods and learning methods of the university
4.Have the ability to learn and think independently.
Program categories
Chinese Class (long term): The long-term class (more than half a year) enrolls students twice a
year, starting in March and September respectively, with different levels: primary, intermediate
and advanced courses. The core courses include: reading and writing, listening and speaking,
reading, intensive reading, scientific Chinese, and Chinese culture, etc.
According to the applicants’ application and class placement interview results, several small
classes will be organized for teaching. Each class will have listening, speaking, reading and
writing courses to comprehensively develop students’ four skills. Classes will run from Monday
to Friday. Every week 22-30 class hours will be arranged. In addition, there will be special
tutoring courses for HSK.

Chinese class (short-term): Short-term class (2-6 weeks); usually held in the winter and

Chinese culture courses cover different areas such as Chinese culture, economic development,
history and geography, helping students get a comprehensive understanding of China.
Specialized skills courses can lay a solid foundation for students to adapt to undergraduate
courses in advance.
Completion and further education
Upon completion: Students who pass the required courses and obtain the required credits will
be awarded the Certificate of Completion for Chinese Language Training issued by the Institute
of International Education.
Further education: Students who have completed all the teaching procedures prescribed in the
training program and passed the undergraduate qualification examination, they must take the
China Scholastic Competency Assessment（CSCA） and obtain the corresponding scores before
they can enter the undergraduate program of our school for study. Those who do not meet the
eligibility criteria for completion or fail to pass the qualification examination for admission to
the undergraduate program and do not take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment
（ CSCA） are not allowed to enter the undergraduate study and can continue to study the
language program until they meet the requirements.

6、Scholarship Application:
1） Category: Language Teachers (Confucius Institute) Scholarship（ Can not apply without
Chinese language foundation）
Admission category: one-academic year trainee and one-semester trainee.
a) Scholarship for Academic Year Study: The program commences in September 2026, and
provides scholarship for a maximum of 11 months. International students who have benefited
from similar scholarships are not eligible.
For the area of TCSOL, applicants shall have a minimum score of 270 on the HSK test (Level
3), and an HSKK test score is required.
For the areas of Chinese Language and Literature, applicants shall have a minimum score of
180 on the HSK test (Level 4) and 60 on the HSKK test (Intermediate Level).
For the area of Chinese Language Study, applicants shall have a minimum score of 210 on the
HSK test (Level 3).Priority will be given to applicants who provide an HSKK test score.
b）Scholarship for One-Semester Study: The program commences either in September 2026
or March 2027, and provides scholarship of a maximum of five months. Applicants holding the
X1 or X2 visa are not eligible.
For the areas of TCSOL, Chinese Language and Literature, applicants shall have a minimum
score of 180 on the HSK test (Level 3), and an HSKK test score is required.

2）Applicants Requirements:
a) non-Chinese citizens;
b) in good physical and mental conditions, and with good academic performance and conduct;
c) committed to the Chinese language education and related work;
d) between the ages of 16 and 35 (as of September 1st). Applicants currently working as
Chinese language teachers shall not be over 45, while undergraduate student applicants shall not
be over 25.
Application Procedure
1. Contact and choose a recommending institution. Usually, the Confucius Institute which you
registered should be the recommending institution. Confucius Institutes, independently operated
classrooms with the history for more than half a year (can be found
at http://www.hanban.org).Applicants may log on the application website of China University
of Petroleum (Beijing) (https://cie-registration.cup.edu.cn/) for registration, submit the
application, and attend the interview. After passing the interview, they will receive a letter of
recommendation.
2. Online Application: Applicants log in scholarship website http://cis.chinese.cn , register then
fill in the application on line.
3. Scholarship holders need to confirm with the host institutions to go through the procedures of
studying in China, print out the scholarship certificate online, and register at host institutions on
the designated date as per the letter of admission.
3）Application Materials：
1. Scanned copies of all China visa pages and China entry and exit record pages
2. Scanned copies of score reports of the HSK and HSKK tests (within the two-year validity);
3. A reference letter by the head of the recommending institution
4. Online application form
5. Notarized copies of your Highest Degree certificates, and transcripts
6. Certificates of Chinese competitions you participated in institutions.
7. Applicants currently working as Chinese language teachers shall provide proof of
employment as well as a reference letter by the employer.
8. Applicants under the age of 18 shall provide certified documents of designation signed by
their entrusted legal guardians in China.
Language Teachers (Confucius Institute) Scholarship website: http://cis.chinese.cn
HSK, HSKK examination site: http://www.chinesetest.cn
