People’s Republic of China. Yangtze University is an esteemed institution with 
a rich heritage, and its history can be traced back to 1936. In 2003, it

and universities including Former Jianghan Petroleum Institute, Former Hubei

Yangtze University is in Jingzhou, Hubei province, a famous historical and 
cultural city along the middle reaches of the Yangtze River, and has a campus 
in Wuhan, which occupies an area of 341.2 hectares. The university has  2,989 
faculty members, 36,503 undergraduate students,  7,491 master’s degree and

2. How to apply
Please visit yangtzeu.at0086.cn/StuApplication/Login.aspx to submit necessary 
materials. Admission will be determined after the verification and appraisal of the 
application materials. If applicants are admitted, they will receive a Visa Application 
Form for International Students to China (form JW202) and Admission Notice. With 
related documents, applicants can apply for their student visa (X1 or X2) at the Chinese 
embassy in their home country.
3. Application Pre-requisites
Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens in good health, with valid passport, below 45 
years old, and should abide by Chinese law and the regulations of Yangtze University. 
Applicants should also respect Chinese social customs and codes of conduct. 
Applicants who apply for Preparatory Program should be below 23 years old. 
Applicants who apply for bachelor’s degree should be below 25 years old. Applicants 
who apply for master degree should be below 35 years old. Applicants who apply for 
doctor degree should be below 40 years old.

5. 申请程序
第1步：了解学校和相关专业信息。国际学生在开始申请之前，请仔细阅读学校
网站上的详细信息，获取学校和相关专业课程信息，决定要申请专业和层次。
第2步：完成网上注册。国际学生需在网上申请系统中上传必要的个人材料，包
括护照、学位证、毕业证、成绩单、申请表（Word版本）等等，所上传全部文
件需扫描完整、清晰、彩色。
第3步：查收录取通知书。如果国际学生申请材料符合录取要求，通过相关面试
（奖学金生和研究生），学校将录取通知书发到国际学生预留的邮箱。
第4步：支付申请费。国际学生收到录取通知书后需将申请费（人民币800元）
转账至学校账户以保留录取资格。付款后需将缴费凭据扫描件发给学校。
Application Procedures
Step 1: Learn more about the school and the programs of YU. Get information 
about university and programs, then read details in our website before 
starting your application. Make a decision on which program and which 
degree you are going to apply for.
Step 2: Register in YU online application system. Upload all necessary
