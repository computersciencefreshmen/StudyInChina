I. Overview of Yunnan University of Finance and Economics
Established in 1951 and after 74-year legacy and dedication through generations,
Yunnan University of Finance and Economics (YUFE) has developed into a
time-honored university in the ethnic minority area in the borderland. At present, it
has 4 first-class discipline doctoral programs, 5 postdoctoral research stations, 12
first-class discipline master’s programs, 18 professional degree master’s programs and
66 undergraduate programs. It stands as a preeminent teaching-and-research-oriented
university with distinctive strengths in economics and management, while fostering
multidisciplinary collaborative development in law, literature, science, engineering
and arts.
Yunnan University of Finance and Economics upholds the motto “Studious and
Steadfast, Virtuous and Visionary”, and holds fast to the ethos of “Relentless Pursuit
of Truth and Innovation”. YUFE adheres to an open policy in education, actively
serving and integrating into the national Belt and Road Initiative. It recruits
international exchange students from various countries, offering programs at the
language, undergraduate, master’s and doctoral levels. It is authorized by the Ministry
of Foreign Affairs to issue visa invitation letters for foreigners coming to China and is
exempt from verification responsibilities. YUFE has established educational
exchanges and partnerships with over 160 universities and institutions from more than
30 countries and regions, including the United States, France, and the United
Kingdom. It is the earliest university in Yunnan Province to offer Sino-foreign
cooperative education programs. YUFE has partnered with Rangsit University in
Thailand to establish the “Bangkok Business School of Yunnan University of Finance
and Economics”. This institution is among the first batch of overseas educational
entities approved by the Ministry of Education. It enrolls students from Thailand and
other Southeast Asian countries, offering academic degree programs and business
training. As a training base for international business officials under the Ministry of
Commerce, YUFE has hosted degree programs and short-term training for officials
from developing countries, funded by the Ministry of Commerce. Currently, YUFE
has ongoing short and long-term student exchange programs with universities or
institutions from over 40 countries, including France, the UK, the United States,
Australia, Thailand and Myanmar. In 2025, it successfully passed the quality
certification for higher education of international students in China by the Ministry of
Education. YUFE has trained over 6,000 international students from 107 countries,
making significant contributions to enhancing YUFE’s international reputation and
promoting cross-cultural understanding and common development.

IV. Scholarship
New international students can apply for Chinese Government Scholarships
and Yunnan Provincial Government Scholarships, which cover all tuition fees,
accommodation fees, living expenses and insurance premiums. The Enrollment
Guide of each type of scholarship program will be released separately.
Scholarship selection methods are carried out in accordance with the relevant
regulations of the nation and the university. Please refer to the official website of
Yunnan University of Finance and Economics for more details:
https://www.ynufe.edu.cn/sooe/index.htm
V. Eligibility And Application Documents
i. Application Requirements：
1．Non-Chinese citizens holding valid foreign passports, who are physically and
mentally healthy；
2 ． Applicants for bachelor's degree (including preparatory undergraduates)
should have high school graduation diploma or the same academic ability, with
excellent academic performance, and generally not more than 25 years old；
3．Applicants for master's degree (including preparatory postgraduates) should
have a bachelor's degree or equivalent, with excellent academic performance, and
generally not older than 35 years old；
4.Applicants for doctor's degree (including preparatory doctoral students) should
have a master's degree or equivalent, with excellent academic performance, and
generally not older than 40 years old；
5．Chinese advanced students should have graduated from high school or above,
with excellent academic performance, and generally under the age of 55；
6．Applicants should also meet the university's entry requirements in terms of
academic ability, language proficiency and other relevant aspects. Current academic
qualifications, language ability and age meet the university's admission requirements.

VI. Application and Admission Process
i. Online Application
Applicants who meet the application requirements log in the YUFE International
Student Service System (https://ynufe.at0086.cn/StuApplication/Login.aspx) to
register for an account, fill in the application information online according to the
requirements and upload the required application documents.
ii. Application Deadline
Chinese Government Scholarship “High-Level Postgraduate” Program：February
27, 2026
Yunnan Provincial Government Scholarship：January 16, 2026
Self-funded Program：June 15, 2026
iii. Admission Process
1.The school conducts a comprehensive evaluation of the application docum
ents submitted by applicants；
2.Applicants who pass the preliminary review must participate in the assessment
organized by the school. Specific arrangements will be announced separately；
3. Organize expert reviews and announce the list of candidates；
4.The school issues admission letters to accepted applicants.
