招生简章 Admissions

首 页 >> 来华留学生 >> 招生简章 Admissions
当前位置:

学校拥有良好的国际化氛围和丰富的留学生培养经验、教师团队，学校提供优良的住宿条件和各类奖学金，热忱欢迎留学生来校学习。

 

一、学习层次及要求

1. 本科生：年龄18至30周岁，HSK4级，

学费：理工科16000元/年，文科15000元/年，艺术类16000元/年。

2. 硕士研究生：年龄18至35周岁，

学费：理工科21000元/年，文科16500元/年，艺术类20000元/年。

3. 博士研究生，年龄18至40周岁，

学费：28000元/年。


二、住宿及费用

1. 本科生、硕士研究生：2人/间，费用400元/月

2. 博士生：1人/间，费用600元/月

注：以上所有费用均为人民币标准。


申请时间：每年3-5月。


ADMISSIONS AND ENROLLMENT

Eligibility:

(1). Undergraduate Program:

The candidate shall be over 18 and under 30 years old, healthy, with senior high school diploma or above and qualified HSK scores (at least 4-level).

Length of Schooling: 4 years for all undergraduate programs

Tuition: 16000 yuan RMB per year for students of sciences and engineering

              15000 yuan RMB per year for students of liberal arts

              18000 yuan RMB per year for students of arts


 

(2). Postgraduate Program:

The candidate shall be over 18 and under 35 years old, healthy, with bachelor diploma or above and qualified HSK scores

Length of Schooling: 3 years for all postgraduate programs

Tuition: 21000 yuan RMB per year for students of sciences and engineering

              16500 yuan RMB per year for students of liberal arts

              20000 yuan RMB per year for students of arts


 

(3). Doctoral Program:

The candidate shall be over 18 and under 40 years old, healthy, with master diploma or above and qualified HSK scores

Length of Schooling: 4 years for all doctoral programs

Tuition: 28000 yuan RMB per year



Accommodation

1. Undergraduate and Postgraduate: 400 yuan RMB/month (twin room)

2. Doctoral: 600 yuan RMB/month (single room)
