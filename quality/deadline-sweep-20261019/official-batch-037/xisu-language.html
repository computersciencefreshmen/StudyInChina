Chinese Programs
Xi'an International Studies University (XISU), located in the ancient capital of Xi’an, was the first institution of higher learning in Northwest China to offer Chinese language programs for international students, admitting its first cohort in 1979. Over the years, we have educated more than 20,000 students from 45 countries and regions. Currently, XISU offers a comprehensive range of academic programs spanning from bachelor's to doctoral degrees, as well as non-degree programs including Chinese language courses, short-term exchange programs, and customized courses tailored to the Belt and Road Initiative. Leveraging our strengths in multilingual education, we nurture cross-cultural ambassadors with global perspectives, blending rich historical heritage with modern educational approaches. We warmly invite young talents from around the world to join the XISU family and write our shared story of growth together!

Program Features

Small class teaching: No more than 25 students per class.

Diverse course formats: An excellent team of Chinese teachers provides face-to-face instruction, improving students'listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills.

Well-structured levels: Courses are divided into different Chinese proficiency levels to accurately match students' language ability.

Wide range of courses: Optional courses such as HSK exam preparation and Chinese ink painting are available free of charge.

Cultural tours: Organized once every semester to eastern routes such as the Terracotta Warriors and Huaqing Palace, allowing students to explore Shaanxi's history and culture (participation is voluntary; entrance fees are at students' own expense; transportation and accompanying teachers are arranged by XISU).

Academic records: All students will receive a transcript at the end of the semester. Students who meet the completion requirements will be awarded a certificate of completion.

About Refunds

1.Students who withdraw before the official registration date of each semester (with valid reason or proof) will receive a full refund of the tuition fee for that semester.

2.Students who withdraw within the first two weeks after the semester starts will receive a 70% refund of the tuition fee; no refund will be given after the first two weeks.

Registration fees, insurance fees, and textbook fees are non-refundable.

4.No fees shall be refunded to students who have been ordered to withdraw or expelled from school.

5.Students who suspend their studies and leave school may carry forward their tuition and accommodation fees for use upon resumption of studies.

Visa and On-campus Accommodation

1.The university provides visa application documents for Non-degree Program students.

2.On-campus accommodation applications must be submitted online by the deadline specified in the Enrollment Guide.
