Scholarship policy
SJU offers a range of findings, including Shandong Provincial Scholarship and University scholarships. Outstanding freshmen can get a full scholarship, and other admitted freshmen can get a half scholarship. Please contact Admission Office for more details (admission@sdjzu.edu.cn).
