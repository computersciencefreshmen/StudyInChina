Shandong University of Finance and Economics English-Taught Undergraduate Programs Special Scholarship Application Guide 2026
Time:April 14, 2026 Visitor：[]
I. Program Introduction
To attract outstanding international students and cultivate globally competitive talent, SDUFE offers the Special Scholarship for International Students enrolled in its English-taught undergraduate programs leading to a bachelor's degree.
Programs offered (a minimum of 20 students to commence):
-International Economics and Trade
-Cross-border E-commerce
-Business Administration
II. Scholarship Coverage
1. Standard Program Fees: a total of 12000 RMB for tuition and accommodation (double room) per academic year. Application fee and comprehensive medical insurance are to be borne by the student.
2. Maximum Scholarship Award: Waiver of tuition and on-campus accommodation (double room) fees.
3. Starting from the second academic year, scholarships are awarded based on academic performance and overall conduct. The maximum award covers the tuition and accommodation fees for the following academic year.
III. Eligibility
1. Non-Chinese citizens;
2. Not concurrently hold any other type of scholarship;
3. High school diploma required;
4. English proficiency: Minimum IELTS 5.5 or equivalent (Native English speakers are exempt from providing proof).
IV. Application
1. Online Application: Submit applications via SDUFE Online Application System (http://istu.sdufe.edu.cn). Applicants are advised to regularly check their email and junk mail for further information. Failure to provide the requested information in a timely manner may result in rejection.
2. Application deadline: June 20, 2026.
V. Required Documents
1. Completed SDUFE International Student Application Form (Generated upon completion of the online application);
2. Scanned copy of the personal information page of the passport;
3. 2-inch bareheaded passport photo with white background (JPG);
4. Official high school diploma;
5. Official high school transcript;
6. Language proficiency certificate (within validity period);
7. Resume (for non-current high school graduate);
8. Proof of financial support.
Note: For official documents not originally issued in Chinese/English, both the original and a notarized Chinese/English translation must be submitted.
VI. Supporting Documents (for higher-level scholarship consideration)
1. Valid HSK score report (within 2 years)
2. Award certificates (academic/practical innovation competitions, etc.)
3. CSCA score report
