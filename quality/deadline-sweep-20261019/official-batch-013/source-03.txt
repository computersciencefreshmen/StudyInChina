Reasons for Choosing AHU
A Land of Profound Culture and History
Anhui Province is located at the central part of China. Besides its beautiful
natural and geographic scenery, it is also the birthplace of Daoism and Hui
Culture which are among the most influential cultures in China.
A City of Robust Vitality and Creativity
Hefei is ranked as one of the fastest-growing cities in the world. As one of
the three key scientific and education base containing more than 60 regular
higher institutions and universities, it is also titled as the only national pilot
city for technical innovation due to its remarkable scientific andtechnological
progress.
A University of Remarkable Excellence and Diversity
AHU, founded in 1928，is a key provincial institution co-constructed by
Anhui Provincial Government and Ministry of Education of China. It provides
wonderful learning experience with a profound cultural connotation, a
variety of disciplines, strong academic excellence, distinctive research
characteristics, diverse and innovative atmosphere, beautiful campus
environment and first-class teaching conditions.
