Beijing Forestry University, established in 1952, holds a distinguished status
as a national key university directly administered by the Ministry of
Education. It's jointly supported by the Ministry of Education, the National
Forestry and Grassland Administration, and the Beijing Municipal
Government.
Firmly rooted in biology and ecology, Beijing Forestry University boasts
unique disciplines, including forestry, landscape architecture, ecology and
nature conservation, grass science, and agro-economic management. Our
commitment extends to fostering balanced and harmonious development
across various fields, encompassing agriculture, science, engineering,
management, economics, law, philosophy, education, and the arts.
Beijing Forestry University is building strong international partnerships
through direct student and faculty engagement, collaborative projects, and
scholarship that spans traditional boundaries. We proudly host students
pursuing undergraduate, graduate, and non-degree programs from
approximately 70 countries. Our enduring commitment lies in creating a
welcoming environment for international students and scholars who enrich
our academic landscape with diverse perspectives and innovative ideas.
Our undergraduate programs typically span four academic years and are
predominantly conducted in Chinese. Upon meeting all degree
requirements, graduates are conferred with a bachelor's degree.
Join us at Beijing Forestry University and become part of a vibrant
academic community that values global diversity and innovation.
北京林业大学是教育部直属，教育部、国家林业和草原局、北京市人民政府共建
的全国重点大学，是国家首批“211工程”重点建设高校和国家“优势学科创新平台”
建设项目试点高校，是国家“双一流”建设高校。学校以生物学、生态学为基础，以
林学、风景园林学、林业工程、水土保持与荒漠化防治、草学和农林经济管理为特
色，是农、理、工、管、经、文、法、哲、教、艺等多门类协调发展的全国重点大
学。

of China is responsible for the acceptance and administration of scholarship applications. CGS

candidates are eligible to apply CGS Type A Program.Please ask more application
information from Chinese Embassy/Consulate in your home country. When filling in the
scholarship application form, the applicant should choose Type A and select Beijing Forestry
University as your first priority institution.
