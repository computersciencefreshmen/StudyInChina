海南医科大学简介
Brief Introduction of Hainan Medical University
海南医科大学座落在素有
“椰城”之称的热带滨海城市——海南省省会海口市。其前身始于创立于1947年的海强医事技术学校和创立于1948年的私立海南大学医学院。现有城西（主校区）、桂林洋（新校区）和龙华等三个校区，占地1416.71亩。
学校已经形成了面向全国招生，涵盖全日制本科和硕博士研究生和留学生教育以及成人教育和继续教育的全方位、多层次的人才培养体系。现有全日制在校生
15340人。其中，本科生12207人（含留学生633人）、博士硕士研究生1760人。
学校开设

，

Hainan Medical University is located in Haikou, the capital city of Hainan Province, a tropical coastal city known as the "Coconut City". Its origins can be traced back to Haiqiang Medical Technical School founded in 1947 and the Medical College of Private Hainan University established in 1948. The university has three campuses: Chengxi (main campus), Guilin Yang (new campus) and Longhua, covering an area of 1,416.71 mu.
The university has developed a comprehensive and multi-level talent training system, recruiting students nationwide. It offers full-time undergraduate, postgraduate (master's and doctoral), international student programs, as well as adult and continuing education. There are 15,340 full-time students, including 12,207 undergraduates (633 international students) and 1,760 master's and doctoral students.
The university offers 36 undergraduate programs covering four disciplines: Medicine, Science, Management and Engineering. It boasts 5 national first-class undergraduate program construction sites, 2 national characteristic specialties, and 3 national first-class undergraduate courses. There is one National Experimental Teaching Demonstration Center, one National Clinical Teaching and Training Demonstration Center, and one National Clinical (Traditional Chinese Medicine Clinical) Teaching and Training Demonstration Center respectively. The university has three directly affiliated tertiary general hospitals with the largest scale in the province, namely Hainan Affiliated Hospital (Hainan General Hospital), the First Affiliated Hospital and the Second Affiliated Hospital, with a total of 7,020 open beds. In addition, it runs 10 non-directly affiliated hospitals, 12 teaching hospitals and 2 jointly built centers for disease control and prevention, providing strong support for clinical teaching. It has 15 national key clinical specialties and 7 provincial key disciplines. Clinical Medicine ranks among the top 5‰ in the ESI discipline rankings, and Pharmacology & Toxicology ranks among the top 1% in the ESI discipline rankings. The university is authorized to confer doctoral degrees in the first-level discipline of Clinical Medicine, and master's degrees in five first-level disciplines including Basic Medicine, Clinical Medicine, Pharmacy, Traditional Chinese Medicine and Biology. It also provides professional master's degree programs in five fields: Clinical Medicine, Public Health, Nursing, Pharmacy and Stomatology.

(III) Notes
1. In addition to the above documents, one standard color passport-style photograph is required. The file size shall not exceed 200 KB. Supported formats include *.jpg, *.jpeg and *.png, with a pixel resolution greater than 295*4132.
2. All submitted application materials will not be returned regardless of admission results. The applicant undertakes that all personal information, certificates and supporting documents provided are true and accurate. If verified to be untrue by the admissions authority or certification body, the applicant’s application, admission or study eligibility will be revoked.
3. If your certificates and documents are issued in a language other than Chinese or English, you shall submit notarized documents in Chinese or English.
4. The university and its school admissions offices reserve the right to require applicants to provide hard-copy originals of application materials or certification documents issued by designated certification authorities for further verification.
5. Applicants must provide their contact phone number and email address when submitting application materials.

Applicants failing to obtain the certificate may choose to leave the university or follow the admission requirements for the 5-year program students.
7. For 5-year program students who do not hold an HSK Level 4 certificate upon admission but possess corresponding Chinese proficiency, they may apply to take the Standards for Chinese Language Proficiency examination organized by our university. Students who pass this examination to obtain admission eligibility are allowed to undertake a one-year probationary study in the Clinical Medicine program. They must obtain the HSK Level 4 certificate and complete the corresponding Clinical Medicine studies within this period. Failure to obtain the certificate will result in the cancellation of probationary eligibility.
8. Special Reminder: The major name printed on our university’s graduation certificate and degree certificate is "clinical medicine". Applicants for the 1+5-year and 5-year Clinical Medicine programs shall verify with the competent authorities in their home country in advance whether they can obtain corresponding professional practice qualifications after graduation.

VI. Scholarship Application
Hainan Medical University offers two scholarship programs: Hainan Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students and ASEAN Scholarship. The funding duration for undergraduate students ranges from 4 to 6 years, corresponding to the length of their academic program; for master’s students, the funding duration is 3 years. The scholarship covers tuition fees, accommodation fees and medical insurance premiums, while all other expenses shall be borne by the students themselves.
Eligibility Criteria for Scholarship Applicants
- Be qualified to apply for the university’s degree‑seeking international student programs;
- Not be recipients of the Chinese Government Scholarship or any other types of scholarships.
Application Documents
- Complete the Application Form for Hainan Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students. The form can be downloaded from the website of the School of International Education, Hainan Medical University at http://www.hainmc.edu.cn/sole/. Fill it out and submit it via email to hnmu_enquire@muhn.edu.cn.
- All application documents required for the university’s degree‑seeking international student programs.
Special Reminder: Scholarship recipients must participate in the annual scholarship review after enrolment in accordance with relevant regulations. Those who fail the review shall have their scholarship eligibility suspended or revoked.
