HDU 2026 Application Guideline

2026杭州电子科技大学国际学生申请指南


基本要求

Basic Requirements


  申请人须年满18周岁及以上，持有外国有效普通护照的非中国籍公民。申请人应身心健康，对华友好，无吸毒史，无违法犯罪记录，愿意遵守中国法律法规及学校规章制度，尊重中国人民的风俗习惯。

Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens aged 18 and above. Should be in good physical and mental health,be friendly towards China, have no history of drug abuse or criminal records, be willing to abide by China's laws and the university's rules and regulations, and respect China's social and cultural customs.



语言要求

Language proficiency requirement


申请中文授课：

Degree Program in Chinese:


申请本科，汉语水平需达到HSK4级180分及以上。

Undergraduate Degree:HSK Level 4, Score 180 and above.


申请硕博研究生，汉语水平需达到HSK5级180分及以上。

Master Degree and Doctor Degree : HSK Level 5, Score 180 and above.


申请英文授课：

Degree Program in English:


需提供IELTS、TOEFL、Duolingo、EF SET中的其中一项成绩。

IELTS, TOEFL, Duolingo, or EF SET.



申请材料

Application Documents


（一）本科专业（年龄要求18—25岁）

Ⅰ. Undergraduate Degree Programs（18 - 25 years old）


1.高中毕业证书；

1. Notarized highest education certificate or diploma and formal English/Chinese translation.


2.高中阶段全部课程成绩单。

2. Notarized academic transcript and formal English/Chinese translation.


（二）硕博研究生专业（年龄要求硕士32岁以下，博士35岁以下）

Ⅱ.Master Degree Programs and Doctor DegreePrograms（Master's degree holders should be under 32 years old, and doctoral degree holders should be under 35 years old）


1.本科/研究生学历学位证书；

1. Notarized highest education certificate or diploma and formal English/Chinese translation.


2.本科/研究生阶段全部课程成绩单；

2. Notarized academic transcript and formal English/Chinese translation.


3.两封副教授职称以上人员的推荐信；

3. Two recommendation letters written by 2 associate professors or professors from the related universities in China or abroad for Master and Ph.D applicants.


4.学习计划（不少于1000字）。

4. Study plan of no less than 1000 words for graduate program applicants.


（三）汉语言进修生（年龄要求17—25岁）

Ⅲ.Chinese Language Program (17-25 years old)


1.高中毕业证书。

1. Notarized highest education certificate or diploma and formal English/Chinese translation.


2.高中阶段全部课程成绩单。

2.Notarized academic transcript and formal English/Chinese translation.


3.未满18周岁的申请人应提供未成年人担保文件。

3.The applicant under the age of 18 must submit guardianship document.


其他相关申请材料（中文或英文）

Other related application documents (in Chinese or English)


1.小二寸照片;

1. One digital photo of two-inch;


2.有效护照页；

2.First page of the passport (valid for at least 6 month) and valid visa page;


3.外国人体格检查表；

3.Foreigner Physical Examination Form;


4.国际学生个人健康情况报告书；

4.International Student Health Report(Download from the website);


5.无犯罪证明(有效期3个月内)；

5.Non-criminal record certificate issued by the local security department from the applicants’ host country (Valid for 3 months);


6.银行存款证明；

6.Financial support statement issued by the local bank from the applicants’ host country;


7.日常表现证明（如有在中国读书经历）。

7.Students who have studied in a China university or college need to provide statement of Daily performance issued by the university or college.


·以上申请材料如为中、英文以外的文本，还需提供公证过的翻译件。

·If the above application materials are in a language other than Chinese or English, a notarized translation must also be provided.


·无需提交纸质版申请材料。

·No hard copy application materials are required.


·网上申请提交成功后，请务必及时关注网上申请系统的状态变化及个人邮箱，如果您被录取，系统将通过电子邮件发送关于如何下载JW202表格的说明。请确保提供的电子邮件地址是正确且有效的。

·After successfully submitting your online application, please closely monitor the status updates in the online application system and your personal email. If you are admitted, the system will send instructions on how to download the JW202 form via email. Please ensure the email address you provided is correct and valid.


·如申请者曾经来华或在华，还需提交最近一次出入境签证页和有海关“关签”页扫描件。

·Applicants who have previously been to or resided in China must submit scanned copies of the most recent entry/exit visa page and the page with the customs entry stamp.


·如申请者曾在本校或中国其它高校就读过“一年制汉语进修生课程或相关汉语课程”，申请必须先行提交曾就读高校开具的正规成绩单、出勤率（到课率）证明、在读证明和结业证书原件扫描件。

·Applicants who have previously enrolled in a one-year Chinese language program or related Chinese courses at this university or other Chinese institutions must first submit scanned copies of the original official transcript, attendance certificate, enrollment certificate, and completion certificate issued by the former institution.



申请流程（申请截至时间：6月15日/名额满）：

Application Procedure (Deadline: June 15th/When seats are full):


1.登录国际学生在线申请网站:https://lxsgl.hdu.edu.cn/

1.Log into the online application website for international students:https://lxsgl.hdu.edu.cn/


2.创建个人账户

2.Create a personal account


3.填写个人信息

3.Complete your personal information


4.上传所需材料

4.Upload required documents


5.提交在线申请

5.Submit your online application


6.查看审核结果
