海南医科大学简介

Brief Introduction of Hainan Medical University


海南医科大学座落在素有“椰城”之称的热带滨海城市——海南省省会海口市。其前身始于创立于1947年的海强医事技术学校和创立于1948年的私立海南大学医学院。现有城西（主校区）、桂林洋（新校区）和龙华等三个校区，占地1416.71亩。

学校已经形成了面向全国招生，涵盖全日制本科和硕博士研究生和留学生教育以及成人教育和继续教育的全方位、多层次的人才培养体系。现有全日制在校生15340人。其中，本科生12207人（含留学生633人）、博士硕士研究生1760人。

学校开设36个本科专业，涵盖医学、理学、管理学、工学等4个学科门类，拥有国家一流本科专业建设点5个、国家级特色专业2个、国家一流本科课程3门，国家实验教学示范中心、国家临床教学培训示范中心和国家临床（中医临床）教学培训示范中心各1个。学校拥有附属海南医院（海南省人民医院）、第一附属医院、第二附属医院等3所全省规模最大的三级甲等综合直属附属医院，总开放床位数7020张。此外，学校还拥有10家非直属附属医院、12家教学医院、2个合作共建疾控中心，为临床教学提供了有力支撑。拥有国家临床重点专科15个，省级重点学科7个。临床医学进入ESI学科排名前5‰，药理学与毒理学进入ESI学科排名前1%。现有临床医学一级学科博士学位授权点，基础医学、临床医学、药学、中医学、生物学等5个一级学科硕士学位授权点，临床医学、公共卫生、护理、药学、口腔医学等5个专业硕士学位授权点。

Hainan Medical University is located in Haikou, the capital city of Hainan Province, a tropical coastal city known as the "Coconut City". Its origins can be traced back to Haiqiang Medical Technical School founded in 1947 and the Medical College of Private Hainan University established in 1948. The university has three campuses: Chengxi (main campus), Guilin Yang (new campus) and Longhua, covering an area of 1,416.71 mu.


The university has developed a comprehensive and multi-level talent training system, recruiting students nationwide. It offers full-time undergraduate, postgraduate (master's and doctoral), international student programs, as well as adult and continuing education. There are 15,340 full-time students, including 12,207 undergraduates (633 international students) and 1,760 master's and doctoral students.

The university offers 36 undergraduate programs covering four disciplines: Medicine, Science, Management and Engineering. It boasts 5 national first-class undergraduate program construction sites, 2 national characteristic specialties, and 3 national first-class undergraduate courses. There is one National Experimental Teaching Demonstration Center, one National Clinical Teaching and Training Demonstration Center, and one National Clinical (Traditional Chinese Medicine Clinical) Teaching and Training Demonstration Center respectively. The university has three directly affiliated tertiary general hospitals with the largest scale in the province, namely Hainan Affiliated Hospital (Hainan General Hospital), the First Affiliated Hospital and the Second Affiliated Hospital, with a total of 7,020 open beds. In addition, it runs 10 non-directly affiliated hospitals, 12 teaching hospitals and 2 jointly built centers for disease control and prevention, providing strong support for clinical teaching. It has 15 national key clinical specialties and 7 provincial key disciplines. Clinical Medicine ranks among the top 5‰ in the ESI discipline rankings, and Pharmacology & Toxicology ranks among the top 1% in the ESI discipline rankings. The university is authorized to confer doctoral degrees in the first-level discipline of Clinical Medicine, and master's degrees in five first-level disciplines including Basic Medicine, Clinical Medicine, Pharmacy, Traditional Chinese Medicine and Biology. It also provides professional master's degree programs in five fields: Clinical Medicine, Public Health, Nursing, Pharmacy and Stomatology.

二、申请办法

Application Method

申请者应在海南医科大学规定的申请时间内登录海南医科大学国际学生申请服务系统https://ism.muhn.edu.cn/stu//进行在线申请，或登录海南医科大学国际教育学院网站https://www.muhn.edu.cn/gjxy/zsgl/xzzx.htm下载相关材料填写后，提交至邮箱：hnmu_enquire@muhn.edu.cn。

申请时间一般为每年的2月-7月底，建议申请者至少在7月中旬前完成申请。如需申请奖学金，请在5月20日前完成申请。

Applicants shall log on to the Hainan Medical University International Student Application Service System at https://ism.muhn.edu.cn/stu// to submit online applications within the application period specified by Hainan Medical University. Alternatively, applicants may visit the website of the School of International Education, Hainan Medical University at https://www.muhn.edu.cn/gjxy/zsgl/xzzx.htm, download and fill in relevant documents, and submit them to the email address: hnmu_enquire@muhn.edu.cn.

The application period is generally from February to the end of July each year. Applicants are advised to complete their applications no later than mid‑July. Those applying for scholarships must finish their applications before May 20.

III. Application Requirements and Document Checklist

请填写并提交以下申请材料

（一）本科学历生教育

1. 个人基本条件：年龄：18-35周岁（自费生可适当放宽）；持外国有效普通护照的非中国籍公民；遵守中国法律、法规及学校规章制度； 诚实守信；身体健康；

2. 个人陈述及学习计划；

3. 学历认证报告：原则上须提供经当地教育部门公证的高中及以上毕业文凭或同等学历证书；

4. 成绩单：高中毕业成绩单（须提交公证件）；

5. 语言能力成绩报告：申请者须根据所申请项目的要求提供相应等级的汉语水平考试（HSK）成绩报告（汉语授课专业要求HSK4及以上证书）；

6. 护照：申请者须提供个人因私普通护照的个人信息页（护照有效期须大于1年）。原为中国公民、后加入外国国籍者，还须提供：户籍注销证明和加入其他国家国籍证明；

7. 入学申请表：在海南医科大学国际教育学院官网下载《海南医科大学国际学生入学申请表》填写并提交；

8. 其他材料：个人体检报告；经公证的无犯罪记录证明；其他可证明自己综合能力的 材料可根据实际情况自行提交。

9.来华留学本科入学学业水平测试（CSCA）成绩单（数学为必考科目，选择化学、物理作为附加科目）。

10.《海南医科大学来华留学生经济担保函》及担保材料扫描件，证明个人财力或家庭资助、政府资助、经济担保人赞助等方面的财力来源。

Please complete and submit the following application materials

(1) Undergraduate Programs

1. Basic personal requirements: Age: 18-35 years old (the age limit may be appropriately relaxed for self-funded students); non-Chinese citizens holding a valid ordinary foreign passport; abiding by Chinese laws, regulations and university rules; being honest and trustworthy; in good physical health.

2. Personal statement and study plan.

3. Academic qualification verification report: In principle, notarized high school diploma or equivalent academic certificate issued by local education authorities is required.

4. Academic transcript: High school transcript (notarized copy is required).

5. Language proficiency score report: Applicants shall provide Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi (HSK) score report of the corresponding level as required by the applied program (programs taught in Chinese require HSK Level 4 or above certificate).

6. Passport: Applicants must provide the personal information page of their ordinary private passport (passport validity must be more than 1 year). Those who were originally Chinese citizens and later acquired foreign nationality shall also provide household registration cancellation certificate and proof of foreign nationality acquisition.

7. Admission application form: Download, fill in and submit the Hainan Medical University International Student Admission Application Form from the official website of the School of International Education, Hainan Medical University.

8. Other documents: Personal physical examination report; notarized certificate of no criminal record; other materials that can prove your comprehensive ability may be submitted at your discretion.

9. Score report of the Undergraduate Academic Proficiency Test for International Students Coming to China (CSCA) (Mathematics is a compulsory subject; Chemistry or Physics can be selected as an additional subject).

10. Scanned copy of the Financial Guarantee Letter for International Students of Hainan Medical University and supporting financial documents to prove sources of financial support such as personal funds, family sponsorship, government funding or financial guarantor sponsorship.

（三）注意事项

1. 除以上材料外，还需提供1张彩色标准证件照，大小不超200kb，支持*.jpg,*.jpeg,*-png为后缀的图片，像素大于295*4132；

2. 不论录取与否，提交的申请材料一律不予退还。本人承诺所提供的个人信息、证件及证明材料真实准确，一经招生单位或认证部门查证为不属实，将被取消申请、录取或学习资格；

3. 若您的证书材料语言非中英文，请您提交中文或英文公证件；

4. 我校及院系招生部门有权要求申请者提供申请材料的书面版原件或者指定认证机构出具的认证文件供进一步查验；

5. 提交申请材料的同时须提供申请人联系电话和电子邮箱；

6.对于1+5年制学生，一年后如获得HSK4级证书可申请进入临床医学本科专业学习；未能取得证书者，可选择离校或按照5年制学生入学标准要求执行。

7.对于5年制学生，入学时未取得HSK4级证书，但已具备相应的汉语语言能力，可申请参加我校组织的《国际汉语能力标准》水平考试，通过该考试获得入学资格的学生允许在临床医学专业试读一年，但必须在此期间获得HSK4级证书，并完成临床医学专业相应的学习。如仍未能取得证书，将取消试读资格。

8.特别提醒：我校毕业证与学位证专业名称为“clinical medicine”，申请我校临床医学1+5年制及5年制学生，申请前请自行与所在国家确认，毕业后是否具备相应执业资格。

(III) Notes

1. In addition to the above documents, one standard color passport-style photograph is required. The file size shall not exceed 200 KB. Supported formats include *.jpg, *.jpeg and *.png, with a pixel resolution greater than 295*4132.

2. All submitted application materials will not be returned regardless of admission results. The applicant undertakes that all personal information, certificates and supporting documents provided are true and accurate. If verified to be untrue by the admissions authority or certification body, the applicant’s application, admission or study eligibility will be revoked.

3. If your certificates and documents are issued in a language other than Chinese or English, you shall submit notarized documents in Chinese or English.

4. The university and its school admissions offices reserve the right to require applicants to provide hard-copy originals of application materials or certification documents issued by designated certification authorities for further verification.

5. Applicants must provide their contact phone number and email address when submitting application materials.

6. For students under the 1+5-year program, those who obtain the HSK Level 4 certificate after one year may apply to start undergraduate studies in Clinical Medicine. Applicants failing to obtain the certificate may choose to leave the university or follow the admission requirements for the 5-year program students.

7. For 5-year program students who do not hold an HSK Level 4 certificate upon admission but possess corresponding Chinese proficiency, they may apply to take the Standards for Chinese Language Proficiency examination organized by our university. Students who pass this examination to obtain admission eligibility are allowed to undertake a one-year probationary study in the Clinical Medicine program. They must obtain the HSK Level 4 certificate and complete the corresponding Clinical Medicine studies within this period. Failure to obtain the certificate will result in the cancellation of probationary eligibility.

8. Special Reminder: The major name printed on our university’s graduation certificate and degree certificate is "clinical medicine". Applicants for the 1+5-year and 5-year Clinical Medicine programs shall verify with the competent authorities in their home country in advance whether they can obtain corresponding professional practice qualifications after graduation.


四、申请与录取

IV. Application and Admission

（一）提交入学申请  

申请人将所有申请材料在申请截止日期前发送至学校指定邮箱 hnmu_enquire@muhn.edu.cn。

（二）申请及报到截止日期

2026年5月20日，海南省政府国际学生相关奖学金申请截止；

2026年 7 月 15 日，自费生申请截止；

2026年 9 月 15 日，报到截止。

（三）资格审查及考核

学校组织专家进行资格审查、入学考核，确定拟录取名单并发放录取通知书。

（四）发送通知书

学校确认学生学习意向后，将《录取通知书》和《外国留学人员来华签证申请表》（JW202 表）发送给申请人。

（五）特别注意

因故不能按期报到者，应提前向我校提交证明材料办理请假手续，请假时间最长不得超过 4 周，经批准后方可延期入学，否则取消入学资格。

(1) Submission of Admission Application

Applicants shall send all application documents to the designated school email address hnmu_enquire@muhn.edu.cn before the application deadline.

(2) Deadlines for Application and Registration

May 20, 2026: Deadline for applications for Hainan Government Scholarships for international students;

July 15, 2026: Deadline for self-funded student applications;

September 15, 2026: Deadline for registration.


(3) Eligibility Review and Assessment

The university will organize experts to conduct eligibility review and admission assessment, determine the proposed admission list and issue admission notices.

(4) Issuance of Admission Documents

After confirming the applicant’s intention to study, the university will send the Admission Notice and Visa Application Form for Foreign Students to China (Form JW202) to the applicant.

(5) Special Notes

Those who cannot register on schedule due to special reasons shall submit supporting documents to our university in advance to apply for leave. The maximum leave period shall not exceed 4 weeks. Deferred admission is permitted only upon approval; otherwise, the admission qualification will be revoked.

六、奖学金申请

海南医科大学提供的奖学金项目为海南省政府国际学生奖学金和东盟奖学金，本科生资助期限为专业学制年限4-6年；硕士研究生资助期限为3年；资助内容为学费、住宿费和医疗保险费，其它费用自理。

（一）奖学金生申请条件

1. 具备我校国际学生学历生项目申请资格；

2. 未获得中国政府奖学金或其他各类奖学金。

（二）申请材料

1. 填写《海南省政府国际学生奖学金生申请表》，可登录海南医科大学国际教育学院网站http://www.hainmc.edu.cn/sole/ 下载填写后，提交至邮箱：hnmu_enquire@muhn.edu.cn；

2. 我校国际学生学历生项目的所有申请材料。

特别提示：奖学金获得者入学后须按规定参加奖学金年度评审。评审不合格者，将被中止或者取消其享受奖学金的资格。

VI. Scholarship Application

Hainan Medical University offers two scholarship programs: Hainan Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students and ASEAN Scholarship. The funding duration for undergraduate students ranges from 4 to 6 years, corresponding to the length of their academic program; for master’s students, the funding duration is 3 years. The scholarship covers tuition fees, accommodation fees and medical insurance premiums, while all other expenses shall be borne by the students themselves.


Eligibility Criteria for Scholarship Applicants

- Be qualified to apply for the university’s degree‑seeking international student programs;

- Not be recipients of the Chinese Government Scholarship or any other types of scholarships.


Application Documents

- Complete the Application Form for Hainan Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students. The form can be downloaded from the website of the School of International Education, Hainan Medical University at http://www.hainmc.edu.cn/sole/. Fill it out and submit it via email to hnmu_enquire@muhn.edu.cn.

- All application documents required for the university’s degree‑seeking international student programs.


Special Reminder: Scholarship recipients must participate in the annual scholarship review after enrolment in accordance with relevant regulations. Those who fail the review shall have their scholarship eligibility suspended or revoked.
