2026 Fall Semester Chinese Language Training Program (Non-Degree) for International Students at Guizhou Normal University
Guizhou Normal University (GZNU), established in 1941, has over 80 years of history in academic excellence. In 2015, it became a co-constructed university by the Ministry of Education and the People’s Government of Guizhou Province. The university offers a comprehensive range of disciplines including Philosophy, Literature, Education, Engineering, and Science, across 12 academic categories. Currently, GZNU hosts 10 primary discipline doctoral programs, 25 primary discipline master’s programs, and 25 professional master’s degree programs. It comprises 28 colleges with nearly 35,000 full-time students.
The School of International Education at GZNU offers year-round international Chinese language classes. Known for its high-quality pedagogical system and diverse cultural activities, it provides an excellent platform for international students to master the Chinese language and gain a profound understanding of Chinese culture. With decades of development, this program has built an outstanding reputation in the field of international education.
At present,
GZNU has launched various Chinese language courses for the Fall Semester of 2026, including beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels, tailored to meet the diverse needs of students. We warmly welcome students to apply and begin a rewarding language learning journey at Guizhou Normal University
.
I. Eligibility, Application Period, and Enrollment Capacity
1. Eligibility: Non-Chinese citizens aged 18 to 50, in good physical and mental health.
2. Conduct: Applicants must be of good character with no criminal record or history of misconduct.
3. Application Deadline: Applications must be submitted by May 30, 2026. Students may apply for either one semester or one academic year.
4. Enrollment Quota: 100 students (admission is based on merit).
II. Program Highlights
1. Small Class Sizes.
2. Diverse Course Types: Face-to-face instruction by an elite team of Chinese language teachers aimed at improving listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills.
3. Certification: Upon completion of the program, the School will issue official transcripts and certificates of completion.
4. Enrollment Quota: 100 students (admission is based on merit).
