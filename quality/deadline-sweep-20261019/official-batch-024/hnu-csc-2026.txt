Chinese Government Scholarship Application Notices for 
High-level Postgraduate Program of Hunan University  
in 2026 
1. Majors Offered 
 The Scholarship of CSC for High-level Postgraduate Program is 
oriented to the students from all countries. 
a. The majors taught in Chinese can be found in the website of 
Graduate School of Hunan University http://gra.hnu.edu.cn/index.htm . 
b. The majors taught both in English and Chinese are as follows 
--Business Administration (Master or Ph.D) 
--Computer Science and Technology (Master or Ph.D) 
--Applied Economics (Master or Ph.D) 
--Chemistry (Ph.D) 
--Mechanical Engineering (Ph.D) 
2. Scholarship Coverage  
a. Tuition fee. 
b. Free university dormitory or accommodation subsidy. 
c. Stipend: 
      Master’s students/general scholars: CNY 3,000 per month; 
     Doctoral students/senior scholars: CNY 3,500 per month; 
d. Comprehensive Medical Insurance: 
  CNY 800 per person per year. 
3. Eligibility 
a. Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens and in good health both 
mentally and physically. 
b. Applicants should be an academically excellent holder of a 
Bachelor’s degree or its equivalent under the age of 35 when applying for a 
Master’s program. 
c. Applicants should be an academically excellent holder of a Master’s 
degree or its equivalent under the age of 40 when applying for a doctoral 
program. 
d. Applicants should have passed the HSK (Level 4) or above when 
applying for a Chinese-taught postgraduate program.

e. Applicant should also meet the admission requirements of Hunan 
University in terms of language proficiency, academic ability and other 
relevant aspects. 
4. Application Process 
a. Applicants shall learn about Hunan University by visiting websites 
(https://www.hnu.edu.cn/) and learn about the majors offered by Hunan 
University by visiting websites (http://www.campuschina.org).  
b. Applicants should visit “CSC Study in China” website 
(http://www.campuschina.org), click “Scholarship Application for Students”, 
register an account through “Create An Account” and log in with your 
account. 
c. Applicants should click “Edit Personal Details” to input personal 
details and fill in the application information (please select Program 
Category “Type B” and input the Agency Number “10532”). 
d. Applicants should upload “Supporting Documents” as requested, 
then click “Submit” to complete the application. 
e. Hunan University will contact the applicants and issue 
“Pre-admission” to the applicants who achieve the required standard in the 
preliminary verification. 
f. Applicants should upload the “Pre-admission” to the application 
system and click “Submit” again. 
g. Hunan University shall submit the recommended candidates list and 
their application documents to the CSC. 
h.The CSC will organize experts concerned to examine applicants’ 
eligibility and academic performance before finalizing the short-list 
candidates; 
i. Hunan University will inform the applicants who finally get admitted 
with the Scholarship by email or by phone. 
j. Hunan University will collect the information including contact 
person, detailed post address, contact number etc from the applicants by 
email for delivering the admission documents.  
5. Application Documents 
The applicants must fill in the form and provide the following 
documents truly, correctly and completely.

a. Application Form for Chinese Government Scholarship （filled in 
Chinese or English）: The website of “CSC Study in China”, also called as 
Online Application System, is http://www.campuschina.org , Program  
Category is “Type B” ， Hunan University Agency Number is 10532. 
Applicants should fill in the application form, submit it online and print it. 
b. Passport copy. The period of validity of your passport should be 
expired after September of 2026.  
c. Highest diploma (notarized photocopy): Prospective diploma 
winners must submit official proof of student status by the current school. 
Documents in languages other than Chinese or English must be attached 
with notarized translations in Chinese or English.  
d. Academic transcripts (notarized photocopy): The transcripts should 
be offered from the undergraduate program onwards. Transcripts in 
languages other than Chinese or English must be attached with notarized 
translations in Chinese or English. 
e. Language Requirements  
For Chinese-taught Programs:  
-- Certificate of HSK 4 or above 
For English-taught Programs:  
--English as mother Tongue;  
--English learning transcripts with recognition of Hunan University 
such as IELTS 6.0 Or TOEFL 80 or others; 
f. A study plan: All applicants are required to submit a study or 
research plan no less than 1000 words and must be in Chinese or in English.     
g. Recommendation letters: Applicants for postgraduate studies, or 
study in China as senior scholars must submit two letters of 
recommendation written only in Chinese or English and signed by 
professors or associate professors.  
h. Works: The applicant applying for the arts or design program shall 
provide his/her own works (such as videos and paintings); 
i. Photocopy of Foreigner Physical Examination Form (the original 
form should be kept by the applicant): The form printed by Chinese 
quarantine authority could be downloaded from http://www.csc.edu.cn  
The form should be filled in English. The medical examinations must cover 
all the items listed in the Foreigner Physical Examination Form. Incomplete 
records or those without the signature of the attending physician, the

6. Notes 
1. Once the documents you submit are found to be false or fake, your 
application or your admission will be canceled immediately. 
2. The applicant granted a CSC scholarship is not allowed to 
simultaneously take another scholarship sponsored by the local 
government at all levels, the universities or other authorities of China. 
Once the cheat is found, the CSC scholarship of the applicant will be 
canceled immediately and the stipends already received by the applicant 
must be returned. The applicant who deliberately hides the fact is also not 
allowed to apply CSC scholarship again in the next three years.
