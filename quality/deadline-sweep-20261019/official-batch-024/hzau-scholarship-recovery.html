SCHOLARSHIPS

Overview

Huazhong Agricultural University Scholarship is established by Huazhong Agricultural University (HZAU) to encourage and support some excellent foreign students to study at HZAU as Master’s or Doctoral students. It has two types: full scholarship and partial scholarship.
