湖南工商大学2026年来华留学生招生简章 Enrollment Guide for International Students to Study at Hunan University of Technology and Business in 2026

一、学校简介

湖南工商大学拥有经济学、管理学、工学、理学、法学、文学、艺术学、教育学、交叉学科等多个学科门类，是博士学位授予单位、推荐优秀应届本科毕业生免试攻读研究生资格高校、湖南省原本科一批招生高校、教育部本科教学工作水平评估优秀高校、全国首批百强“深化创新创业教育改革示范高校”，享有“经济湘军基地，企业名家摇篮”的盛誉。

学校现有在校研究生和本科生28000余人，近五年获全国“挑战杯”大赛特等奖、“累进创新金奖”、中国国际大学生创新大赛银奖、全国大学生职业规划大赛金奖等国家级奖励1200余项，获奖数跻身2019-2023年全国人文社科类本科院校大学生竞赛排行榜的前十。为国家和区域经济社会发展累计输送了20余万名优秀人才。

学校具备招收来华留学生资格，与美国、英国、澳大利亚、韩国等国家和港澳台地区高校广泛合作。现有4个中外合作办学本科专业，实施“跨学科专业、跨理论实践、跨学院学校、跨国家地区”的国际化人才培养模式。

湖南工商大学全体师生正秉承“至诚至信、为实为新”的校训精神，弘扬“厚德、博学、笃行、自强”的优良校风，凝心聚力、踔厉奋发，努力将学校建设成特色鲜明的高水平工商大学！

I. Introduction to HUTB

Hunan University of Technology and Business (HUTB) offers a wide range of disciplines, including economics, management, engineering, science, law, literature, arts, education, and interdisciplinary studies, is a doctoral-degree-conferring institution, has the qualification to recommend outstanding undergraduates for admission to postgraduate programs without entrance examinations, and is among the first-batch groups to enroll students in the province. It was previously among the first-batch undergraduate enrollment universities in Hunan Province and was rated “Excellent” in the Evaluation of Teaching Quality in Undergraduate Programs organized by the Ministry of Education. It is also one of the nation’s first-batch top 100 “Model Universities for Deepening Innovation and Entrepreneurship Education Reform”, and enjoys the prestigious title of “the Base of Hunan’s Economic Forces and the Cradle of Distinguished Entrepreneurs”.

HUTB currently has more than 28,000 graduate and undergraduate students. Over the past five years, its students have won over 1,200 national-level awards, including the Grand Prize and the "Progressive Innovation Gold Award" at the National “Challenge Cup” Competition, the Silver Award of the China International College Students’ Innovation Competition, and the Gold Award of the National College Students Career Planning Competition. The University ranks among the top 10 in the 2019–2023 National Competition Rankings for Undergraduate Institutions in Humanities and Social Sciences. HUTB has cumulatively delivered more than 200,000 outstanding talents for national and regional economic and social development.

HUTB is qualified to enroll international students and maintains extensive cooperation with universities in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, South Korea, as well as the Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan regions. The University currently offers 4 undergraduate programs in cooperation with foreign institutions and implements an international talent cultivation model characterized by interdisciplinary programs, the integration of theory and practice, inter-college and inter-university collaboration, and cross-national and cross-regional cooperation.

Guided by the motto of “Sincerity and Credibility; Pragmatism and Innovation”, all HUTB faculty and students carry forward the school spirit of “Virtue; Erudition; Practice; Self-enhancement”, remain united and forge ahead with determination, striving to build HUTB into a high-level university of technology and business with distinctive characteristics.

三、申请条件

（一）申请资格

1. 截至2026年6月1日年龄在18周岁（含）以上且身心健康，品行端正的非中国籍公民。

2. 遵守中国法律和学校规章制度，尊重中国风俗习惯，学习成绩优异。

（二）申请资料

1. 湖南工商大学留学生申请表

2. 彩色2寸免冠证件照

3. 有效普通护照（护照剩余有效期须不少于12个月）

4. 《外国人体格检查表》(半年有效期内)

5. 无犯罪记录证明（半年有效期内；若为中英文以外文本，需附上经公证的中文或英文译本）

6. 最高学历证书

7. 学习成绩单（申请攻读硕士提供）

8. 语言水平证明（申请攻读硕士提供）

9. 来华学习和研究计划（申请攻读硕士提供）

10. 两名教授或副教授的推荐信（申请攻读硕士提供）

11.经济条件证明（提供足以支付在中国留学的学费、住宿费、生活费等担保证明，同时提交经费担保人的工作或收入证明及护照复印件）

12.目前在华或曾在华工作、学习等申请者，需提供相应的证明资料。如有其它可以证明申请者学业能力和综合素质的文件，可在申请时一并提交。

（三）语言要求

官方语言非英语的学生，申请攻读硕士生，须提供雅思5.5分及以上，或托福50分及以上，或同等英语水平证书，或高中阶段英语授课证明。

（四）其他

我校将对初审合格的申请者进行面试或专业复试，面试或专业复试不合格者，不予录取。

III. Application Requirements

(I) Application Qualification

1. Non-Chinese citizens who are over 18 years old as of June 1, 2025, sound in body and mind, and well-conducted;

2. Non-Chinese citizens who abide by the laws of the Chinese government and rules & regulations of HUTB, respect Chinese customs and habits, and are excellent for academic performance.

(II) Application Materials

1. Application form for international students studying at HUTB

2. Bareheaded photo (3.5 cm × 5.3 cm, color)

3. Valid ordinary passport (its remaining validity period shall be no less than 12 months)

4. Physical examination record (within the validity period of half a year)

5. Certificate of no criminal record (within the validity period of half a year, and with notarized Chinese/English translations for texts other than Chinese/English)

6. Highest diploma

7. Academic transcripts (for postgraduate)

8. English proficiency certificate (for postgraduate)

9. Study Plan at HUTB (for postgraduate)

10. Recommendation letters from two professors or associate professors (for postgraduate)

11. Proof of economic conditions (guarantee certificates to pay tuition, accommodation and living expenses for studying at HUTB, and the work or income certificate of the financial guarantor and a copy of passport)

12. Student who is currently working or studying or has ever worked or studied in China shall provide corresponding supporting materials. In addition, other documents that can prove the academic ability and comprehensive quality of the applicant may be submitted.

(III) English Requirements

Non-English-speaking students for postgraduate shall provide IELTS (score at 5.5 or above) or TOEFL (score at 50 or above) or equivalent English proﬁciency certiﬁcates or high school English learning certiﬁcate.

(IV) Others

HUTB will conduct an interview or a professional reexamination for those who pass the preliminary examination. Those who fail the interview or professional reexamination will not be admitted.

六、奖学金

1.湖南省来华留学生奖学金

湖南省政府为来华学习留学生提供奖学金，每年若干名优秀学生。

2.湖南省“一带一路”沿线国家语言生奖学金

湖南省政府为“一带一路”沿线国家的语言进修生提供一学年奖学金。

3.湖南工商大学来华留学生奖学金

湖南工商大学为在校优秀留学生提供奖学金。根据每位留学生在校表现，语言生、硕士研究生可分别获得最高5500、16800元奖学金。

VI. Scholarship

1. Scholarship for international students studying in Hunan Province

Hunan Provincial Government provides scholarship for several outstanding students per year.

2. Scholarship for Chinese language learning students from the countries along “The Belt and Road”

Hunan Provincial Government provides scholarship of an academic year for outstanding students from the countries along “The Belt and Road”.

3. HUTB International Students Inclusive Scholarship

HUTB provides scholarship for outstanding international students according to the performance of each student. Chinese language learning students and postgraduates may receive scholarships of up to RMB 5,500 and RMB 16,800 respectively.
