 2025 Guidelines to Application for Self-financed 
 Programs of Hunan University 
I. Majors 
1. Chinese-taught programs 
Hunan University (HNU) offers the same Chinese-taught programs for 
international students as it does for Chinese students. Please visit the official  
website of HNU: https://www.hnu.edu.cn  
2. English-taught programs 
Master degree programs: Business Administration, Computer Science and  
Technology, Applied Economics. 
PhD programs: Business Administration, Computer Science and Technology, 
Applied Economics, Chemistry, Mechanical Engineering.  
3. Chinese Language Courses 
Chinese Language courses: Applicants may independently choose to s tudy  
Chinese language  courses for one semester or one academic year in China.  
II. Requirements for application 
1. Educational background 
Undergraduate programs: Applicants must have a high school diploma or 
equivalent prior to formal enrollment. 
Master degree programs: Applicants must have obtained a valid  
bachelor's degree prior to formal enrollment. 
PhD programs: Applicants must have obtained a valid master's degree  
prior to formal enrollment. 
2. Age 
Undergraduate Program: We only accept applicants who are between  
18 and 30 years old at the time of formal enrollment. 
Master degree programs: The age of applicants for Master degree  
program must be under 35 at the time of formal enrollment. 
PhD Program: The age of applicants for Ph.D program must be under  
40 at the time of formal enrollment. 
3. Nationality 
This program is ONLY open to the citizen of a country other than China and in 
good health both mentally and physically.

III. Application Materials 
1. Passport 
- A scanned copy of the Passport Photo Page.  
-Applicants who are in China now should submit a clear scanned copy of the 
visa page of his/her passport; 
2. Physical Examination Report 
- Foreigner Physical Examination Form shall be filled in in English or Chinese.  
- The medical examinations must cover all the items listed in the Foreigner 
Physical Examination Form. 
- Incomplete records or those without the signature of the attending physician, 
the official stamp of the hospital, or a sealed photograph of the applicant are 
invalid.  
- Physical examination report are valid for only six months; 
3. Non-Criminal Record 
-An applicant shall submit a valid Certificate of Non-Criminal Record (or 
Police Clearance Certificate) issued by the local public security (such as the 
police station), usually issued within six months prior to the submission date of 
the application. 
4. Educational Certificates 
- Diploma and certificate of highest education (notarized photocopy). 
5. Academic Transcript 
- A scanned copy of the transcript of the previous academic records, which 
must be stamped by the school and notarized by a notary organization. 
6. Study Or Research Plan 
- An applicants for a undergraduate program is not required to provide a 
study or research plan; 
- An applicants for a master degree program or above shall submit a study or 
research plan written in Chinese or in English (no less than 800 
words/characters for scholars; no less than 1500 words/characters for 
postgraduates); 
7. Recommendation letters 
- An applicants for a undergraduate program is not required to provide a

IV. Application Process 
1. Online Application 
The online application website for international students of Hunan University 
Is https://hnu.17gz.org/  
Please submit the application and upload the required documents to this 
website, ensuring that the documents are clear and complete. 
2. Interview Or Written Test 
You may be invited to an interview or written tests from the school where the 
program you are applying for. Please pay attention to e-mail in time to ensure 
that you do not miss any important interviews or written tests. 
3. Decision Outcome 
Decision outcome will be completed before the end of July, please confirm by
