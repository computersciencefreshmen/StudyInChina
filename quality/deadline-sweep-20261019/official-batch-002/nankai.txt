International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship of Nankai University (2026)
To meet the growing international demand for Chinese language teachers, facilitate Chinese language education around the world, and support the professional development of Chinese language teachers, the Center for Language Education and Cooperation (CLEC) has established the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship (hereinafter referred to as the Scholarship) for eligible Chinese language teachers outside of China. Confucius Institutes, independent Confucius Classrooms, selected HSK test centers, foreign educational institutions, Chinese language teacher training programs/Chinese departments at foreign universities, professional associations for Chinese language teaching, Chinese embassies (consulates) abroad (hereinafter collectively referred to as Recommending Institutions) may recommend outstanding students and in-service Chinese language teachers to further their studies in international Chinese language education and related programs at Nankai University.
01 ELIGIBILITY
a) Be a non-Chinese citizen
b) Be friendly to China, have no criminal record, abide by the laws and regulations of China and the rules and regulations of the Host Institution;
c) Be in good physical and mental health, be in good academic and behavioral standing;
d) Be interested in Chinese language education and related work;
e) Be aged between 16 and 35 years (up to September 1, 2026). The maximum age limit for in-service Chinese teachers may be extended to 45, but those who apply for a scholarship for a Bachelor’s degree shall be younger than 25 under most circumstances.

03 APPLICATION PROCEDURES
a) Firstly, Please register at (
https://nankai.at0086.cn/StuApplication/Login.aspx
) to complete the online application of Nankai University and make the application fee payment as well. Application Fee is listed as follows: Non-degree program:  CNY 400；
The application fee policy for the degree students is subject to the admission brochure of the relevant year.
b)Then Online registration will be available from March 1, 2026 on the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship website of the Center for Language Education and Cooperation (
http://www.chinese.cn
). You may search for Recommending Institutions and Host Institutions(Nankai Univeristy); submit application materials online.
04 REVIEW AND ADMISSION
a. Applications submitted with all the information correct and completed will be evaluated by Nankai University. And those who passed application review will be entered into the integrative assessment. The assessment method, and interview date will be informed in advance. Applicants who pass both the preliminary review and the interview will be admitted.
b. Nankai University will nominate qualified applicants as scholarship candidate of International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship. Center for Language Education and Cooperation (CLEC) will organize expert panel to review all the candidates, and those who pass the review will be awarded the scholarship.
c. CLEC will announce the final results in June.
d. Scholarship recipients need to contact Nankai University regarding the enrollment, and print out the scholarship certificate online (
http://cis.chinese.cn
).
e. Nankai University will issue the admission notice and visa materials. Scholarship awardees must register at Nankai University at the time specified in the admission notice.
05 FUNDING
a)International Chinese Teacher Scholarship awardees will be waived of tuition fees, on- campus accommodation fees, and will be provided with monthly living allowance (four-week Chinese language training course (non-degree) is not included), and comprehensive medical insurance.
b)Accommodation is arranged by the university and is usually in a two-bedroom apartment.
c)Allowances are given on a monthly basis. For undergraduate students, one-year Chinese Language Training course (non-degree) and one-semester Chinese Language Training course (non-degree), the living allowance is CNY2,500/month; for postgraduate students(masters), the living allowance is CNY 3,000/month；
for doctoral students in International Chinese Language Education, the living allowance is CNY 3,500/month.
d)The comprehensive medical insurance will be purchased by the university with reference to the relevant regulations of the Chinese Ministry of Education on studying in China.
06 POLICIES REGARDING IN-SERVICE CHINESE LANGUAGE TEACHERS ANDCHINESE BRIDGE WINNERS
a)In-Service Chinese Language Teachers
In-service Chinese language teachers may be exempted from HSK/HSKK certificates if they provide proof of employment and a letter of recommendation from the employer.
b)Chinese Bridge Winners
Applicants who have received the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship Certificate during Chinese Bridge competitions may submit their application documents along with their scholarship certificates to the Host Institutions directly at the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship website.
For any further inquiries, please contact chinesebridge@chinese.cn.
07 OTHERS
1.For relevant professional training plans and teaching courses, please consult the target receiving institution.
2.Applicants should understand the specific admission requirements and application deadlines of the target receiving institution and submit application materials in accordance with relevant regulations.
3. Degree students are required to participate in the annual review. Please refer to the "Annual Review Measures for the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship".
4. For matters related to enrollment, please contact the School of International Education of Nankai University in a timely manner.
5. Scholarship eligibility will be revoked in cases such as failure in the entrance physical examination, withdrawal from school midway, failure to register on time without permission, and suspension of studies.
