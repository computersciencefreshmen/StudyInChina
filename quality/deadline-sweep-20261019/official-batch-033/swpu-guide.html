Application Guide for International Students Programs, Southwest Petroleum University

一、学校简介 About us

西南石油大学是新中国创建的第二所石油本科院校，是一所中央与地方共建、以四川省人民政府管理为主的高等学校。2013年，学校入选“国家中西部高校基础能力建设工程”，成为入选该工程的100所高校之一。石油与天然气工程学科连续两轮入选国家一流学科，全国学科评估连续获评A+。我校石油与天然气工程学科形成“油气并举、气为特色”并在全国乃至全球学科覆盖最广、研究方向最完整的学科格局，石油与天然气工程及其配套学科水平较高，工程学进入ESI排名世界前1‰行列，化学、材料学、地球科学、环境/生态学进入ESI排名世界前1%行列。

Southwest Petroleum University (SWPU), founded in 1958, is the second petroleum university since the establishment of P.R. China. It is jointly built by the central and local governments, with the Sichuan Provincial Government as the main administrator. In 2013, SWPU was selected into the “National Central and Western Universities Basic Capacity Construction Project” and became one of the 100 universities in the project. The discipline of Petroleum and Natural Gas Engineering has been selected as the national first-class discipline for two consecutive rounds, and has been rated as A+ discipline in the national discipline assessment. Now it has formed the discipline development pattern of “co-developing oil and gas, with gas being featured”, making the broadest discipline coverage and the most complete research directions across China and even around the world. Engineering has entered the top 1‰ of ESI rankings in the world, while Chemistry, Materials Science, Geo-science and Environmental/Ecology are among the top 1% of ESI rankings in the world.

经过60多年的建设，学校现已发展成为一所以工为主、多学科协调发展、石油天然气优势突出和特色鲜明的大学，石油主干学科专业在全国处于领先水平。

After more than 60 years of development, SWPU has evolved into a multi-disciplinary university focusing on engineering, with advantage in oil and gas discipline that ranks the top in China.

学校现有15个教学学院、1个工程训练中心。学科专业涵盖理学、工学、管理学、经济学、文学、法学、教育学、艺术学8个学科门类。本科具有招生资格专业74个，一级学科博士学位授权学科7个、专业学位博士点1个，一级学科硕士学位授权学科24个、硕士专业学位授权类别15个，自主设置能源类交叉学科博士学位授权点2个、硕士学位授权点4个。

SWPU consists of 15 schools and 1 engineering training center, covering 8 discipline fields: Science, Engineering, Management, Economics, Literature, Law, Education and Art. There are 74 undergraduate majors eligible for enrollment, 7 first-level disciplines with doctoral degree authorization, 1 professional degree doctoral program, 24 first-level disciplines with master's degree authorization, 15 categories of master's professional degree authorization, and 2 self-established interdisciplinary doctoral degree authorization points and 4 master's degree authorization points in the field of energy.

学校现有全日制在校学生41449人，其中普通本科生31413人，硕士研究生8486人，博士研究生1033人，外国留学生510人。现有教职工2753人，有正高级专业技术职务人员382人，副高级专业技术职务人员721人。其中，中国工程院院士1人，德国工程院院士1人。

Currently, there are 41,449 full-time students, including 31,413 undergraduates, 8,486 master students, 1,033 doctoral students and 510 international students. There are 2,753 faculty and staff members, among whom 382 hold senior professional titles and 721 hold associate senior professional titles. There is 1 academicians of the Chinese Academy of Engineering and 1 academicians of the German Academy of Engineering.

学校重视国际交流与合作，已与美、俄、英、德、日、法、加等10多个国家和地区的高等院校和相关学术研究机构建立了密切的交流与合作关系。

SWPU has always attached great importance to international exchange and cooperation and has established close relationships with higher education institutions and relevant academic research institutions in more than 10 countries and regions such as the United States, Russia, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, France, Canada, etc.

七、申请及录取流程Application and Admission Procedure

第一步：网上申请

申请者登录西南石油大学国际学生在线服务系统（https://swpu.17gz.org/），注册账号、在线填报、提交申请。

Step 1 Online Application

Applicants should log in to the Online Service System for International Students of Southwest Petroleum University at https://swpu.17gz.org/ to register an account, fill in the online forms and submit the application.

第二步：审核材料 确认录取

收到学生申请后，西南石油大学将审核申请者材料，确认录取名单，将办理《录取通知书》和《外国留学人员来华确认表》（JW201/202表）。

Step 2 Documents reviewing and admission confirmation

After reviewing of application materials in accordance with the provisions of the university, successful applicants will be issued Admission Notice and Confirmation Form for Study in China (the JW201/202 Form).

第三步：申请来华学习签证

被录取学生凭学校出具的电子版《录取通知书》到中国驻外签证机关申请学习类签证。

Step 3 Application for Study Visa to China

Successful applicants can apply for a study visa at the Chinese visa agencies stationed abroad with the electronic version of the Admission Notice issued by the university.

注：中国政府奖学金申请及录取详见具体通知。（https://www.swpu.edu.cn/oice/info/1233/3690.htm）

Note: For the application and admission of Chinese Government Scholarship, please refer to the specific notice.
