Shanghai University of Political Science and Law Evaluation Regulation of Foreign Students “One Belt and One Road”Scholarship(Amendment)

Publisher：王罗超Release time：2025-12-01Browse times：2934


       Shanghai University of Political Science and Law “Belt and Road” Scholarship is a university-level scholarship specifically established to fully implement the national policy on international students in China and the Study in China Program, encouraging and attracting more outstanding international students to study at our university.

Application Website:  https://admission.shupl.edu.cn/studentLogin


I. Eligible Applicants


Applicants must be foreign nationals in good health.



II. Application Period: November of each year to May 30 of the following year



III. Duration of Support


Master's: 2-3 years


Undergraduate: 4 years


Non-degree Language Training Students: 1 year



IV. Categories and Coverage


1. Scholarships are categorized into Type A, Type B, and Type C.


2. Coverage:


Type A: Covers tuition and accommodation fees

Type B: Covers tuition fees

Type C: Covers 10 months of living expenses (RMB 1,000/month)


Note: Categories A and B are only available to degree-seeking students. Category C is only available to non-degree students.



 V. Application Requirements


1. Age and Academic Qualifications


(1) Applicants for undergraduate programs must hold a high school diploma or equivalent and be no older than 25 years of age.


(2) Applicants for master's programs must hold a bachelor's degree or equivalent and be no older than 35 years of age.


2. Language Proficiency


(1) Undergraduate applicants must achieve at least HSK Level 4 with a score of 180 or higher, and provide corresponding certification;


(2) Master's applicants must achieve at least HSK Level 5 with a score of 180 or higher, and provide corresponding certification;


Note: Applicants must not be receiving funding from other scholarships.
