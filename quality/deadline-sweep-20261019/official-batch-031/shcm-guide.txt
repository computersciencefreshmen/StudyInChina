I. Applicant Eligibility
1) Applicants should be non-Chinese citizens with a valid foreign passport.
Chinese citizens with only a permanent resident card from another country
shall not be deemed eligible international candidates.
2) Applicants for Master’s programs (hereinafter referred to as Master’s
candidates) are required to hold a degree equivalent to a Bachelor’s Degree
in China. Applicants for Doctoral programs (hereinafter referred to as
Doctoral candidates) should have a degree equivalent to a Master’s Degree
in China.
Please note that Master’s and Doctoral programs do not accept applications
from those who do not meet the above-mentioned degree requirements.
3) Applicants must be at least 18 years old, possess good moral character, and
be mentally and physically healthy. They must have a clean criminal record
and be willing to comply with the laws of the People’s Republic of China
and the rules and regulations of the SHCM.
4) Applicants for Doctoral programs should submit two letters of
recommendation from professors (or experts with equivalent titles)
specialized in the field they are applying for. Applicants for Master’s
programs need to provide two letters of recommendation from associate
professors (or experts with equivalent or higher titles) specialized in the field
they are applying for.
5) Applicants for Doctoral programs must obtain a New HSK Level 5
Certificate with a score of 180 points or above.
Applicants for Master’s programs must obtain a New HSK Level 4
Certificate with a score of at least 210 points.
However, those who have completed a degree program taught in Chinese at a
Chinese university may present a stamped certificate issued by their
institution instead of the HSK certificate.

III. Application Procedure
The application process consists of two steps: submitting application materials online
and paying the application fee. The application will be considered valid only when
both steps are completed.
 Note: Each applicant can apply for only one major; applications for a second
major will not be accepted.
1) Online Submission of Application Materials
Eligible applicants should register and upload scanned copies of all required
documents through the “外籍生 /International Applicants” module of the
SHCM Graduate Program Admission System (https://yjszs.shcmusic.edu.cn)
between January 4 and January 23 , 202 6. Documents in languages other than
Chinese and English must be accompanied with Chinese or English
translations.
★ List of Application Materials
a) An application statement that includes both study and work experience, with
a maximum length of 1,000 Chinese characters.
b) A scanned copy of the passport information page along with all pages that
contain entry/exit of China stamps. The passport should be valid until
September 2026 or later. If the validity of the applicant’s current passport
does not meet this requirement (meaning the passport expires before
September 2026), please apply for a new passport before submitting the
application.
c) A scanned copy of the Foreigner Physical Examination Form (See the
attachment). The result of the physical examination is valid for 6 months.
d) A valid Chinese Proficiency Test (HSK) Certificate or a certificate proving
completion of a Chinese-taught degree program. This item should be
submitted no later than May 31, 2026.
e) A recently-taken colored passport-size photo (full face, front view, bare head)
in JPG format. Ensure it is clear and undamaged.
f) A scanned copy of the original degree or diploma in your previous stage of
study (student ID card or student status certificate for upcoming graduates).
g) A scanned copy of your academic transcripts from undergraduate programs
onwards.
h) Dissertations, theses, graduation projects, or recordings of your graduation
concerts (upcoming graduates don’t need to provide all of these).
i) Comprehensive evaluation materials (see Appendix III for further
information).
j) Two letters of recommendation (See the attachment).

IV. Entrance Exam
1) The exam will consist of two rounds: the preliminary round and the second
round. Both rounds are scheduled to take place in mid-March 2026.
2) The preliminary round will involve a comprehensive review of the
application materials. Please refer to Appendix III for detailed information
on the comprehensive evaluation materials.
3) The second round will be conducted as an online interview. For detailed
information on the examination requirements, please refer to Appendix III.
Any further updates regarding the exam will be posted on the website of the
SHCM Division of the Graduate Study Programs.
V. Admission and Enrollment
Admission decisions will be made on the basis of competitive selection according
to the candidate’s application materials, exam results, review of their moral
character, and physical examination results.
Admitted applicants will receive an Admission Notice. Please strictly observe the
requirements provided therein. Health and eligibility verification will be
conducted after your registration at the SHCM in the upcoming semester. Those
who fail to meet the admission requirements will be disqualified.

Note on Basic Materials
1) Items listed under Basic Materials are essential. Applicants must submit all these
materials to be considered for the application.
2) The same paper or music work cannot be submitted twice as both the basic
material and academic achievement.
Note on Art Achievements
1) Academic achievements may include published papers, publications, audio-visual
products, and research projects, among others. Art practice achievements include
prizes and awards from professional competitions, participation in major artistic
events, and original compositions that have been publicly performed.
2) You may present a maximum of five items for each category, meaning that you
can list up to five academic achievements and up to five art practice
achievements, with scanned copies of relevant certificates or proof documents
attached.
II. Second Round
1301 Art Theory
The second-round exam for students majoring in Art Theory and related
specializations will take the form of an interview. Candidates are required to
deliver a presentation with a PowerPoint file based on their research proposal and
then answer questions from the examiners. The presentation should not exceed 8
minutes, while the Q&A session will not exceed 7 minutes.
1352 Music & 1354 Drama and Film & Television
Auditions will be held to evaluate the professional competence of students
majoring in conducting and music performance. For students in other
specializations, a comprehensive interview will be conducted, during which they
will deliver an 8-minute PowerPoint presentation based on their art practice and
study plan, followed by a 7-minute Q&A session with the examiners.
