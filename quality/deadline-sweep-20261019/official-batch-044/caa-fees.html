Tuition and Fee

Tuition for the whole academic year is paid in one installment on registration.


Students who give up their qualification before registration,due to personal reasons, will be refunded 50% of paid tuition.

Students who wish to withdraw from a program within first week of classes, due to personal reasons, will be refunded 70% of paid tuition. The accommodation fee will be refunded from the next month.
