2026 BIT Scholarships
A. Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)
1. Type A
(1) Undergraduate Program (only for Chinese-taught program)
The scholarship application is directed to the local Chinese Embassy or Consulate. The application time is usually from November to April next year. Please ask for more application information from Chinese Embassy/Consulate in your home country.
When filling in the scholarship application form, the applicant should choose Type A and select Beijing Institute of Technology as the first priority institution.
(2) Graduate Program (Bilateral Program)
The program accords with the cooperation and exchange agreements or consensus reached by the Chinese government and the governments and institutions of other nations. An applicant shall file an application with the dispatching department of their home countries.
2. Type B (University Program)
Including High Level Graduate Program, Silk Road Program, etc.
(1) Full Scholarship Coverage
Tuition fees; Free university dormitory or accommodation subsidy; Stipend: Master’s students: CNY 3,000/month, Ph.D. students: CNY 3,500/month; Comprehensive medical insurance: CNY 800/person/year
(2) Application Procedure
l Register at https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn and upload required documents. (BIT’s Agency No. is 10007; Type: B).
l Register at http://apply.isc.bit.edu.cn and upload required documents.
(3) Application Period
October 15th, 2025 to February 15th, 2026 (For CSC applicants, first round)
February 16th, 2026 to April 15th, 2026 (For CSC applicants, second round)
