Application Requirements

Education: Undergraduate — diploma of high school or above

                   Master — bachelor degree or above

                   Doctor — master degree or above

Age:  Undergraduate — under 35-year-old

         Postgraduate — under 45-year-old

Language:

         HSK Level 4 or above (Major of Traditional Chinese Medicine Level 6 or above)

Health：no disease in color discernment & scent

Application Deadline:

  Fall Semester — July 15

  Spring Semester — Jan 15
