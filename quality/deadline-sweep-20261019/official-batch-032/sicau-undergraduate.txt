Admissions to Undergraduate Programs 2026
作者： 时间：2025年11月27日 17:51 点击数：
1. Admission Plan
Enrollment plan for full-time undergraduate students is 40. (See Attachment 1 for list of majors）
2. Basic Admission Requirements
1) Good physical and mental health
2) Senior high school diploma (this year’s graduate should submit diploma at the latest when register)
3) Starting from the 2026 academic year, applicants must take the "China Scholastic Competency Assessment Test" (CSCA Test) before submission, with scores being a mandatory part of our admission review.
4) Language requirement: HSK 4 ≥180.
5) Under the age of 30
6) Complies with the regulations of the Ministry of Education’s document [2020] No.12.
3. Application Schedule
December 1st 2025 to May 31st 2026
4. Application Documents
1) Valid passport, applicants in China are required to provide residence permit page
2) Original or notarized senior high school transcript and diploma (current graduates need to provide a pre-graduation certificate), non-Chinese or non-English versions must be accompanied by notarized Chinese or English translations.
3) Scan of the CSCA Test results.
Applicants for Chinese Language and Literature undergraduate program who provide a valid HSK 4 certificate with a score of 180 or above are exempt from the subject Humanities Chinese of the CSCA Test (official website: https://csca.cn/home , official instruction: https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/eG1MJkE-LVZH8Bc1T6ubpA).
4) Personal Resume or CV (See Attachment 2 for the template)
5) Language Certificate (HSK certificates, etc)
6) Valid medical certificate (See Attachment 3 for the template)
7) Certificate of Non-criminal Record
8) Financial statement
9) Other supporting materials (such as award certificates, recommendation letters, etc.)
Note:
a. Applications with incomplete materials will not be accepted.
b. In addition to the above-mentioned materials, supplementary materials may be required by the situation.

6. Admission Results
Shortlisted applicants must pass a video interview. Results will be published on the university website and notified via email. Applicants may also check their status through the Online Application System.
7. Scholarships
International students can apply for university scholarship, the standard is tuition waiver. Additionally, 10 outstanding freshmen will be selected to receive the first-class academic scholarship (RMB 6,000/year), while the remaining freshmen will receive the third-class academic scholarship (RMB 2,000/year).
Students can also apply for Sichuan Government Scholarship (SGS) ，Chengdu Government Sister-Cities Scholarships ，Chengdu Government “The Belt and Road” Scholarship.
For detailed information, please visit the scholarship website.
