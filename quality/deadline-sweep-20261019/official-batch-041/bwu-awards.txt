Tuition and Scholarship
Beijing Municipal Scholarship for International Cooperation and Exchange
The project was approved by the International Department of the Beijing Municipal Education Commission and implemented by the International College of Beijing Wuzi University on behalf of the school. It is mainly used to attract overseas students to study in China and increase the number and level of international students in the College of Materials; Develop recruitment channels and improve the quality of students; Improve the social visibility of our university and expand its overseas influence.
1. Funding Recipients
（1）Foreign students studying at our university;
（2）Physical and mental health, excellent character and learning, law-abiding, no violations of rules and disciplines and other bad records.
2. Application conditions
For details, please refer to Annex 1: Administrative Measures for Foreign Student Scholarships of Beijing Wuzi University.
3. Documents required for the application
For details, please refer to Annex 2: Administrative Measures for Foreign Student Scholarships of Beijing Wuzi University.
4. Procedures
（1）All international students who meet the application requirements stipulated in these Measures may apply to the International College within the specified time and fill in the corresponding application form.
（2） The scholarship for international students in China adopts a comprehensive scoring system, and the scholarship is evaluated in order of total score ranking. The comprehensive score includes transcript GPA, international student apartment performance, social behavior performance, HSK (additional points), club practice activities awards, etc., of which academic performance is the main reference standard.
（3）The leading group for the evaluation of the scholarship for international students from Beijing Wuzi University and Materials organizes the preliminary evaluation and re-evaluation according to the application of international students, and after being reviewed and approved by the school leaders, it will be announced to the whole school for a period of not less than 5 days, and the selection results will be reported to the relevant departments for the record after no objection is announced.
