2026 International Admission  Beijing University of Technology

Beijing University of Technology (BJUT) , established in 1960 , is a key university 
administered by the Beijing Municipal Government with a focus on engineering and a 
multidisciplinary nature, covering nine major disciplines including engineering, science, 
economics, management, literature, law, education, art, and interdisciplinary studies. In 
1981, BJUT was approved as one of the first universities in China to confer Master's 
Degrees. In 1985, BJUT was approved to confer Doctoral Degrees. In 1996, BJUT 
entered the list of key universities in the national "211 Project". In 2017, BJUT officially 
entered the list of universities in the national "Double First -Class" Initiative.  
Successfully passing the first -round construction evaluation in 2022, BJUT has been 
included in the list of "Double First-Class" initiative universities and disciplines for the 
second round. 9 disciplines entered Top 500 of the QS World University Rankings by 
Subject. 11 disciplines including Engineering, Materials Science, Chemistry, 
Environment and Ecology, Computer Science, Biology and Biochemistry, General 
Social Sciences, Physics, Earth Science, Clinical Medicine and Mathematics entered 
Top 1% of the ESI Rankings, among which Engineering and Materials Science ranked 
Top 1‰. 
 
I. ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS  
1. Applicants must be in good physical and mental health with no criminal record. 
2. Applicants must be non -Chinese citizens with a valid passport, between the ages 
of 18 and 25 (starting from September 1, 2008), in good physical and mental health, 
and have attained a senior high school diploma. 
3. Applicants should meet the conditions set by  the Document No. 12 (2020) of 
Ministry of Education of the PRC.

4. Language requirement: new HSK-4 or above level 
5. Applicants should take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) 
organized by the China Scholarship Council of the Ministry of Education of

* Applicants who do not simultaneously meet the No.4 and No. 5 requirements are 
recommended to take one -year self -funded preparatory course at Northeast Normal

IV. ADMISSION PROCEDURE 
Step1: Take China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) 
The Applicant shall take the assessment and obtain the score. Please visit CSCA 
website: www.csca.cn for detailed information. For the test subjects of each major, 
please refer to the attached 2026 BJUT Undergraduate Programs.  
Note: For the 1st application period, we accept scores from session held in 
December 2025. For the 2nd application period, we accept scores from the 4 sessions in 
December 2025, and January, March and April 2026.  
Step 2: Fill in and submit online application 
The Applicant shall register and submit online application at https://bjut.17gz.org/. 
Step 3: Pay application fee 
The application fee is 500 RMB. Once paid, it is non-refundable. Applications will
