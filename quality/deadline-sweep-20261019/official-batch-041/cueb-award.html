CUEB Scholarship

 

 

Scholarship for New Foreign Student at CUEB

 

To encourage and support outstanding international students to further study at Capital University of Economics and Business (hereinafter referred to as CUEB), CUEB sets up a" New Foreign student scholarship of Capital University of Economics and Business ".

 

 

Categories of Applicants and Duration of Scholarship

 

 

This scholarship sponsors new outstanding foreign students who come to study the degree and language programs(for their first year).

 

 

Criteria of Scholarship

 

The scholarship partially covers fees of tuition, accommodation and allowance. For more details please check with International Student Office.

 

 

 

 

 

 Scholarship for Excellent(Academically)Foreign Student at CUEB

 

To encourage foreign students to study diligently and stimulate their innovation ability, Capital University of Economics and Business (hereinafter referred to as CUEB) sets up an " Excellent (Academically) Foreign student scholarship of Capital University of Economics and Business " to help students to improve the comprehensive quality as well as to promote the improvement of training quality.

 

Categories of Applicants 

 

This scholarship sponsors outstanding foreign students who study the degree programs at CUEB. 

 

 

Criteria of Scholarship

 

The scholarship  partially covers fees of tuition, accommodation and living expenses. For more details please check with International Student Office

 

 

 

 

Criteria & Eligibility

 

1.Applicants should be foreign students who have studied the degree program at CUEB for a complete academic year;

 

2.The student obeys the law of China, regulations of the university, works hard and behaves well;

 

3.According to curriculum design, the applicant has no make-up tests or retaking courses. And their academic score should meet the certain requirements.

 

4.Student who extends the program is not qualified for the application.

 

 

 

 

 

 

Scholarship for Foreign Student of Language Program at School of International Education,CUEB

 

 

To encourage foreign students to work diligently and learn Chinese well, School of International Education sets up the "Scholarship for Foreign student of Language program".

 

 

 Categories of Applicants  

 

This scholarship sponsors outstanding foreign students who study the language program for one or over one semester at CUEB. 

 

 

 Criteria of Scholarship

 

The scholarship is divided into several types: awards for perfect attendance, diligence, HSK awards, learning excellence.For more details please check with International Student Office
