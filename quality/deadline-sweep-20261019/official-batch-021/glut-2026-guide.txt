1.桂林理工大学简介 Guilin University of Technology Profile
桂林理工大学成立于1956年，位于世界著名的风景旅游城市—广西桂林，是一所历史悠久的公办高等学校，是一所以工学为主，理、管、文、经、法、艺、农8大学科门类，研究生教育、本科生教育、高等职业教育和成人教育协调发展的多科性高等学校。
学校现有桂林屏风、桂林雁山、南宁安吉、南宁空港四个校区，校园总面积3300余亩。学校下设19个学院，有68个本科招生专业，37个硕士学位授权点，3个博士后科研流动站，6个博士学位授权点。材料科学、化学、工程学、环境生态学、地球科学5个学科进入ESI世界学科排名前1%。15个专业通过工程教育专业认证（评估），2个专业通过联合国旅游组织旅游教育质量国际认证（TedQual）。在职教职员工2600多人，各类全日制在校生46000余人。
学校坚持开放办学，以推进国际交流与教育合作，提升国际影响力和竞争力为目标，积极构建全方位、多层次、多形式的对外交流与合作体系。学校先后与美国、英国、澳大利亚、日本、俄罗斯、马来西亚、越南等30多个国家的100多所高校建立了友好合作关系，与其中50余所高校签订了学术交流、人才培养与科研合作协议。来华留学生教育工作稳步开展，至今已培养了来自60多个国家的各类留学生8000余人。
Guilin University of Technology (GUT), founded in 1956, is a long-established public university situated in the world-famous scenic city of Guilin in Guangxi Province. GUT has developed into a university of multiple disciplines. With Engineering taking the lead, there are a total of 8 primary disciplines, including sciences, management, liberal arts, economics, law, arts and agriculture. GUT provides a coordinated development of postgraduate education, undergraduate education, higher vocational education and continuing education.
Covering more than 220 hectares, GUT has four campuses: Guilin Pingfeng, Guilin Yanshan, Nanning Anji, and Nanning Konggang. GUT has 19 colleges with 68 undergraduate programs, 37 master's degree programs, 3 post-doctoral research stations, as well as 6 doctoral degree programs. Materials Science, Chemistry, Engineering, Environmental Ecology and Earth Science have entered the top 1% in ESI global rankings. 15 programs have passed Engineering Education Accreditation (evaluation), and 2 programs have earned UN Tourism TedQual Certification. GUT is home to more than 2,600 faculty and staff members, 46,000 full-time students.
Committed to open education, GUT actively develops a comprehensive, multi-level international exchange and cooperation framework to enhance global influence and competitiveness. So far GUT has established friendly cooperative relations with over 100 universities in more than 30 countries, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Japan, Russia, Malaysia, Vietnam, etc., and has signed academic exchanges, personnel training and scientific research cooperation agreements with more than 50 of them. The education of international students has developed steadily, and so far GUT has cultivated over 8,000 students from more than 60 countries.
