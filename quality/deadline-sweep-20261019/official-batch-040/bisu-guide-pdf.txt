BISU Application Guide for Beijing 
International Students Scholarship 
(Freshmen) 
 
Beijing International Students Scholarship (Freshmen) 
is provided by the People’s Government of Beijing Municipality 
for outstanding international students who come to Beijing for 
their studies. It is aimed at developing high -level international  
student enrollment scale in Beijing’s higher education 
institutions and promoting the overall level of 
internationalization of education in Beijing’s universities. 
I. Category  
1. Applicants for  master’s degree: o ne academic year 
tuition fee reduction or exemption. 
2. Applicants for  bachelor’s degree: one academic year 
tuition fee reduction or exemption. 
3. Applicants for non -degree advanced study or long -term 
Chinese language program: one semester tuition fee reduction or 
exemption. 
II. Qualifications 
Outstanding international students who apply to study in 
BISU. 
III. Requirements

1. Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens; 
2. Applicants must be friendly to China and abide by the 
law and rules of Chinese government; 
3. Applicants should have excellent academic performance 
and good health; 
4. Applicants’ academic qualifications and age 
requirements are as follows: 
(1) Applica nts for bachelor’s degree must have at least a 
high school diploma and be generally no older than 30 years old; 
applicants for master’s degre e must be at least a bachelor’s 
degree holder and generally no older than 35 years old. 
(2) Th ose who apply for non-degree advanced study  in 
related majors must have a degree equivalent to a bachelor’s 
degree or be in the second year of college or above, and they 
should generally not exceed 50 years old; applicants who apply 
for long -term language learning program should have a high 
school degree and above, and they should generally not exceed 
60 years old. 
IV . Application Materials 
Related m aterials can be written either in Chinese or  
English. The submitted application documents will NOT be 
returned regardless of acceptance or denial:

1. Application Form for Beijing International Students 
Scholarship for International Students (Freshmen) (see 
Attachment 1); 
2. Notarized highest academic qualification and  academic 
transcripts; 
3. For those who have been studying for more than 6 
months (including 6 months)  in China, a “Physical Examination 
Record for Foreigners” (printed by the Chinese health and 
quarantine authorities) is required; 
4. Applicants for an academic degree or non-degree 
advanced study should provide a study plan ( of no less than 400 
words) or a research plan (of no less than 800 words); 
5. Applicants under the age of 18 should submit relevant 
legal documents of their legal guardians in China. 
V . Application Procedures 
1. Please first complete  the online application at 
http://apply.bisu.edu.cn/, choosing Lo cal G overnment 
Scholarship, after which your online application form will be 
generated, and then mail the form to study@bisu.edu.cn 
2. After receiving students’ materials, BISU will review the 
application docum ents and conduct a merit -based selection 
featuring openness, fairness and impartiality.  The final 
interpretation for the admission of students belongs to the 
International Admissions Office.
