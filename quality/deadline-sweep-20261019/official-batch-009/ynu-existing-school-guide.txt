云南大学 2026 年国际中文教师奖学金 
招生简章 
 
 
一、资助对象 
1. 非中国籍人士； 
2. 对华友好，无违法犯罪记录，遵守中国政府的法律、法规和云南大学的规章制度； 
3. 身心健康，品学兼优； 
4. 有志于从事中文教育、教学及相关工作； 
5. 年龄为 16-35 周岁（统一以 2026 年 9 月 1  日计），在职中文教师放宽至 45周岁
。 
 
二、招生类别及申请条件 
（一）国际中文教育专业硕士研究生 
资助期限为 2 年。申请者应具有大学本科学历。中文水平考试成绩达到  HSK（
五级）210 分、HSKK（中级）60 分。提供毕业后拟任教机构工作协议或相关证明者
优先。 
（二） 一学年研修生 
资助期限为11 个月。 
1.国际中文教育方向：申请者中文水平考试成绩达到 HSK（三级）270 分，具有 
HSKK 成绩； 
2.汉语研修方向：申请者中文水平考试成绩达到  HSK（三级） 210 分，提供  
HSKK 成绩者优先。 
（三）一学期研修生 
资助期限为5 个月。国际中文教育方向，申请者中文水平考试成绩达到 HSK（三
级）180 分，具有 HSKK成绩。 
（四）四周研修生 
资助期限为4周。申请者需具有HSK成绩。该项目由推荐机构组团进行报名，需
事先联系云南大学确定在华学习计划，并提前报语合中心审批，每团10-15人。

三、资助内容 
国际中文教师奖学金资助内容包括：学费、住 宿费、生活费 （四周研修生除外 ）
和综合医疗保险费。 
注：学历生须参加年度评审，详见《国际中文教师奖学金年度评审办法》。 
 
四、申请流程 
第一步： 
所有申请者需注册并登录国际中文教师奖学金报名网站（ cis.chinese.cn）进行申
请。具体操作如下： 
国际中文教师奖学金申请网站操作流程： 
自2026年3月1日起，申请者可登录国际中文教师奖学金申请网站（ cis.chinese.cn
）注册账户、查询推荐机构并选择 云南大学作为接收院校，在线填写、提交申请材
料并按要求附上有关证明材料的电子扫描件。同时，申请人需要同步在云 南 大 学 国 
际 学 生 服 务 平 台 （https:// ynu.at0086.cn/StuApplication/Login.aspx）注册账号 ，按
要求在线填写申请信息，并提交申请材料。 
第二步： 
语合中心将委托专家组进行集中评审，于入学前约  3 个月完成奖学金评审工作，
公布评审结果； 
第三步： 
申请者可在线查看申请进程、审核意见与奖学金评审结果，获奖者可在线打印
获奖证书； 
第四步： 
云南大学将根据语合中心评审结果与奖学金获得者确认录取； 
第五步： 
奖学金获得者需根据云南大学录取通知书规定的时间、方式和程序入学报到。

五、关于在职中文教师和汉语桥获奖者 
1. 在职中文教师 
在职中文教师申请各类奖学金，可提供就职机构出具的在职证明和推荐信，同
等条件下优先录取。 
2. 汉语桥获奖者 
在各类汉语桥比赛中获得 “国际中文教师奖学金证书 ”者，登录国际中文教师奖
学 金 网 站 ， 凭 奖 学 金 证 书 向云南 大 学 提 交 申 请 材 料 。如 有 问 题 ， 请 咨 询 
chinesebridge@chinese.cn. 
 
六、申请材料 
推荐机构需对申请者护照用名完整、国籍无误、永久通信地址 真实有效等情况
进行审核。以下材料如非中文或英文版本，需提供相应的公证文件。 
1. 护照照片页扫描件。 
2. 未满 18 周岁的申请者，须提交在华监护人署名的委托证明文件。 
3. HSK、HSKK 成绩报告（有效期两年）扫描件。 
4. 推荐机构负责人签发的推荐信。 
5. 国际中文教育专业硕士须提供经过公证的最高学历证明（毕业预期证明）和在
校学习成绩单。 
6. 国际中文教育专业硕士须提供两名副教授以上职称导师的推荐信。提供毕业后
拟任教机构工作协议者优先资助。 
7. 在职中文教师须附上就职机构出具的在职证明和推荐信，  同等条件下优先录
取。

8. 在各类汉语桥比赛中获得“国际中文教师奖学金证书”者,可提交奖学金证书。 
 
七、注意事项 
1. 申请材料不完整或不符合招生条件的，概不受理。 
2. 申请材料弄虚作假，或非本人填写提交的，一经查实，申请资格将被取消。 
3. 奖学金获得者因故不能报到，应在报到日前 15天书面告知我校并注明原因。
无故未报到者，取消奖学金资格。 
4. 入学体检不合格者，或中途退学、休学者，奖学金资格将被取消。

I. Eligibility 
1. Be a non-Chinese citizen; 
2. Be friendly to China, have no criminal record, abide by the laws and regulations of 
China and Yunnan University; 
3. Be in good physical and mental health ,  be   in  good  academic  and  behavioral 
standing; 
4. Be interested in Chinese language education and related work; 
5. Be aged between 16 and 35 years on September 1, 2026. The maximum age limit 
for in-service Chinese teachers may be extended to 45. 
 
II. Categories and Application Requirements 
A. Chinese Language Teachers Scholarships 
1. Scholarship for Master’s Degree in International Chinese Language Education 
This program provides sponsorship for 2 academic years. Applicants shall hold a 
Bachelor’s degree. Applicants shall have a minimum score of 210 on the HSK test (Level 
5) and 60 on the HSKK test (Intermediate Level). Priority will be given to applicants who 
may provide an employment contract with a  teaching institution upon completing their 
studies in China or proof to the same effect. 
2. Scholarship for One-Year Study Program 
This program provides scholarship for a maximum of 11 months. Applicants who had 
the same scholarship within 3 years will not be admitted. 
a) International Chinese language education: 

PAGE 6
Applicants shall have a minimum score of 270 on the HSK test (Level 3), and an HSKK 
test score is required. 
b) Chinese Language: 
Applicants shall have a minimum score of 210 on the HSK test (Level 3), and priority 
will be given to applicants who provide an HSKK test score. 
3. Scholarship for One-Semester Study Program 
This program provides sponsorship of a maximum of 5 months. Applicants who had 
the same scholarship within 3 years will not be admitted. 
a) International Chinese language education: 
Applicants shall have a minimum score of 180 on the HSK test (Level 3), and an HSKK 
test score is required. 
4. Scholarship for Four-Week Study Program 
This program provides scholarship for 4 weeks. Applicants shall submit an HSK score 
report. This program provides Chinese language & culture course to student group (10 to15 
participants) which should be organized by a Recommending Institution. The 
Recommending Institution should contact with TJU to negotiate the study plan in advance, 
and such plan shall be submitted to CLEC for review and approval. 
 
III. Scholarship Coverage 
Full International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship supports tuition, 
accommodation, living allowance (not include Four -week Study Program) and 
comprehensive medical insurance fee. 
Note: 
Degree students are  subject to  the annual assessment according to the Annual 
Assessment Procedures of the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship.

V. Policies Regarding In -service Chinese Language Teachers and Chinese Bridge 
Winners 
1. In-Service Chinese Language Teachers  Priority will be given to the In -service 
Chinese language teachers if they provide proof of employment and a letter of  
recommendation from the employer. 
2. Chinese Bridge Winners  Applicants who have received the International Chinese 
Language Teachers Scholarship Certificate during Chinese Bridge competitions may  
submit their application documents along with their scholarship certificates to the Host 
Institutions directly at the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship 
website. For any further inquiries, please contact chinesebridge@chinese.cn. 
 
VI. Application Documents 
Recommending institutions need to complete the whole application procedure and 
make sure the name, nationality, and permanent address of the applicant are correct. If 
the following documents are not in Chinese or English, notarization documents should be 
provided. 
1. A scanned copy of the passport photo page. 
2. Applicants under the age of 18 shall submit certified documents of  designation 
signed by their entrusted legal guardians in China. 
3. A Scanned copy of  score reports of the HSK/HSKK tests (within the two-year 
validity). 
4. A reference letter signed by the head of the Recommending Institution. 
5. Applicants for a Master’s Degree in International Chinese Language Education are 
required to submit notarized certificate of the highest diploma (or proof of 
expected graduation) and notarized academic transcript. 
6. Applicants for a Master’s Degree in International Chinese Language Education are 
required to submit two reference  letters from professors or associate professors 

PAGE 9
(in Chinese or English). Priority will be given to applicants who may provide an 
employment contract with a teaching institution upon completing their studies in 
China. 
7. In-service Chinese language teachers shall provide proof  of employme nt and a  
reference letter from the employer. 
8. Applicants who have received the International  Chinese Language Teachers 
Scholarship Certificate during Chinese Bridge competitions need to submit t heir 
application documents along with their scholarship certificates. 
 
VII. Disclaimer 
1. Incomplete or unqualified application materials will not be processed. 
2. Applicants will be disqualified from application in case of fraudulent application 
materials or application materials are completed and submitted by someone else. 
3. Scholarship winners who cannot register with YNU on time should inform us about 
the reasons in written form within 15 days before the admission date. For those who do not 
register as requested without reason, the scholarship will be canceled. 
4. Scholarship will be canceled for those who do not pa ss the admission physical 
examination, drop out, or take schooling suspension during the study.
