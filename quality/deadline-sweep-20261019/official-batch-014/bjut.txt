Beijing University of Technology (BJUT) was established in 1960, in the southeast area of Beijing. It is a 
high-level multi-disciplinary research university providing programs in science, engineering, economics, man-
agement, literature, law, art and education, with engineering as a core focus. In 1981, BJUT was approved as 
one of the ﬁrst universities in China to confer Master’s Degrees. In 1985, BJUT was approved to confer Doctoral 
Degrees. In 1996, BJUT entered the list of key universities in the national “211 Project”. In 2017, BJUT oﬃcially 
entered the list of universities in the national “Double First-Class” Initiative.
College of Economics and Management opens English-instructed undergraduate program International 
Economics and Trade for International Students.  Set against the backdrop of economic globalization and 
Beijing’s development as an International Exchange Center, the undergraduate degree program in International 
Economics and Trade (English) leverages Beijing’s new opening-up strategy and aims to cultivate well-rounded 
talents with solid specialized knowledge, familiarity with China’s economic policies and international market 
environments, innovative and entrepreneurial spirit, social responsibility, professional ethics, and an under -
standing of Chinese business culture. This program adapts to new international business trends, specializing 
in two areas: Digital Trade and Brand Economics.
COLLEGE OVERVIEW
Founded in 1997, the College of Economics and Management comprises four Departments: Management 
Science and Technology, Applied Economics, Business Administration, and Law. It offers nine undergraduate 
degree programs: International Economics and Trade, Economic Statistics, Finance, Big Data Management 
and Application, Information Management and Systems, Accounting, Business Administration, Marketing, and 
Law. Notably, three programs—International Economics and Trade, Information Management and Systems, 
and Accounting—have earned recognition as national ﬁrst-class undergraduate degree programs. The College 
is actively pursuing AACSB international accreditation, which it expects to obtain in 2026.
