Advanced Diploma Programs
All the undergraduate and postgraduates programs offered by BWU can admit into international advanced diploma students with a length of study of less than two years.
Non-Degree Chinese Language Study Programs
Category
Study Length
Course Content
Long-term
More than one term
It falls into the following sub-categories: elementary level, intermediate level and advanced level. The main aim of the courses is to teach modern Chinese with an emphasis on training in the skill areas of listening and speaking. The programs not only open required courses of Comprehensive Chinese , Listening, Spoken Chinese, Reading, and Writing in Chinese Language, but also the elective ones such as Chinese Calligraphy, Shadowboxing and Beijing Opera Appreciation.
Short-term
From two weeks to three months
The course can be tailored to the specific needs of the students. Besides basic Chinese courses, handwriting, Cooking and Shadowboxing study can also be included in the package. The study can be combined with the sightseeing and on-site visits.

Ø  The application processing fee is non refundable. It can either be sent to the International Education Exchange Center directly or be paid together with tuition fee and accommodation fee after registration.
Ø  There are 18 weeks of class in each semester. The applicant can audit the class for two weeks. The two-week tuition fee will be deducted from the total if the applicant at last decides to quit the study. If the audit exceeds two weeks, the total tuition fee won��t be returned back.
Ø  The teaching materials fee will be charged in advance according to the above table. Based on the actual fee, any overpayment will be refunded and any deficiency needs a supplemental payment.
Ø  All expenses in connection with physical examination will be paid by the applicants themselves.
Ø  Insurance Issue: If the applicants have already bought international health insurance plan in their home country, they should submit the related verification when attending class officially. Those who do not have the overseas insurance should go through the formalities of joining in Accidental Injury Insurance and Hospitalization Medical Insurance plans when registration. GRADUATION CREDENTIALS
Ø  The international students who have completed their undergraduate and postgraduate studies and passed all the required courses will be awarded the gradation diploma and degree credentials accredited by Chinese government.
Ø  Long-term Chinese language learners and advanced students who have completed their studies will be issued completion certificates.
Ø  Short-term Chinese language learners and students who quit their study will be issued study certificates.
