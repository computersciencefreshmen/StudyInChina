Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications
I．Brief Introduction of BUPT
Established in 1955, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) is directly administered by the
Ministry of Education (MOE) and co-built by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT). It has been one
of the key universities in China that has entered the national first“211 Project”and“985 Innovative Platforms for Advanced
Disciplines Project”. BUPT is also a multidisciplinary and research-oriented one with information technology as its main
feature and a focus on engineering and science, offering a wide range of subjects including engineering, management,
humanities and sciences for the pursuit of its coordinated development. It serves as an important talent base for China’s
information technology industry.
After over seven decades of development, BUPT boasts a strong academic background in information technology as
well as an edge in disciplines featuring IT, two of which, namely“ Information and Communication Engineering” and
“Computer Science and Technology”, have been included in China’s “double first-class” list. In addition, according to
results released by China’s Ministry of Education in the Fourth Round National Evaluation of Disciplinary Development in

Universities, BUPT’s “Information and Communication Engineering (ICE)”,“Computer Science and Technology”and
“ Electronic Science and Technology” were evaluated at or above A Class, and ICE was evaluated at A+. Besides, our
“Electronic Science”discipline was ranked 19th in 2020 US News Best Global Universities Rankings and “Computer
Science and Technology”ranked among the Top 1% of ESI worldwide.
The university actively enhances the connection with universities for building friendly and cooperative relationships
and strengthens the inter-university exchanges as the breakthrough point of the international educational exchanges, and
continuously expands the cooperation with world-class universities and scientific research institutions. At present, it has
established cooperative relationships with over 160 famous universities in more than 30 countries and regions, and signed
nearly 300 agreements. The Confucius Institute at the University of the South Pacific has established a pattern of running
schools in three countries and four regions.
The university has four campuses, namely, Xitucheng Campus, Shahe Campus, Hongfu Campus and Xiaoxitian
Campus, as well as advanced research institutes in Wuxi, Jiangsu Province and Shenzhen, Guangdong Province.There are
nearly 28,000 full-time undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral and international students. Currently, international students
come from more than 50 countries.

II. Length of Study
Undergraduates at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications study for four years. Graduate students
(Master’s Degree) study for two or three years. Graduate students (Doctor’s Degree) study for four years. Visiting students
and research scholars generally stay for a year .

Application Materials
The following documents are needed for all applicants:
1. The completed BUPT Admission Application Form for Overseas Students;
2. Applicants’ certificate of highest completed academic level, diploma of highest completed academic institution and
high school/college transcripts (photocopies or notary English translations);

=== PAGE 9 ===
3. Letters of recommendation required of different student categories;
4. The completed Physical Examination Record for Foreigners;
5. Proof of minimum HSK level 5 for those entering programs taught in Chines(HSK 4 is required for the major of
Chinese Language).
Application Procedure
1. Get ready all the required documents and complete the application process on BUPT website
(https://ois.bupt.edu.cn)(The original documents must be presented on the applicants’ arrival)
2.All application materials will be evaluated by the university. Once the application for admission is endorsed, BUPT
will grant the BUPT Admission Notice and the Confirmation Form for Study in China (JW 202 Form for short) and email
the two documents to the successful applicant;
3.The applicant goes in person to Chinese Embassy in one’s own country to apply for a (X) visa to study in China with
the following materials: JW 202 Form, BUPT Admission Notice, Physical Examination Record for Foreigners;
4. After going through the above application procedures, the applicant shall enroll in BUPT within the time notified in
the BUPT Admission Notice.
