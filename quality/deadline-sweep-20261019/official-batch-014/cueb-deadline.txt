Application Documents and Deadline
Application Documents
:
1.Student application form with recent photo
2.Credential and school transcript of your highest education(photocopy or certified English Translation)
3.Application form of on-campus international student accomodation
4.Letter of Guarantee,supplemented with a photocopy of the guarantor's identity's credential
5.Health Certificate,including an original blood test report covering AIDS and syphilis tests.
6.A photocopy of your passport
7.Application letter, including plan of study, purpose of study and two recommendation letters.
8. English proficiency test, such as IELTS. TOFEL(for English-Taught program)
9.HSK certificate(for Chinese-Taught program)
Application Deadline
Spring Semester(language program only):
Before December 20th of each year
Fall Semester(both language and degree program):
Before June 10th of each year
