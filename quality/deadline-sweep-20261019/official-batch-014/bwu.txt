Insurance Fee
400RMB/Semester，800RMB/Year
According to the relevant regulations issued by the Ministry of Education of People’s Republic of China, international students shall
purchase the Comprehensive Insurance for International Students
. The premium of insurance at one's own expense is RMB400/Semester, RMB800/Year (The price is subject to change by the  insurance company). Only RMB cash is acceptable.
Payment Instructions
1. Application fee is non-refundable, which should be submitted along with application materials.
2. Tuition and Insurance fee should be paid at one time when making registration.
3. Upon check-in, the students are required to prepay the accommodation fee and a deposit(RMB Cash only) for at least one semester.
