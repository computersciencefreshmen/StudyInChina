2026/2027 Academic Year General Regulations of Enrollment for Bilateral Program of Chinese Government Scholarship at Jilin University (Type A)

发布时间：2025/10/27 14:27    浏览次数：   

Jilin University, founded in 1946, is a national key comprehensive university under the direct administration of the Ministry of Education in China, and among the first batch of “Project 985” and “Project 211” key construction universities. In 2017, it was rated as one of the first groups of A-level “Double First-Class” universities. In 2022, six disciplines were listed in the second-round selection of “Double First-Class” universities and disciplines.

Jilin University offers a wide range of disciplines, covering 13 academic categories: philosophy, economics, law, education, literature, history, science, engineering, agriculture, medical science, management, art and interdiscipline. The university has a complete high-level talent training system with bachelor, master and doctoral programs, and more than 70,000 students on campus. With a focus on the goal of “Double First-Class Initiative”, the university has continuously optimized its global cooperation network and established partnerships with 313 universities and research institutions in 43 countries and regions.

The bilateral program provides full or partial scholarships for applicants in accordance with the agreements or consensus on education cooperation and exchange reached by the Chinese government and the governments, institutions, universities, and international organizations of other countries.

        I. Supporting Categories, Duration, Majors, and Instruction Language

1. Supporting Categories: The program sponsors undergraduates, postgraduates for master’s and doctoral degrees, general and senior advanced students. For details, please contact the international student dispatching department of the applicant’s home country.

2. Instruction Language: For undergraduates, the instruction language is Chinese; For postgraduates and advanced students, generally Chinese, and English classes can be offered for some majors.

3.Language Requirements:  

(1) Applicants for Bachelor's degree programs must take the "China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) " (official website: www.csca.cn). Applicants who do not pass the test are required to undertake preparatory study.

(2) Applicants for disciplines or majors with Chinese as the instruction language must meet the following Chinese proficiency requirements: For general advanced students (excluding “Chinese Language” major) and senior advanced students: at least reach HSK Level 3; For undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degree candidates: at least reach HSK Level 4 or equivalent.

4. Majors: Please refer to the Catalog of Majors for International Students of Jilin University (Attachment 1) for details.

5. Duration of Study:

Student Category

Duration

Undergraduates

4 or 5 years

Master students

2 or 3 years

Ph.D. students

Advanced students

0.5 or 1 year

Students for Chinese tutoring

II. Scholarship Coverage

1. Application fee and tuition fee

2. Stipends Subsidy during the staying period at school

3. Accommodation subsidy at school (Subsidy standards: Undergraduate/Master's students: 700 RMB/person/month; Doctoral students: 1000 RMB/person/month).

4.Comprehensive medical insurance

The scholarship duration includes the periods for both Chinese language study (preparatory course) and major study. The funding duration for Chinese language study (preparatory course) is generally one year. The funding duration for specialized study is consistent with the basic study duration in principle.

Ⅲ. Application Method and Time

Applicants should apply to the international student dispatching department of their home country (usually the office of educational and cultural affairs of Chinese diplomatic missions) within a specified period, generally from early January to April. For details, please consult the international student dispatching department of your home country.

Ⅳ. Eligibility

1. Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens with good physical and mental health;

2. In terms of educational background and age, applicants must:

●be an academically outstanding holder of high school diploma under the age of 25 when applying for undergraduate programs;

●be an academically outstanding holder of a bachelor’s degree under the age of 35 when applying for master’s programs;

●be an academically outstanding holder of a master’s degree under the age of 40 when applying for doctoral programs;

●be a holder of high school diploma or above under the age of 45 when applying for general advanced students’ programs;

●be a master’s degree holder or an associate professor (or above) under the age of 50 when applying for senior advanced students’ programs.

3. For other specific conditions, please consult the international student dispatching department of your home country.

V. Application Process

1. For the application time, the number of the accepting institution and other information, applicants should consult the international student dispatching department of their home country.

2. Applicants should log on to “Online Application System of the China Scholarship Council for International Students” (website:http://www.campuschina.org), fill in the Chinese Government Scholarship Application Form online and submit the required application documents. Please select Jilin University as your first choice, of which the “Number of the Accepting Institution” is “10183”.

3. Applicants should submit the application documents to the international student dispatching department of their home country within the specified period.

VI. Admission and Notification

1. The China Scholarship Council (CSC) will examine whether the applicants meet the requirements.

Unqualified applicants or application documents will not be accepted or handled.

2. The approved application documents will be forwarded to the relevant university for admission.

The CSC has the right to make appropriate adjustments to the university, major, supporting category, and duration of scholarship chosen by the applicant according to the requirements of the scholarship funded by the governments, the acceptance capacity of the host university, the required duration of study for the major and the qualifications of the applicant, etc., and the university will determine the admission result.

The university may advise applicants to change their major according to the application documents and other actual situations, and will then contact the applicant via the email address they have filled in their application. Please pay close attention to emails.

3. Scholarship recipients can study in China.

Upon the admission confirmation by the host university and approval by the China Scholarship Council, applicants are eligible for the Chinese Government Scholarship after passing a review and physical examination upon arrival at the university.

Each scholarship recipient can only receive funding from one Chinese government scholarship program.

4. Admission documents will be forwarded to the student.

Students can check and receive the electronic admission materials: Admission Notice and Confirmation Form for Study in China (JW201 form) on “Online Application System of the China Scholarship Council for International Students”.

5. Scholarship recipients are not allowed to change their study information.

After coming to China, scholarship recipients are not allowed to change the university, major, duration of study and other information as stated in the Admission Notice.

6. Applicants must come to China for study by the deadline.

Scholarship qualifications will not be reserved or deferred for those who cannot come to China for study within the stipulated admission period.
