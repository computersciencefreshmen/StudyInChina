3. Scholarship recipients can study in China.
Upon the admission confirmation by the host university and approval by the China Scholarship Council, applicants are eligible for the Chinese Government Scholarship after passing a review and physical examination upon arrival at the university.
Each scholarship recipient can only receive funding from one Chinese government scholarship program.
4. Admission documents will be forwarded to the student.
Students can check and receive the electronic admission materials: Admission Notice and Confirmation Form for Study in China (JW201 form) on

5. Scholarship recipients are not allowed to change their study information.
