# Verified ICLT expansion — 2026-07-28

## Release decision

This release promotes only school-specific, officially published opportunities for
Spring 2027. The central International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship guide
supports common deadline and coverage rules, while each public record also requires
an official host-university page that explicitly identifies the study category.

The first ten records have an explicit five-month duration and a Spring 2027
application deadline of 2026-10-31. The final three use the same date only where the
university-specific 2026 guide explicitly supports the Spring intake. Self-funded
tuition and application fees are never copied into scholarship routes.

## Quarantined findings

- Northeastern University publishes conflicting HSK score requirements in its
  Chinese and English materials. The Catalog exposes the conflict without choosing a
  numeric score.
- Yunnan University's Spring 2027 label is derived from its published spring intake,
  2026 guide and 2026-10-31 deadline. The record preserves that evidence note; an
  unrelated four-week template error is excluded.
- Northeast Normal University's remaining future four-week route requires a
  recommending organization and a 10–15 person group, so it is not published as an
  individually available project.
- Capital Normal University, Northwest University, Ningxia University, Shihezi
  University and Ningbo University have only host identity or stale-cycle evidence
  in the reviewed sources. No future cycle was inferred.

## Safety and maintenance

All new facts pass the Catalog schema, source-provenance, current-deadline and
publication gates. Sources enter the weekly current-cycle review queue; unchanged
pages are hash-deduplicated, and a failed or conflicting refresh cannot overwrite the
last verified release.
